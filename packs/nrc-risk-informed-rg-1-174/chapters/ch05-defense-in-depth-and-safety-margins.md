# Chapter 5: Defense-in-depth and safety margins

Sources: NRC RG 1.174 Rev. 3, An Approach for Using Probabilistic Risk Assessment in Risk-Informed Decisions on Plant-Specific Changes to the Licensing Basis, 2018, ML17317A256; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared).

## Core Idea

Alongside the PRA-side input from the engineering analysis stage (ch03), the framework weighs defense-in-depth, safety margins, and performance-based consideration. Defense-in-depth and safety margins are maintained through the change, and performance-based consideration is part of the framework. This chapter carries those statements at framework level and nothing more.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Parallel considerations. Defense-in-depth and safety margins are considerations that stand alongside the PRA results in the integrated decisionmaking weighing (ch02); they are not outputs of it.
- Performance-based consideration. It is part of the framework, named as an input the decision weighs together with the others.

## Key Concepts

- Defense-in-depth: a framework-level consideration the change is weighed against; the framework statement is that it is maintained through the change.
- Safety margins: the second framework-level consideration; the same statement holds, that they are maintained through the change.
- Performance-based consideration: part of the framework, weighed in integrated decisionmaking alongside the PRA insights.
- Scope limit: these are framework statements at orientation level. This chapter carries no plant-specific judgement and no adequacy call for any real plant.

## Mental Models

Read the weighing as balanced on more than one leg: a change that looked acceptable on the change-in-risk metrics alone (ch03) still meets the framework only where defense-in-depth and safety margins are maintained through it and the performance-based side is considered. The legs are weighed together, not ranked.

## Anti-patterns

Turning these framework statements into a plant-specific judgement is the named anti-pattern: the chapter states that defense-in-depth and safety margins are maintained through the change as a framework condition, not how much margin any real plant has or how to measure it. A companion error is treating performance-based consideration as optional; it is named as part of the framework.

## Key Takeaways

- PRA insights stand alongside defense-in-depth, safety margins, and performance-based consideration in the integrated decisionmaking weighing.
- Defense-in-depth and safety margins are maintained through the change.
- Performance-based consideration is part of the framework.
- This chapter is descriptive orientation: no plant-specific judgement, no adequacy call for any real plant.
