# Chapter 6: Uncertainty and PRA technical adequacy hooks

Sources: NRC RG 1.174 Rev. 3, An Approach for Using Probabilistic Risk Assessment in Risk-Informed Decisions on Plant-Specific Changes to the Licensing Basis, 2018, ML17317A256; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared).

## Core Idea

Two named considerations sit behind any risk-informed decisionmaking use of a PRA: the technical adequacy of the PRA, and the treatment of uncertainty in the decision. The framework weighs PRA insights as one input (ch02); this chapter names the two companion guidance documents that address those considerations, as name-only hooks. Neither is packaged here.

## Frameworks Introduced

- PRA technical adequacy as a named consideration: the decision leans on the PRA, and the companion guidance on whether the PRA is adequate for the application is RG 1.200.
- Uncertainty as a named consideration: risk-informed decisionmaking works with uncertain inputs, and the companion guidance on treating those uncertainties is NUREG-1855.

## Key Concepts

- RG 1.200 Rev 3 covers PRA technical adequacy for applications like this one; its criteria are not packaged here. RG 1.200 Rev 3 is dated December 2020, accession ML20238B871, owner U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission. The RG 1.200 PRA technical adequacy pack now carries that topic; this chapter carries no adequacy criteria.
- NUREG-1855 covers treatment of uncertainty in risk-informed decisionmaking; its methods are not packaged here. NUREG-1855 is guidance on the treatment of uncertainties associated with PRAs in risk-informed decisionmaking, owner U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission. This chapter carries no uncertainty methods.
- Hook discipline: both documents are named with revision and owner only, never linked, and their text is never reproduced here.

## Mental Models

Read the two hooks as quality checks standing behind the PRA input: before a PRA insight is weighed, the companion guidance asks whether the PRA is adequate for the application and how uncertainty in it is treated. This pack names where those questions are addressed; it does not carry the answers.

## Anti-patterns

Treating these name-only hooks as coverage is the named anti-pattern: naming RG 1.200 or NUREG-1855 does not package their criteria or methods, and an answer that recites either from memory has invented content. A companion error is treating the hooks as steps in this pack's framework; they are pointers, not stages.

## Key Takeaways

- PRA technical adequacy and uncertainty treatment are named considerations behind the framework's PRA input.
- RG 1.200 Rev 3 (December 2020, ML20238B871, U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission) covers PRA technical adequacy; its criteria are not packaged here.
- NUREG-1855 (U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission) covers treatment of uncertainty in risk-informed decisionmaking; its methods are not packaged here.
- Both are name-only: no link, no criteria, no methods, no pack.
