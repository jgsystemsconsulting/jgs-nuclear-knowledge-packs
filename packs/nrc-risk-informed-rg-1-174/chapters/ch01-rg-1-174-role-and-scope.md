# Chapter 1: RG 1.174 role and scope

Sources: NRC RG 1.174 Rev. 3, An Approach for Using Probabilistic Risk Assessment in Risk-Informed Decisions on Plant-Specific Changes to the Licensing Basis, 2018, ML17317A256; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared).

## Core Idea

RG 1.174 is Nuclear Regulatory Commission guidance on using probabilistic risk assessment in risk-informed decisions on plant-specific changes to the licensing basis. Rev. 3 (2018, accession ML17317A256) is the current revision as of the 2026-11-08 verify: no Rev 4 was found as of that search date, and DG-1285 was the proposal that became Rev 3. This pack orients the guide's framework; the guide itself, not these notes, is what a change decision reads against.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Orientation, not the guide. The pack carries synthesized notes on the framework shape: what the guide covers, the stages it names, and the inputs it weighs. The guide's own text, including its numeric guideline tables, stays in the guide.
- The licensing basis connection. The guide's subject, plant-specific changes to the licensing basis, sits alongside the Part 50 licensing basis material carried by the live Part 50 licensing basis pack; ch04 orients where the risk-informed framework meets the 50.59 change lane.

## Key Concepts

- The pin: RG 1.174 Rev. 3, 2018, accession ML17317A256. Rev 3 is current as of the 2026-11-08 verify, with no Rev 4 found as of that search date; DG-1285 was the Rev 3 proposal.
- Subject scope: using probabilistic risk assessment in risk-informed decisions on plant-specific changes to the licensing basis.
- Companion hooks, named only: RG 1.200 Rev 3 on PRA technical adequacy and NUREG-1855 on uncertainty treatment (ch06); neither is packaged here.
- Relation to the Part 50 root material: the licensing basis facts that anchor Part 50 itself live in the Part 50 licensing basis pack; this pack pairs with it and carries the risk-informed framework side.

## Mental Models

Read the guide as a decisionmaking framework, not as a numeric rulebook: the chapters that follow describe how a proposed change moves through the guide's stages and how its inputs are weighed together. Anything that looks like a number belongs to the guide's own tables, which this pack does not reproduce.

## Anti-patterns

Reading these orientation notes as a substitute for the guide is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the notes locate and frame, the guide decides. A companion error is treating a later revision of the guide as covered; this pack pins Rev. 3 and later revisions are out of scope.
