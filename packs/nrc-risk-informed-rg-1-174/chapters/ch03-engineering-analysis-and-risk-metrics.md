# Chapter 3: Engineering analysis and risk metrics

Sources: NRC RG 1.174 Rev. 3, An Approach for Using Probabilistic Risk Assessment in Risk-Informed Decisions on Plant-Specific Changes to the Licensing Basis, 2018, ML17317A256; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared).

## Core Idea

Engineering analysis is the guide's second stage: once a proposed change is defined, the analysis works out what the change does to risk. The metrics the guide works with are the change in core damage frequency (ΔCDF) and the change in large early release frequency (ΔLERF). The guide carries numeric guideline tables keyed to those metrics; those tables live in the guide and are not reproduced here.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Engineering analysis as one stage. It sits between defining the proposed change and the integrated decisionmaking weighing (ch02); its output feeds the weighing as the PRA-side input.
- Metrics named, values not. ΔCDF and ΔLERF are the change-in-risk metrics the guide works with. This pack names the metrics and never states a value attached to them.

## Key Concepts

- ΔCDF: change in core damage frequency, the core-damage-side change-in-risk metric the guide works with.
- ΔLERF: change in large early release frequency, the release-side change-in-risk metric the guide works with.
- The guide's numeric guideline tables: they live in the guide. They are not reproduced here, never reconstructed from memory, and never restated as thresholds in this pack.
- This pack states no numeric acceptance threshold, guideline value, or region boundary: any number a question seems to need is a number to look up in the guide itself.

## Mental Models

Read engineering analysis as producing a measured change-in-risk picture in ΔCDF and ΔLERF terms, and read the guide's guideline tables as the place where such a picture is compared against numeric guidance. This pack carries the first half and only names the second: the tables are guide content, and guessing at them from orientation notes is worse than not guessing.

## Anti-patterns

Stating a guideline value or region boundary from memory of the guide is the chapter's named anti-pattern: this pack contains no numeric acceptance threshold, guideline value, or region boundary, and any answer that produces one has left the fact base these notes were built from. A companion error is sliding into PRA how-to: the pack carries no PRA methodology instruction, no guidance on building, updating, or quantifying a PRA.

## Key Takeaways

- ΔCDF (change in core damage frequency) and ΔLERF (change in large early release frequency) are the change-in-risk metrics the guide works with.
- The guide's numeric guideline tables live in the guide and are not reproduced here; no numeric threshold, guideline value, or region boundary appears in this pack.
