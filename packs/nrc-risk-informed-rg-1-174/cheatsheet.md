# Cheatsheet: nrc-risk-informed-rg-1-174

Decision rules for which chapter owns a question, the pin rule, the not-advice rule, the numeric limits, and where questions run past this pack.

## Which chapter owns the question

- What RG 1.174 Rev. 3 is, its pin, what it covers, or its relation to the Part 50 licensing basis pack: ch01.
- The framework's stages and how PRA insights, defense-in-depth, safety margins, and performance-based consideration are weighed together: ch02.
- The engineering analysis stage and the change-in-risk metrics ΔCDF and ΔLERF: ch03.
- The 50.59 two-limb gate, the "more than a minimal increase" threshold wording, the FSAR (as updated) baseline, and where a risk-informed change meets that lane: ch04.
- Defense-in-depth, safety margins, and performance-based consideration as framework considerations: ch05.
- PRA technical adequacy (RG 1.200) and uncertainty treatment (NUREG-1855) as name-only hooks: ch06.
- Implementation and monitoring after an approved change, and the staff review framing: ch07.

## Pin rule

RG 1.174 Rev. 3, 2018, accession ML17317A256; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared; Rev 3 current, DG-1285 was the Rev 3 proposal). Later revisions of RG 1.174 are out of scope. Verify any point against the guide itself before relying on it.

## Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized, not copied: this pack quotes nothing, and verbatim excerpts of RG 1.174 text are capped at two sentences by licence policy. These notes are not legal advice and are not plant-specific risk advice. Verify everything against the current guide text.

## Numeric limits

- No numeric guideline values appear in this pack: no acceptance threshold, guideline value, or region boundary.
- The guide's numeric guideline tables live in the guide and are not reproduced here.
- ΔCDF (change in core damage frequency) and ΔLERF (change in large early release frequency) are metric names only here, with no values attached.
- RG 1.200 criteria and NUREG-1855 methods are named only, not packaged.

## When the question is thin

Questions that need guideline values, PRA adequacy criteria, uncertainty methods, a 50.59 screening, or plant-specific risk content are out of scope for this pack: say so, and point the reader at the guide and its companion documents rather than improvising from these notes.
