# Glossary: nrc-risk-informed-rg-1-174

Synthesized orientation definitions keyed to this pack's RG 1.174 Rev. 3 facts, not substitute source text. Alphabetical.

- **50.59 lane**: the no-amendment change lane in 10 CFR 50 section 50.59: a change passes only when both limbs hold, no technical specification amendment is required and none of the eight paragraph (c)(2) criteria applies, screened against the FSAR (as updated). If either limb fails, the change does not qualify under that lane. This pack orients the lane and carries no criterion list or screening procedure. (ch04)
- **change in core damage frequency**: spelled out, ΔCDF: the core-damage-side change-in-risk metric the guide works with. A metric name in this pack only; no numeric value is stated here, and the guide's numeric guideline tables live in the guide. (ch03)
- **change in large early release frequency**: spelled out, ΔLERF: the release-side change-in-risk metric the guide works with. A metric name in this pack only; no numeric value is stated here, and the guide's numeric guideline tables live in the guide. (ch03)
- **defense-in-depth**: a framework-level consideration weighed alongside PRA insights in integrated decisionmaking; the framework statement is that defense-in-depth is maintained through the change. This pack carries the statement, not any plant-specific judgement. (ch05)
- **engineering analysis**: the guide's second stage, between defining the proposed change and integrated decisionmaking, where the analysis works the change-in-risk metrics ΔCDF and ΔLERF. (ch02)
- **FSAR (as updated)**: the 50.59 screening baseline: the final safety analysis report as amended and supplemented, updated per 50.71(e) or (f). Carried here as the baseline fact only. (ch04)
- **implementation and monitoring**: the guide's fourth stage: implementation and monitoring follow an approved change, and the change is followed after the decision. (ch07)
- **integrated decisionmaking**: the weighing stage of the framework: PRA insights, defense-in-depth, safety margins, and performance-based consideration are weighed together rather than resting on PRA numbers alone. (ch02)
- **licensing basis**: what a plant-specific change under the guide changes. The Part 50 root facts live in the live Part 50 licensing basis pack; this pack pairs with it on the risk-informed framework side. (ch01)
- **NUREG-1855**: guidance on the treatment of uncertainties associated with PRAs in risk-informed decisionmaking, owner U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission. Name-only hook: its methods are not packaged here. (ch06)
- **performance-based consideration**: one of the framework inputs weighed in integrated decisionmaking alongside PRA insights, defense-in-depth, and safety margins; part of the framework. (ch05)
- **PRA technical adequacy**: the named consideration of whether the PRA behind a risk-informed decision is adequate for the application. RG 1.200 Rev 3 covers it; its criteria are not packaged here. (ch06)
- **probabilistic risk assessment**: the analysis whose insights are one input in the framework, combined with defense-in-depth, safety margins, and performance-based consideration. This pack carries no PRA methodology instruction. (ch01)
- **RG 1.200**: NRC Regulatory Guide 1.200, Rev 3, December 2020, accession ML20238B871, owner U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission, covering PRA technical adequacy. Name-only hook: its criteria are not packaged here. (ch06)
- **risk-informed decision**: a decision, here on a plant-specific change to the licensing basis, that weighs PRA insights together with defense-in-depth, safety margins, and performance-based consideration through integrated decisionmaking. (ch01)
- **safety margins**: a framework-level consideration weighed alongside PRA insights; the framework statement is that safety margins are maintained through the change. This pack carries the statement, not any plant-specific judgement. (ch05)
