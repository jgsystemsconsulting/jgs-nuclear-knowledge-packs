## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Integrated decisionmaking, not PRA numbers alone

The framework weighs inputs together: PRA insights are one input, combined with defense-in-depth, safety margins, and performance-based consideration, and integrated decisionmaking weighs those inputs together. A PRA number never carries a decision by itself in this framework; the other considerations stand alongside it, and defense-in-depth and safety margins are maintained through the change.

### Stages of a risk-informed change

The guide's stages run: define the proposed change, engineering analysis, integrated decisionmaking, implementation and monitoring. The engineering analysis stage works the change-in-risk metrics ΔCDF (change in core damage frequency) and ΔLERF (change in large early release frequency), and implementation and monitoring follow an approved change. A question is located by naming which stage it touches.

### Pin rule

This pack pins RG 1.174 Rev. 3, 2018, accession ML17317A256, pin date 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared: Rev 3 is current, no Rev 4 found as of that search date, and DG-1285 was the Rev 3 proposal). Later revisions of the guide are out of scope. The guide text, not this pack, states the framework detail and the numeric guideline tables themselves.

### Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized. They are not the guide, not the regulation, and not counsel. This pack quotes nothing; RG verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences by licence policy. RG 1.200 Rev 3 and NUREG-1855 are named with revision and owner only, never linked, and their text is never reproduced here.

- RG 1.200 Rev 3 and NUREG-1855 are named only, with revision and owner, never linked; no adequacy criteria and no uncertainty methods are packaged.
