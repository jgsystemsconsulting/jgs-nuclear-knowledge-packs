# Glossary: nrc-non-lwr-pdc-rg-1-232

Terms this pack uses. Definitions are synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin (RG 1.232 Rev 0, April 2018, ML17325A611; verified via 83 FR 15178 on 2026-11-13), not a substitute for guide or regulation text. Alphabetical.

- **adaptation**: the criterion-area work of judging whether a GDC area applies to a non-LWR technology and recording one outcome with its basis, so the PDC set stays traceable to the GDC baseline. (ch02)
- **contents of applications**: the application-content rule family that requires principal design criteria in a safety analysis report path. Detailed text owned by neighbour packs; named here only. (ch01)
- **design certification**: one Part 52 licensing action in the same application-content family as the PDC requirement. Mechanics owned by the live Part 52 pack; named here only. (ch01)
- **functional containment**: containment performance framing judged against the functional role of multiple barriers rather than a single prescribed leak-tight structure. Orientation concept only; no method ships. (ch06)
- **general design criteria**: the 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix A criteria set written for water-cooled reactors and used as the baseline structure for developing non-LWR PDC. Overview owned by the live Part 50 pack. (ch01)
- **licensing basis**: shorthand for the set of requirements a facility is held to. This pack maps PDC development orientation only and is never itself a plant licensing basis. (ch01)
- **mechanistic source term**: source term derived from physical mechanisms of fuel behaviour and radionuclide transport rather than a generic bounding release. Orientation concept only; no method ships. (ch06)
- **MHTGR-DC**: the RG 1.232 appendix family label for modular high-temperature gas-cooled reactor design criteria. Family label only; no criterion text packaged. (ch04)
- **MSR-DC**: the RG 1.232 appendix family label for molten salt reactor design criteria. Family label only; no criterion text packaged. (ch05)
- **non-light water reactor**: a reactor other than a water-cooled light water design, whose PDC are developed by adapting the GDC baseline rather than adopting it unchanged. (ch01)
- **principal design criteria**: the design criteria an application must include as required application content. This pack orients how a non-LWR applicant develops the set; it carries no criterion text. (ch01)
- **SFR-DC**: the RG 1.232 appendix family label for sodium-cooled fast reactor design criteria. Family label only; no criterion text packaged. (ch03)
