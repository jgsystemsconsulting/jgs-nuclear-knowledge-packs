## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### GDC baseline, not GDC adoption

10 CFR Part 50 Appendix A general design criteria were written for water-cooled reactors. Non-LWR applicants develop principal design criteria by treating the GDC set as a baseline structure and adapting it area by area, not by adopting GDC wording unchanged.

### Adaptation outcomes, recorded per area

For each criterion area the method asks whether the area applies to the non-LWR technology, then records an outcome: carry over, adapt wording, replace with a technology-specific criterion, or judge inapplicable. Traceability back to the GDC baseline is documentation discipline, not optional commentary.

### Three families as exemplars, never a closed set

RG 1.232 Appendix A carries sodium-cooled fast reactor design criteria (SFR-DC), Appendix B modular high-temperature gas-cooled reactor design criteria (MHTGR-DC), and Appendix C molten salt reactor design criteria (MSR-DC). Families illustrate the approach at technology-family level. Designs outside the three still need their own PDC by the same method. Families are never ranked and never blended.

### Pin rule

This pack pins RG 1.232 Rev 0, April 2018, accession ML17325A611, verified via Federal Register notice 83 FR 15178 on 2026-11-13. Live NRC listing page checked the same date returned 404 (dead listing); FR pin stands. Guide PDF body was not captured. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope.

### Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin and the closed statement list. This pack is not the guide, not regulation, and not counsel. A Regulatory Guide describes one method NRC staff finds acceptable; it is guidance, not regulation.
