# Cheatsheet: nrc-non-lwr-pdc-rg-1-232

Decision rules for which chapter owns a question, the pin rule, not-advice limits, family-level limits, and the honest fallback.

## Which chapter owns the question

- RG 1.232 scope, purpose, pin facts, and regulatory foundation: ch01.
- Adaptation methodology, applicability, outcomes, traceability, numbering families: ch02.
- SFR-DC family-level orientation: ch03.
- MHTGR-DC family-level orientation: ch04.
- MSR-DC family-level orientation, plus the route for designs outside the three families: ch05.
- Functional containment and mechanistic source term as orientation concepts: ch06.
- Neighbours, boundaries, forward hooks, and pack limits: ch07.

## Pin rule

Pack pins RG 1.232 Rev 0, April 2018, ML17325A611; verified via Federal Register notice 83 FR 15178 on 2026-11-13. Guide PDF body not captured. Later revisions out of scope.

## Not source and not advice

Synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin and closed statement list; zero quotations. This pack is not the guide, not regulation, not legal advice, and not licensing counsel. It is never a plant licensing basis and carries no design-basis or criterion-compliance instruction.

## Family-level limits

- SFR-DC, MHTGR-DC, and MSR-DC covered at family level only; criterion wording, numbers, titles, and counts not packaged here.
- Families never ranked and never blended; each family stays in its own chapter.
- Designs outside the three families develop their own PDC by the ch02 GDC-baseline adaptation approach; exemplars illustrate and do not bound.
- Functional containment and mechanistic source term at orientation depth only; no method, no target, no calculation ships.
- RG 1.233 and TICAP named only as the forward hook; 10 CFR Part 53 named only with no rule text and no status claim.

## When question is thin

Three classes stop at the pack boundary: (1) appendix criterion level, meaning wording, number, or text of any SFR-DC, MHTGR-DC, or MSR-DC criterion, or a per-criterion GDC to PDC mapping; (2) design-specific analysis, meaning whether a named design meets a criterion, a mechanistic source term computation, or a functional containment demonstration; (3) forward method, meaning RG 1.233, TICAP, or Part 53 methodology detail.

This knowledge pack provides high-level orientation on non-LWR principal design criteria adaptation under RG 1.232; it does not reproduce criterion text or evaluate plant-specific designs. For full criterion text, consult Regulatory Guide 1.232; for plant-specific licensing basis, consult licensed engineering personnel.
