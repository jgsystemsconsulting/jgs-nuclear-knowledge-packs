# Chapter 7: Cross-Pack Neighbours and Limits

Sources: NRC RG 1.232 Rev 0, Developing Principal Design Criteria for Non-Light Water Reactors, April 2018, ML17325A611; verified via Federal Register notice 83 FR 15178 on 2026-11-13. RG 1.233 and 10 CFR Part 53 named only, not sources.

## Core Idea

This pack orients one slice: developing non-LWR principal design criteria under RG 1.232. Everything past that slice belongs to neighbours this chapter names in prose only, never by link. The live Part 50 pack carries the 10 CFR 50.34 application-content text and the Appendix A general design criteria overview; this pack names the foundation and does not restate their text. The live Part 52 pack carries design certification, combined license, ITAAC, and RG 1.206 application structure. Non-LWR licensing-basis methodology beyond PDC development (RG 1.233, TICAP) is named only here; its pack is a later backlog item. 10 CFR Part 53 is named only; no rule text is packaged here and its status is not described from drafts.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Part 50 neighbour boundary. Application-content text and the GDC overview live with the Part 50 pack. This pack names that foundation and stops there.
- Part 52 neighbour boundary. Design certification, combined license, ITAAC, and application structure live with the Part 52 pack. This pack never restates Part 52 mechanics.
- Forward hook boundary. Licensing-basis methodology past PDC development belongs to a later pack, named only here.
- Rule boundary. Part 53 is named only. No rule text ships and no status claim ships.

## Key Concepts

- Application-content requirement: the rule family that requires principal design criteria in a safety analysis report path. Text owned by neighbour packs; named here only.
- Design certification: one Part 52 licensing action whose application-content mechanics belong to the live Part 52 pack, not this pack.
- Combined license: one Part 52 licensing action whose mechanics belong to the live Part 52 pack, not this pack.
- ITAAC: inspection, test, analysis, and acceptance criteria mechanics owned by the live Part 52 pack; named here only.
- Forward methodology: non-LWR licensing-basis work past PDC development, including RG 1.233 and TICAP; named only here, never summarized.
- Pack stop line: the point where orientation ends and the reader returns to the guide, the neighbour packs, or licensed engineering personnel.

## Mental Models

Read this chapter as a boundary map, not a second body of content. Inside the line sits PDC development orientation under RG 1.232. Outside the line sit the Part 50 text, the Part 52 mechanics, the forward licensing-basis methodology, and the Part 53 rule. When a question points outside the line, the honest move is to name the neighbour and stop, not to stretch this pack to cover it.

## Anti-patterns

Restating neighbour text inside this pack is the named anti-pattern: no 10 CFR text, no Appendix A overview, no Part 52 mechanics, no RG 1.233 or TICAP summary, and no Part 53 rule description ship here. A companion error treats a named-only hook as coverage, as if naming RG 1.233 or Part 53 answered a method or rule question. A third error asks this pack for a design-specific or plant-specific verdict it cannot give.
