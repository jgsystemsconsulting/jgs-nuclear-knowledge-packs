# Chapter 3: SFR-DC, Sodium-Cooled Fast Reactor Design Criteria Family

Sources: NRC RG 1.232 Rev 0, Developing Principal Design Criteria for Non-Light Water Reactors, April 2018, ML17325A611; verified via Federal Register notice 83 FR 15178 on 2026-11-13.

## Core Idea

RG 1.232 Appendix A carries the sodium-cooled fast reactor design criteria (SFR-DC) as one technology-family exemplar. At family level the set shows where sodium coolant properties, fast-spectrum reactivity behaviour, and metallic-fuel-system characteristics change which GDC areas apply and how adaptation questions arise. The number, titles, and text of criteria inside Appendix A are not on record in this repo and are not stated in this pack. No individual criterion is named, numbered, titled, counted, or quoted here.

## Frameworks Introduced

- SFR-DC family label. SFR-DC names the sodium-cooled fast reactor numbering family used as an RG 1.232 Appendix A exemplar. The label marks a family, not any single criterion.
- Family-level adaptation focus. The chapter frames the class of GDC-area questions the SFR technology raises under the ch02 adaptation walk, without restating appendix text.
- On-record limit. Appendix mapping is on record at family level only. Criterion wording, numbers, titles, and counts stay outside this pack.

## Key Concepts

- Sodium-cooled fast reactor (SFR): a non-LWR technology class that uses sodium coolant in a fast neutron spectrum, often with a metallic fuel system.
- SFR-DC: the RG 1.232 Appendix A exemplar family for sodium-cooled fast reactor principal design criteria, treated here only as a family label.
- Sodium coolant properties: coolant behaviour that differs from water and therefore changes which GDC areas apply and what wording those areas need.
- Fast-spectrum reactivity behaviour: reactivity characteristics of a fast spectrum that raise adaptation questions the LWR GDC baseline did not frame for water-cooled designs.
- Metallic-fuel-system characteristics: fuel-form behaviour that shifts barrier, coolability, and related GDC-area questions relative to LWR practice.
- Family-level statement only: the chapter classifies where adaptation questions arise for this technology class. It never decides a real design's PDC set.

## Mental Models

Treat Appendix A as a worked example of the ch02 walk applied to one technology family, not as a criterion catalog this pack holds. Ask where sodium coolant, fast-spectrum reactivity, and metallic fuel force a different applicability answer or a different adaptation outcome than an LWR baseline would. Keep every answer at that class-of-questions depth. When the question names a specific SFR-DC criterion, number, title, or count, the pack has no on-record answer and routes the reader to RG 1.232 itself.

## Anti-patterns

Inventing or restating individual SFR-DC criterion text, numbers, titles, or counts is the chapter's named anti-pattern: those details are not on record here. A companion error is reading this family-level chapter as a finished PDC set for any real sodium-cooled fast reactor. A third error is blending SFR adaptation questions with MHTGR or MSR questions as if the three families were interchangeable.
