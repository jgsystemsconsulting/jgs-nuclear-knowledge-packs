# Chapter 6: Functional Containment and Mechanistic Source Term in PDC Context

Sources: NRC RG 1.232 Rev 0, Developing Principal Design Criteria for Non-Light Water Reactors, April 2018, ML17325A611; verified via Federal Register notice 83 FR 15178 on 2026-11-13. Functional containment and mechanistic source term framed orientation concepts only; no source line beyond RG 1.232 Rev 0 added.

## Core Idea

Functional containment and mechanistic source term are concepts that bear on how non-LWR principal design criteria are framed. Functional containment is a containment performance framing judged by the functional role of multiple barriers rather than a single prescribed leak-tight structure. Mechanistic source term is a source term derived from the physical mechanisms of fuel behaviour and radionuclide transport rather than a generic bounding release. Both concepts touch the PDC areas a non-LWR applicant works through, and both stay at orientation depth in this pack.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Functional containment framing. Containment performance is judged by what the set of barriers does together, not by matching one prescribed leak-tight structure shape.
- Mechanistic source term framing. The source term follows from physical mechanisms of fuel behaviour and radionuclide transport, not from a generic bounding release picked without those mechanisms.
- PDC bearing. Both concepts bear on how some non-LWR PDC are framed and on which PDC areas they touch, without deciding any design PDC set.

## Key Concepts

- Functional containment: containment performance framing judged against the functional role of multiple barriers rather than a single prescribed leak-tight structure. (ch06 orientation concept only)
- Mechanistic source term: source term derived from physical mechanisms of fuel behaviour and radionuclide transport rather than a generic bounding release. (ch06 orientation concept only)
- Multiple barriers: the set of barriers whose combined functional role carries containment performance in this framing, rather than one structure alone.
- Fuel behaviour mechanisms: physical behaviour of the fuel that feeds a mechanistic source term view.
- Radionuclide transport mechanisms: physical transport behaviour that feeds a mechanistic source term view.
- Orientation depth only: this chapter names the concepts and their PDC bearing; it states no calculation method, no acceptance value, and no analysis sequence.
