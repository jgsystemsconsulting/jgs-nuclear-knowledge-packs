# Chapter 1: RG 1.232 Scope, Purpose, and Regulatory Foundation

Sources: NRC RG 1.232 Rev 0, Developing Principal Design Criteria for Non-Light Water Reactors, April 2018, ML17325A611; verified via Federal Register notice 83 FR 15178 on 2026-11-13. 10 CFR 50.34, 10 CFR Part 52, and 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix A named as the regulatory foundation; their regulatory text is owned by live sibling packs and is not restated here.

## Core Idea

RG 1.232 Rev 0 (April 2018, ML17325A611; 83 FR 15178, April 9, 2018) gives guidance for developing principal design criteria for non-light water reactors. A Regulatory Guide describes one method the NRC staff finds acceptable; it is guidance, not regulation, and its text binds no licencee through this pack. Principal design criteria are required application content under 10 CFR 50.34 and the Part 52 application-content family. The 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix A general design criteria (GDC), written for water-cooled reactors, supply the baseline structure a non-LWR applicant adapts rather than adopts unchanged.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Purpose and applicability. RG 1.232 orients how a non-LWR applicant develops a principal design criteria set. The guide is the staff-position source; this pack is synthesized orientation keyed to the recorded pin, not the guide and not a substitute for it.
- Regulatory requirement for PDC. 10 CFR 50.34 requires the principal design criteria for a facility in the preliminary safety analysis report. 10 CFR Part 52 carries the same application-content family into design certification, combined license, and related Part 52 actions.
- GDC as baseline structure. Part 50 Appendix A GDC were written for water-cooled (light water) reactors. That set is the baseline structure for developing non-LWR principal design criteria, not a ready-made text a non-LWR design may adopt unchanged.

## Mental Models

Read RG 1.232 as guidance on how to develop a PDC set, not as a regulation that itself supplies the set. Place the GDC first as baseline structure: a non-LWR applicant starts from the Appendix A areas and adapts them to the technology, rather than copying LWR wording into a non-LWR application. Treat this pack as a map to the pin and the method shape; the guide itself, and the live Part 50 and Part 52 packs for foundation text, hold what a real application reads against.

## Anti-patterns

Treating orientation notes as a substitute for RG 1.232 is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the notes locate scope, purpose, and foundation; the guide carries staff-position detail. A companion error is adopting the Appendix A GDC unchanged for a non-LWR design, as if water-cooled criteria were technology-neutral text. A third error is restating 50.34 or Part 52 mechanics from this pack; those texts stay with their live sibling packs.

## Key Takeaways

- RG 1.232 Rev 0 (April 2018, ML17325A611; 83 FR 15178) guides development of principal design criteria for non-light water reactors; it is guidance, not regulation.
- Principal design criteria are required application content under 10 CFR 50.34 and the Part 52 application-content family.
- Part 50 Appendix A GDC were written for water-cooled reactors and serve as the baseline structure for non-LWR PDC development, not as unchanged text to adopt.
- This pack orients foundation and pin only; foundation regulatory text is owned by live sibling packs and is not restated here.
