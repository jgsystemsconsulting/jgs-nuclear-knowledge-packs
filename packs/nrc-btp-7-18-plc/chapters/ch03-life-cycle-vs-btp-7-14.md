## Core Idea

PLC life-cycle method questions leave this pack. `nrc-nuclear` ch02 holds life-cycle process notes. `nrc-nuclear` ch07 holds the BTP 7-14 staff review of implementation and design outputs. This chapter only marks that handoff. The handoff is thin here.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Split labour. This pack orients the PLC question. It does not carry the BTP 7-14 review method.
- Edition rule. Do not treat a later BTP 7-18 revision as this pack's text.

## Key Concepts

- A life-cycle question about plans, implementation, or design outputs is a `nrc-nuclear` question.
- This chapter does not restate BTP 7-14 acceptance criteria.
- This chapter does not invent PLC life-cycle criteria from the unread PDF.
