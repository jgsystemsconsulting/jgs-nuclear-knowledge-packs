# Chapter 7: Pack-use anti-patterns

Sources: NUREG-0800 Branch Technical Position 7-18, Rev. 6, August 2016, ML16019A327. Listing-page title: "Guidance on the Use of Programmable Logic Controllers in Digital Computer-Based Instrumentation and Control Systems". PDF body was not captured. No page range.

## Core Idea

This chapter holds pack-use failures. They are limits on how to use these notes. They are not unread BTP findings.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Pack-use list. The failures below are rules of this pack, not frameworks attributed to BTP 7-18.

## Key Concepts

- Quoting the BTP is a pack-use failure.
- Citing unread section numbers is a pack-use failure.
- Treating `nrc-nuclear` as the PLC position is a pack-use failure.
- Treating IEEE text as in-pack is a pack-use failure.
- Ignoring the edition pin is a pack-use failure.
- Giving licensing advice is a pack-use failure.

## Mental Models

If a sentence claims the BTP said it, and the PDF was not captured, the sentence is the anti-pattern. Delete it.

## Anti-patterns

- Do not quote the BTP.
- Do not cite an unread section number.
- Do not treat `nrc-nuclear` as the PLC position.
- Do not treat IEEE or IEC text as in-pack.
- Do not ignore the edition pin (Rev. 6, August 2016, ML16019A327).
- Do not give licensing advice.

## Key Takeaways

- These six failures are the pack-use list.
- Other chapters point here instead of restating a new BTP framework.
