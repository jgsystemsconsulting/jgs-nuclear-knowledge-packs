# Chapter 5: Failure modes as a staff-eye question

Sources: NUREG-0800 Branch Technical Position 7-18, Rev. 6, August 2016, ML16019A327. Listing-page title: "Guidance on the Use of Programmable Logic Controllers in Digital Computer-Based Instrumentation and Control Systems". PDF body was not captured. No page range.

## Core Idea

Failure modes are a review-lens question in this pack. This chapter does not state a failure mode as something BTP 7-18 requires. It does not give a test procedure.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Review lens, not test procedure. The question is what a reader should ask next, not how to test a PLC.
- Not source and not advice. A lens is not a staff finding.

## Key Concepts

- The staff-eye label means the question is about review attention, not about a captured test method.
- No failure-mode list from the BTP appears here, because the PDF body was not captured.
- Do not convert this question into licensing advice.

## Mental Models

A failure-mode question stays a question until a captured source states the mode. This pack does not have that capture.

## Anti-patterns

See ch07. Local limit: do not list a failure mode as a BTP requirement.

## Key Takeaways

- Failure modes here are a review lens.
- They are not a test procedure and not a captured BTP list.
