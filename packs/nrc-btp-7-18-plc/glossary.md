# Glossary: nrc-btp-7-18-plc

Terms this pack uses. IEEE and IEC appear by designation only. Alphabetical.

- **BTP 7-18**: NUREG-0800 Branch Technical Position 7-18, Rev. 6, August 2016, ML16019A327. Listing-page title: "Guidance on the Use of Programmable Logic Controllers in Digital Computer-Based Instrumentation and Control Systems". PDF body was not captured. (ch01)
- **commercial dedication**: the question of a commercial PLC in a digital safety I&C review. Not a procedure. This pack does not invent dedication steps. (ch04)
- **edition rule**: this pack pins Rev. 6, August 2016, ML16019A327. Later revisions are out of scope. The plant licensing basis, not this pack, decides which revision binds. (ch01, cheatsheet)
- **firmware**: the embedded logic image on a PLC, used here as a question label, not as an unread BTP criterion. (ch02)
- **ML accession**: the ADAMS identifier for the pinned document. This pack's accession is ML16019A327. (ch01)
- **name-only rule**: IEEE and IEC standards are cited by designation and edition only. IEEE Std 603 and IEEE Std 7-4.3.2 may be named. Their text is not in this pack. (ch08)
- **PLC**: programmable logic controller. The device class this pack orients. Not a captured BTP definition. (ch01)
