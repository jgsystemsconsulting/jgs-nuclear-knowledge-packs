# Cheatsheet: nrc-btp-7-18-plc

Decision rules for which chapter owns a PLC question, the edition pin, the name-only rule, and when the question is thin.

## Which chapter owns the question

- PLC role in digital safety I&C: ch01.
- Software versus firmware as a question boundary: ch02.
- Life-cycle method versus BTP 7-14: ch03, then leave for `nrc-nuclear`.
- Commercial dedication as a question, not a procedure: ch04.
- Failure modes as a review lens, not a test procedure: ch05.
- Documentation set as a question, not an invented product list: ch06.
- Pack-use failures: ch07.
- RG 1.152 or Appendix 7.1-D from a PLC question: ch08, then leave for `nrc-nuclear`.

## Edition rule

Pin Rev. 6, August 2016, ML16019A327. Later revisions are out of scope. The plant licensing basis, not this pack, decides which revision binds.

## Name-only rule

IEEE and IEC: designation and edition only. IEEE Std 603 and IEEE Std 7-4.3.2 may be named. No clause text.

## When the question is thin

- BTP 7-14 software life-cycle method, plan acceptance, and design-output scoring: thin here, use `nrc-nuclear` ch02 and ch07.
- RG 1.152 criteria and secure development and operational environment: thin here, use `nrc-nuclear` ch09.
- Appendix 7.1-D evaluation of IEEE Std 7-4.3.2: thin here, use `nrc-nuclear` ch10.
