# Chapter 6: Neighbours and limits

Sources: NRC RG 1.200 Rev. 3, Acceptability Probabilistic Risk Assessment Results Risk-Informed Activities, December 2020, ML20238B871; listing-page pin 2026-11-08, re-checked 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

This pack stops at adequacy orientation for named applications, pinned to RG 1.200 Rev. 3 pin facts re-checked at build. Three neighbours and the limits close the pack. What this pack is not: not the guide, not the endorsed standard, not a PRA, not a legal or plant-specific how-to. Its product is orientation questions about a named use, never findings about a real plant PRA.

## Frameworks Introduced

- The decision framework home: the RG 1.174 risk-informed decisions pack carries the risk-informed decision framework that these adequacy questions stand behind; it is named in prose here and never linked or restated.
- The uncertainty guidance home: NUREG-1855 is NRC guidance on the treatment of uncertainties associated with PRAs in risk-informed decisionmaking; it is named as number and description only, and the full title and revision are not on record in this repo.
- The review plan hook: NUREG-0800 Standard Review Plan Chapter 19 is named only; its review procedures are not packaged here, and nothing further is named.

## Key Concepts

- Neighbour discipline: each neighbour is named for orientation, and naming a neighbour packages nothing from it.
- Endorsed standard: ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 (Addendum A) stands in this pack as designation and endorsement fact only (ch03).
- Guidance frame: a Regulatory Guide describes one method the staff finds acceptable; guidance, not regulation, binding no licensee through this pack (ch01).
- Pin: RG 1.200 Rev. 3, December 2020, ML20238B871; listing-page pin 2026-11-08, re-checked 2026-11-11; the pack carries adequacy orientation only.

## Mental Models

Read the neighbour names as signposts at the edge of this pack: the signpost says where a topic lives, and the signpost itself carries no content from the place it names.

## Anti-patterns

Summarizing a neighbour from memory is the named anti-pattern: a named neighbour is not a coverage promise, and restating its content here would invent material. A companion error is reading the neighbour names as links; neighbours stay prose-only in this pack.

## Key Takeaways

- The pack is adequacy orientation only: not the guide, not the endorsed standard, not a PRA, not legal or plant-specific how-to.
- The RG 1.174 risk-informed decisions pack holds the decision framework; NUREG-1855 holds the uncertainty treatment guidance; SRP Chapter 19 is named only.
- Two thin classes stop at the pack boundary: guide-position, endorsement-detail, and standard-requirement questions; and PRA method, quantification, and plant-specific adequacy questions.
