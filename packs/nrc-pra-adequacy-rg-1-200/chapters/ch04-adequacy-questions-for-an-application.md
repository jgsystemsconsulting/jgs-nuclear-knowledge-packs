# Chapter 4: Adequacy questions for an application

Sources: NRC RG 1.200 Rev. 3, Acceptability of Probabilistic Risk Assessment Results for Risk-Informed Activities, December 2020, ML20238B871; listing-page pin 2026-11-08, re-checked 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

Before leaning on a PRA for a named application, the reader asks orientation questions about it: what the PRA covers, what it leaves out, and whether that scope matches the named use. These questions elaborate the adequacy subject of ch01 and the scoping rule of ch02. They locate where an adequacy answer would come from; they are not themselves the answer, and this pack carries no criteria for answering them.

## Frameworks Introduced

- The question set, in question form: What does the PRA cover for this application? What does it leave out that the use would lean on? Does the scope match the named application and the decision the PRA would inform?
- The scoping tie: each question is tied to the named use from ch02, so the set is asked again, and answered again, whenever the use changes.

## Key Concepts

- The questions are orientation prompts: they point at where an adequacy answer would live, in the guide and its endorsed standard, not in this pack.
- The endorsement hook behind the questions: the guide names an endorsed standard (ch03), and the standards-side detail the questions point at stays outside this pack.
- The weighing that follows: once a reader has answers from the guide-side sources, they are weighed inside a risk-informed decision as ch05 describes.
- Naming first: with the application named, the three questions have a target; without a named use, they have none.

## Mental Models

Ask the three questions with the application named first: coverage, gaps, and the match between scope and use. If no application is named, stop at ch02 and name the use, then ask.

## Anti-patterns

Reading the question set as a checklist or a pass/fail procedure is the named anti-pattern: these are orientation questions, not a review procedure. A companion error is answering the questions here for a real plant PRA; this pack makes no plant-specific adequacy findings.

## Key Takeaways

- The orientation questions are what the PRA covers, what it leaves out, and whether the scope matches the named use.
- Each question is tied to a named application; a change of use re-asks the set.
- The questions locate where an adequacy answer would come from; they are not a checklist and not an answer.

## Connects To

- ch02 for the scope judgement the questions serve.
- ch03 for the endorsement hook standing behind the questions.
- ch05 for where the answers would be weighed in a decision.
