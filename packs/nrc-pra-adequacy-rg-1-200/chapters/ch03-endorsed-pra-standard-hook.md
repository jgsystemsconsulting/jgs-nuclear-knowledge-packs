# Chapter 3: Endorsed PRA standard hook

Sources: NRC RG 1.200 Rev. 3, December 2020, ML20238B871 (listing-page pin 2026-11-08; re-check 2026-11-11). Endorsement on record: Rev. 3 endorses ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 (Addendum A) only.

## Core Idea

Per the pin gate record, RG 1.200 Rev. 3 endorses ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 (Addendum A). That is the endorsement hook: the guide names an endorsed standard for PRA technical adequacy, and this pack carries the designation and the endorsement fact, and nothing further from the standard. The guidance frame of ch01 still applies: the guide is guidance, not regulation.

## Frameworks Introduced

- The endorsement hook: RG 1.200 Rev. 3 names an endorsed standard; the pack records the designation ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 (Addendum A) and the fact of endorsement, at orientation level only.
- The dated-record rule: statements about revisions are worded as dated record states, never as forecasts about what the NRC will issue.

## Key Concepts

- Endorsement fact on record: Rev. 3 endorses ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 (Addendum A), per the pin gate record.
- Rev. 4 note on record: as of the 2026-11-08 search date, a Rev. 4 endorsing RA-Sa-2023 is under development and not yet issued. The build re-check on 2026-11-11 found nothing contradicting this record state; the pack states no forecast beyond it.
- What the pack does not carry: no standard requirement text, no numbering or mapping of standard content, no guide positions, and no review procedure. The endorsement hook names a standard; it does not summarize one.

## Mental Models

Read the endorsed-standard line as a pointer plate, not a summary: it says which standard the guide names as endorsed, and stops there. Questions about what the standard requires for any element are thin in this pack and stop at the pack boundary (ch06).

## Anti-patterns

Reciting standard requirements from memory is the named anti-pattern: a designation line is not standard coverage, and writing requirement detail from recollection invents content. A companion error is naming guide positions or reading the Rev. 4 record note as a forecast of an issuance.

## Key Takeaways

- RG 1.200 Rev. 3 endorses ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 (Addendum A), designation and endorsement fact only.
- As of the 2026-11-08 search date, a Rev. 4 endorsing RA-Sa-2023 is under development and not yet issued.
- Nothing of the standard is packaged here: no requirements, no numbering, no mapping, no procedures.
