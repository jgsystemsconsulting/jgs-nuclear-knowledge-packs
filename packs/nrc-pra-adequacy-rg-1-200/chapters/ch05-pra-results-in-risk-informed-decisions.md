# Chapter 5: PRA results in risk-informed decisions

Sources: NRC RG 1.200 Rev. 3, Acceptability of Probabilistic Risk Assessment Results for Risk-Informed Activities, December 2020, ML20238B871; listing-page pin 2026-11-08, re-checked 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

Before PRA results carry weight in a risk-informed decision, the technical adequacy of the PRA for the application is a named consideration standing behind the decision, alongside the treatment of uncertainty. The decision itself is integrated: adequate PRA results are one input, weighed alongside non-PRA considerations such as defense-in-depth and safety margins. The adequacy of the PRA does not by itself decide the application.

## Frameworks Introduced

- The standing-behind frame: two named considerations sit behind a decision that leans on a PRA, the technical adequacy of the PRA for the application and the treatment of uncertainty; both are named here, and neither is operated in this pack.
- The one-input frame: PRA results enter an integrated risk-informed decision as one input among non-PRA considerations, including defense-in-depth and safety margins; the weighing happens in the decision, not in this pack.
- Risk metrics at name-only depth: risk metrics such as core damage frequency (CDF) and large early release frequency (LERF) may be named where this statement needs them; no value is stated and no computation is described in this pack.

## Key Concepts

- Adequacy is a precondition named behind the decision, not the decision outcome: a reader who has settled adequacy for a named application has not settled the application.
- The companion consideration: the treatment of uncertainty stands alongside adequacy; its guidance home is named in ch06, and no uncertainty methods are packaged here.
- The decision framework these adequacy questions stand behind lives in the RG 1.174 risk-informed decisions pack. This pack does not restate that framework; the weighing of inputs is neighbour territory.

## Mental Models

Picture adequacy as a gate named behind a decision and the treatment of uncertainty as the gate beside it; passing both still leaves the decision to the integrated weighing of PRA results with defense-in-depth, safety margins, and the other non-PRA considerations.

## Anti-patterns

Letting the adequacy judgement read as the decision outcome is the named anti-pattern: adequacy for the application is one named consideration, never the verdict on the application. A companion error is restating framework stages or change-in-risk content; that material belongs to the neighbour pack named above, and this pack carries none of it.

## Key Takeaways

- Technical adequacy of the PRA for the application and the treatment of uncertainty are named considerations standing behind the decision.
- PRA results are one input in an integrated risk-informed decision, weighed with defense-in-depth and safety margins.
- Adequacy does not by itself decide the application, and the decision framework lives in the RG 1.174 risk-informed decisions pack, named in prose only.
