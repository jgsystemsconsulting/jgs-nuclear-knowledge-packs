# Cheatsheet: nrc-pra-adequacy-rg-1-200

Decision rules for which chapter owns the adequacy question, the pin rule, the not-advice rule, and the thin classes.

## Which chapter owns the question

- What RG 1.200 Rev. 3 is, its pin, or the adequacy question it answers: ch01.
- Whether an adequacy judgement carries over to another use: ch02.
- The endorsed standard designation and endorsement fact: ch03.
- What to ask of a PRA before leaning on it for a named application: ch04.
- Where adequacy sits in a risk-informed decision and PRA results as one input: ch05.
- Neighbours and limits, RG 1.174 named in prose and NUREG-1855 and SRP Chapter 19 named only: ch06.

## Pin rule

RG 1.200 Rev. 3, December 2020, accession ML20238B871; listing-page pin 2026-11-08, re-checked 2026-11-11. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope. Verify any point against the current guide listing before relying on it.

## Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized orientation notes; the pack quotes nothing, and RG verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences by licence policy. These notes are not the guide, not the endorsed standard, not legal or licensing advice, and not a plant-specific adequacy verdict.

## Adequacy limits

- Adequacy orientation only: questions about a named use, judged relative to the application.
- Not the RG 1.174 body. The decision framework lives in the RG 1.174 risk-informed decisions pack, named in prose.
- No endorsed-standard text. ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 named as designation and endorsement fact only.
- No PRA quantification how-to. CDF and LERF are named metrics only, no values.
- NUREG-1855 and SRP Chapter 19 named only; content not packaged.

## When the question is thin

Two classes of question are thin in this pack and stop at the pack boundary: (1) guide-position, endorsement-detail, or standard-requirement questions; (2) PRA method, quantification, or plant-specific adequacy questions. State the thin reason and stop.
