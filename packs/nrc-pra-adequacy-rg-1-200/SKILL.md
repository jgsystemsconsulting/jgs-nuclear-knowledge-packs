## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Adequacy is a question about a named use

RG 1.200 addresses the acceptability of PRA results used to support risk-informed activities. Its subject is whether the PRA behind such use is technically adequate for the application. The first move in any answer is to name the application and the decision the PRA would inform; adequacy talk without a named use has nothing to be judged against.

### Relative, never a blanket verdict

The adequacy judgement is scoped to the named application the PRA supports. The same PRA can be adequate for one application and not another, and a judgement made for one application does not transfer to another decision unlabeled.

### One input, never the whole decision

Before PRA results carry weight in a risk-informed decision, the technical adequacy of the PRA for that application is a named consideration standing behind the decision, alongside the treatment of uncertainty. Adequate PRA results are then one input weighed with non-PRA considerations such as defense-in-depth and safety margins; adequacy of the PRA does not by itself decide the application.

### Endorsement hook, designation only

Per the pin gate record, Rev. 3 endorses ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 (Addendum A). This pack states the designation and the endorsement fact only. No standard requirement text, numbering, or mapping detail is packaged.

### Pin rule

This pack pins RG 1.200 Rev. 3, December 2020, accession ML20238B871; listing-page pin 2026-11-08, re-checked 2026-11-11. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope. Verify any point against the current guide listing before relying on it.

### Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized orientation notes from pin facts and closed framework statements. They are not the guide, not the endorsed standard, not a regulation, and not counsel. A Regulatory Guide describes one method the NRC staff finds acceptable; it is guidance, not a regulation. This pack quotes nothing; RG verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences by licence policy.

## Scope & Limits

This pack pins RG 1.200 Rev. 3, December 2020, ML20238B871 (listing-page pin 2026-11-08, re-checked 2026-11-11). Later revisions of the guide are out of scope.

- Adequacy orientation only: whether a PRA is technically adequate for a named application, per RG 1.200 Rev. 3 pin facts. This is not the guide, not the endorsed standard, and not a PRA.
- Not RG 1.174 body. The risk-informed decision framework, change-in-risk metrics, and licensing-basis connection live in the RG 1.174 risk-informed decisions pack, named in prose, never linked.
- No endorsed-standard text. ASME/ANS RA-Sa-2009 is named by designation and endorsement fact only; its requirements are never packaged.
- No plant-specific adequacy verdict and no application acceptance judgement. Adequacy statements here are orientation questions about a named use, never findings about a real plant PRA.
- No PRA quantification how-to. Building, updating, or quantifying a PRA is out of scope; CDF and LERF are named metrics only.
- Not legal advice and not licensing advice. Advice requests refused.
- Zero quotations. Synthesized notes keyed to the recorded pin and build re-check; RG verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences by licence policy and this pack uses none.
- No slug links to any sibling pack. Neighbours are named in prose only, per repo discipline.
- Two question classes are thin in this pack and stop at the pack boundary: (1) guide-position, endorsement-detail, or standard-requirement questions (which position applies, what the standard requires for an element, how a peer review runs); (2) PRA method, quantification, or plant-specific adequacy questions (how to build or quantify a PRA, whether a named plant PRA is adequate).
