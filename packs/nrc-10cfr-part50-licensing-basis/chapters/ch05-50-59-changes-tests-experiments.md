# Chapter 5: 50.59 changes, tests, experiments

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 (eCFR), section 50.59; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

Section 50.59 is the bounded change lane: it lets a licensee change the facility or procedures as described in the updated final safety analysis report, and conduct tests or experiments not described there, without a license amendment, only when no technical specification amendment is required and none of eight screening criteria is met. Otherwise the licensee must obtain an amendment before implementing the change. This chapter maps the definitions, the gate, the criteria, and the paperwork.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Definition-driven screening. The section defines change, departure from a method of evaluation, facility as described in the FSAR (as updated), procedures as described, and tests or experiments not described; the screening runs on those definitions.
- Two-condition gate. A no-amendment change must pass both limbs: a technical specification amendment is not required, and none of the eight paragraph (c)(2) criteria applies.

## Key Concepts

- The FSAR (as updated) means the report submitted under 50.34 as amended and supplemented and updated per 50.71(e) or (f); screening runs against that updated document, including changes made through prior 50.59 evaluations and 50.90 analyses since the last update.
- A change is a modification or addition to, or removal from, the facility or procedures that affects a design function, the method of performing or controlling the function, or an evaluation demonstrating intended functions.
- A departure from a method of evaluation means changing an element of an FSAR-described method unless the results are conservative or essentially the same, or switching to another method unless NRC approved it for the intended application.
- The eight criteria in paragraph (c)(2) requiring prior amendment: more than a minimal increase in the frequency or in the consequences of an accident previously evaluated; more than a minimal increase in the likelihood or in the consequences of a malfunction of a structure, system, or component important to safety previously evaluated; creation of a possibility for an accident of a different type, or for a malfunction with a different result, than previously evaluated; exceeding or altering a design basis limit for a fission product barrier; or a departure from a method of evaluation described in the FSAR (as updated).
- The section does not apply where applicable regulations establish more specific criteria for the change.
- Records: the licensee keeps a written evaluation providing the bases for each no-amendment determination, reports changes, tests, and experiments to the Commission at intervals not exceeding 24 months, and keeps facility-change records until license termination and procedure-change and test records for five years.
- Scope: the section binds operating license and combined license holders, including those that have permanently ceased operations or hold possession-only licenses.

## Mental Models

Run the eight criteria as eight questions about the updated FSAR, not about intent: frequency, likelihood, consequences, different type, different result, fission product barrier limit, method of evaluation. Any yes sends the change to the 50.90 amendment path; all no answers plus no technical specification impact keep it in the 50.59 lane with a written evaluation on file. The lane is narrow by construction: minimal is a floor, not a licence to redesign.

## Anti-patterns

Do not treat 50.59 as permission to change technical specifications: a required technical specification amendment fails the gate by itself. Do not screen against the original FSAR; the screening baseline is the FSAR as updated. Do not fold Appendix B quality program changes, security plan changes, or emergency plan changes into 50.59; those follow their own sections and paragraphs, which the scope exclusion covers.

## Key Takeaways

- 50.59 permits changes, tests, and experiments without amendment only through the two-condition gate and eight screening criteria.
- The screening baseline is the FSAR as updated per 50.71.
- Written evaluations, periodic reports, and record retention are mandatory parts of the lane.
