# Chapter 6: Appendix A General Design Criteria overview

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 (eCFR), Appendix A (intro, definitions, criteria index); eCFR pin-check 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

Appendix A holds the General Design Criteria: minimum requirements for the principal design criteria of water-cooled nuclear power plants similar in design and location to plants with prior construction permits, and guidance for establishing principal design criteria for other nuclear power units. The application hook is section 50.34, which requires principal design criteria in the preliminary safety analysis report, with parallel hooks in part 52 for design certifications, combined licenses, design approvals, and manufacturing licenses. This chapter is an index: group structure, criteria numbers and titles, and the appendix's own defined terms. It is not an essay on any criterion.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Minimum-requirements frame. The criteria set a floor for principal design criteria; an applicant's principal design criteria sit on top of it.
- Group map. Sixty-four criteria organize into six lettered groups, each a protection theme.

## Key Concepts

- Appendix A defines its own terms: nuclear power unit, loss of coolant accidents, single failure, and anticipated operational occurrences.
- Group I, Overall Requirements: Quality Standards and Records (1), Design Bases for Protection Against Natural Phenomena (2), Fire Protection (3), Environmental and Dynamic Effects Design Bases (4), Sharing of Structures, Systems, and Components (5).
- Group II, Protection by Multiple Fission Product Barriers: Reactor Design (10), Reactor Inherent Protection (11), Suppression of Reactor Power Oscillations (12), Instrumentation and Control (13), Reactor Coolant Pressure Boundary (14), Reactor Coolant System Design (15), Containment Design (16), Electric Power Systems (17), Inspection and Testing of Electric Power Systems (18), Control Room (19).
- Group III, Protection and Reactivity Control Systems: Protection System Functions (20), Protection System Reliability and Testability (21), Protection System Independence (22), Protection System Failure Modes (23), Separation of Protection and Control Systems (24), Protection System Requirements for Reactivity Control Malfunctions (25), Reactivity Control System Redundancy and Capability (26), Combined Reactivity Control Systems Capability (27), Reactivity Limits (28), Protection Against Anticipated Operational Occurrences (29).
- Group IV, Fluid Systems: Quality of Reactor Coolant Pressure Boundary (30), Fracture Prevention of Reactor Coolant Pressure Boundary (31), Inspection of Reactor Coolant Pressure Boundary (32), Reactor Coolant Makeup (33), Residual Heat Removal (34), Emergency Core Cooling (35), Inspection of Emergency Core Cooling System (36), Testing of Emergency Core Cooling System (37), Containment Heat Removal (38), Inspection of Containment Heat Removal System (39), Testing of Containment Heat Removal System (40), Containment Atmosphere Cleanup (41), Inspection of Containment Atmosphere Cleanup Systems (42), Testing of Containment Atmosphere Cleanup Systems (43), Cooling Water (44), Inspection of Cooling Water System (45), Testing of Cooling Water System (46).
- Group V, Reactor Containment: Containment Design Basis (50), Fracture Prevention of Containment Pressure Boundary (51), Capability for Containment Leakage Rate Testing (52), Provisions for Containment Testing and Inspection (53), Systems Penetrating Containment (54), Reactor Coolant Pressure Boundary Penetrating Containment (55), Primary Containment Isolation (56), Closed Systems Isolation Valves (57).
- Group VI, Fuel and Radioactivity Control: Control of Releases of Radioactive Materials to the Environment (60), Fuel Storage and Handling and Radioactivity Control (61), Prevention of Criticality in Fuel Storage and Handling (62), Monitoring Fuel and Waste Storage (63), Monitoring Radioactivity Releases (64).

## Mental Models

Place a criterion by group before reading anything else: overall requirements set the cross-cutting duties, group II defends the fission product barriers, group III arms the protection and reactivity systems, group IV keeps the fluid safety systems serviceable, group V contains the containment, and group VI covers fuel and radioactivity control. The number gaps (6 to 9, 47 to 49, 58 to 59) are part of the historical record, not missing content to reconstruct.

## Anti-patterns

This pack carries titles only. Do not paraphrase criterion bodies from memory or treat a title as a requirement statement; the criterion text lives in Appendix A, and the guidance layer for design review sits in `nrc-nuclear`.

## Key Takeaways

- Appendix A is the floor for principal design criteria on water-cooled plants and guidance for other units.
- Six groups hold criteria 1 through 64 with numbering gaps.
- The application hook runs through 50.34 principal design criteria and the part 52 equivalents.
