# Chapter 7: Appendix B pointer

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 (eCFR), Appendix B (introduction only); eCFR pin-check 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

Appendix B, Quality Assurance Criteria for Nuclear Power Plants and Fuel Reprocessing Plants, establishes quality assurance requirements for the design, manufacture, construction, and operation of structures, systems, and components that prevent or mitigate the consequences of postulated accidents causing undue risk to the public. Its introduction ties each applicant stage to where the quality assurance description lives: the preliminary safety analysis report under 50.34, the final safety analysis report, and the part 52 equivalents. This chapter is a pointer. The eighteen criteria (Organization through Audits) belong to the QA pack, not to this pack.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Pointer discipline. This pack names the appendix, its scope sentence, and its hooks into 50.34 and 50.54; it carries no criterion bodies.
- Quality assurance definition. The appendix uses quality assurance to mean all planned and systematic actions necessary to provide adequate confidence that a structure, system, or component will perform satisfactorily in service, including quality control.

## Key Concepts

- The appendix applies to activities affecting the safety-related functions of the covered structures, systems, and components: designing, purchasing, fabricating, handling, shipping, storing, cleaning, erecting, installing, inspecting, testing, operating, maintaining, repairing, refueling, and modifying.
- Criteria I through XVIII run from Organization, Quality Assurance Program, and Design Control through Procurement Document Control, Document Control, Inspection, Test Control, Control of Measuring and Test Equipment, Nonconforming Items, Corrective Action, Quality Assurance Records, and Audits. They are named here as an orientation list only.
- Section 50.34 requires the quality assurance program description in both report stages; section 50.54 paragraph (a) makes implementing that program a standing license condition and splits program changes into no-reduction and reduction paths.

## Mental Models

Hold the Appendix B surface as three facts: what it covers (safety-related activities), where its description enters the licensing basis (the safety analysis report, per 50.34), and who implements it (the licensee, per 50.54(a)). Everything deeper is a question for the QA pack.

## Anti-patterns

Do not quote or paraphrase criteria I through XVIII from this pack: the pointer owns the hook, the QA pack owns the body. Do not treat this chapter as a quality procedure; Appendix B is regulation text, and implementing programs are licensee documents.

## Key Takeaways

- Appendix B sets quality assurance requirements for accident-prevention and mitigation structures, systems, and components.
- Its description enters the licensing basis through the 50.34 safety analysis report items, and 50.54(a) makes it a standing condition.
- Criterion bodies are out of this pack by design.
