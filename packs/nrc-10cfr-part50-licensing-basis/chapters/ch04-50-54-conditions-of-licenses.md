# Chapter 4: 50.54 conditions of licenses

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 (eCFR), section 50.54; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

Section 50.54 writes standing conditions into every license. Its opening paragraph makes most of the section's paragraphs conditions in every nuclear power reactor operating license (all except paragraphs (r) and (gg)), together with the applicable requirements of 50.55a, with a parallel statement for combined licenses in part 52. A licensee does not read these conditions out of the license text: the regulation itself imposes them. This chapter surveys the condition families.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Conditions-by-regulation. The listed paragraphs are conditions in every covered license whether or not the license document repeats them; the license inherits the regulation.
- Standing versus situational duties. Some conditions are continuous (implement the quality assurance program, keep licensed operators at the controls); some are situational (bankruptcy notification, emergency departures).

## Key Concepts

- Paragraph (a): licensees subject to the Appendix B criteria implement the quality assurance program described or referenced in the safety analysis report. Changes that do not reduce commitments may be made without prior approval; changes that do reduce commitments need prior NRC approval.
- Paragraphs (b) through (e): no right to special nuclear material beyond the license; no transfer of the license or control without Commission consent; subjection to suspension and recapture in wartime; subjection to revocation, suspension, modification, or amendment for cause.
- Paragraph (f): licensees submit sworn written statements on Commission request so the Commission can determine whether the license should be modified, suspended, or revoked.
- Paragraph (h): the license is subject to the Act as in effect from time to time and to all rules, regulations, and orders of the Commission.
- Paragraphs (i) through (m): controls are manipulated only by licensed operators or senior operators, with senior operators designated to direct licensed operators and staffing requirements for on-site coverage.
- Paragraph (n): the licensee may not alter the facility as a departure from the technical specifications in the license except as authorized pursuant to a construction permit or other authorization route.
- Paragraphs (q) and (t): the licensee follows and maintains the effectiveness of an emergency plan meeting Appendix E (or 50.160 for certain facility types) and the planning standards of 50.47(b) for nuclear power reactors, and reviews the emergency preparedness program with independent reviewers.
- Paragraphs (p) and (v): security-plan effectiveness may not decrease without prior approval, and Safeguards Information stays protected per part 73.
- Paragraph (w): power reactor licensees take reasonable steps to obtain insurance, or equivalent protection, covering stabilization and decontamination obligations after an accident.
- Paragraphs (x) and (y): a licensee may depart from a license condition or technical specification in an emergency when immediately needed to protect the public health and safety and no consistent action is immediately apparent; the action needs licensed senior operator (or certified fuel handler) approval.
- Paragraph (z): immediate notification to the NRC Operations Center of events specified in 50.72.
- Paragraphs (aa) and (bb): license conditions imposed as a matter of law by the Federal Water Pollution Control Act, and spent fuel management program notification after permanent cessation of operation.

## Mental Models

Read 50.54 as the license's fine print that no individual license repeats: identity and continuity conditions (who may hold and transfer the license), conduct conditions (operators, emergency plans, security, insurance), and boundary conditions (what may never change without an amendment). The emergency-departure paragraphs (x) and (y) are the narrow exception lane; everything else routes through amendment or approved change processes.

## Anti-patterns

Do not map this section onto a specific plant's license; 50.54 states the floor, and individual licenses and orders can add more. Do not treat paragraph (cc) as emergency planning: in the current text (cc) is the bankruptcy-notification condition, and the emergency-plan condition lives in paragraph (q).

## Key Takeaways

- Most of 50.54 is self-executing: the paragraphs are conditions in every covered license by regulation.
- The Appendix B quality assurance program, emergency plan, security plans, and licensed-operator staffing are standing conditions.
- Departing from technical specifications needs an amendment path, with only the narrow emergency departure of paragraphs (x) and (y) outside it.
