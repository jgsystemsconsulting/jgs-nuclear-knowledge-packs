# Chapter 1: Part 50 overview and definitions

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 (eCFR), sections 50.1, 50.2, 50.20 to 50.23; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

10 CFR Part 50 holds the domestic licensing rules for production and utilization facilities: two license classes (construction permits and operating licenses, with part 52 variants named in the definitions), the contents of applications, the standards licenses must meet, and the conditions and change rules that ride on every license. Section 50.2 supplies the pack's working vocabulary. This chapter orients the part's shape and the definitions a licensing-basis question reaches for first.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Two-class licensing. Section 50.20 states the two classes; 50.21 to 50.23 assign medical, research, commercial and industrial, and construction-permit categories to them. The class decides which contents and standards paragraphs apply.
- Vocabulary-first reading. Most later sections lean on 50.2 definitions; a misread definition propagates through every downstream requirement.

## Key Concepts

- Part 50 is promulgated under the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 and Title II of the Energy Reorganization Act of 1974; it licenses production and utilization facilities.
- Definitions in 50.2 that recur across the pack: Act, applicant, basic component, construction permit, design bases, exclusion area, license, licensee, low population zone, nuclear reactor, person, production facility, prototype plant, reactor coolant pressure boundary, safe shutdown (non-design basis accident) for station blackout, safety-related structures systems and components, small modular reactor, special nuclear material, utilization facility.
- Design bases means the information identifying the specific functions a structure, system, or component must perform and the parameter values chosen as reference bounds for design.
- Safety-related structures, systems and components are those relied on to remain functional during and following design basis events to assure reactor coolant pressure boundary integrity, safe shutdown capability, or prevention and mitigation of accidents with comparable offsite exposure potential.
- The exclusion area, low population zone, and dose-evaluation vocabulary connect 50.34 site assessment questions to the part 100 site criteria.
- A small modular reactor is defined as a power reactor producing up to 1,000 megawatts thermal per module under section 50.21 or 50.22.

## Mental Models

Read Part 50 as a stack: subpart A supplies scope and vocabulary, subpart B supplies procedure (filing, contents, standards), the license itself carries technical specifications and conditions, and the appendices hang design criteria and quality requirements on named sections. A question is located by naming which layer it touches.

## Anti-patterns

See ch05 for the 50.59-local limits and the chapter index for pack-use rules. Local limit: do not quote a 50.2 definition out of its context in this part; several definitions (basic component, defect, construction) are scoped by their defining sentence to specific sections such as 50.55(e).

## Key Takeaways

- Part 50 licenses production and utilization facilities in two classes.
- Section 50.2 is the vocabulary layer the rest of the pack builds on.
- Definitions are section-scoped: check the scoping sentence before carrying one into another section.
