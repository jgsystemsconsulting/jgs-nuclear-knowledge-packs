# Chapter 3: 50.36 technical specifications

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 (eCFR), section 50.36; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

Section 50.36 requires each operating-license application to carry proposed technical specifications and puts technical specifications into every operating license for facilities of the 50.21 or 50.22 types. The specifications are derived from the safety analysis report analyses, and the Commission may add more as it finds appropriate. The section then sorts specification content into named categories. This chapter maps those categories and the reactor-specific limiting-conditions criteria.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Derived-from rule. Technical specifications come from the analyses and evaluations in the safety analysis report, so the specification set and the report stay one document family.
- Category taxonomy. Content must land in one of the named categories: safety limits, limiting safety system settings and limiting control settings, limiting conditions for operation, surveillance requirements, design features, administrative controls, plus the decommissioning case.

## Key Concepts

- A summary statement of the bases or reasons for the specifications, other than those covering administrative controls, accompanies the application but does not become part of the technical specifications.
- Safety limits for nuclear reactors are limits on important process variables needed to reasonably protect the integrity of physical barriers against uncontrolled release; if exceeded, the reactor must be shut down, the Commission notified, the matter reviewed and recorded, and operation resumed only after Commission authorization.
- Limiting safety system settings are settings for automatic protective devices on variables with significant safety functions, chosen so automatic protective action corrects the abnormal situation before a safety limit is exceeded.
- Limiting conditions for operation are the lowest functional capability or performance levels of equipment required for safe operation; when one is not met the licensee shuts down or follows a permitted remedial action until the condition is met.
- A reactor limiting condition for operation must be established for each item meeting at least one of four criteria: installed control-room instrumentation detecting significant abnormal degradation of the reactor coolant pressure boundary; a process variable, design feature, or operating restriction that is an initial condition of a design basis accident or transient analysis challenging a fission product barrier; a structure, system, or component in the primary success path that mitigates such an accident or transient; or a structure, system, or component that operating experience or probabilistic risk assessment shows significant to public health and safety.
- Surveillance requirements relate to test, calibration, or inspection to assure the necessary quality of systems and components, operation within safety limits, and that limiting conditions are met.
- Design features are features such as materials of construction and geometric arrangements whose alteration would significantly affect safety.
- Administrative controls are the organization, management, procedures, recordkeeping, review and audit, and reporting provisions needed to assure safe operation.
- Reporting: initial notification follows 50.72 or the regional administrator route, and nuclear power reactor licensees report events in the safety-limit and limiting-condition categories per 50.73.

## Mental Models

Read the categories as a defense-in-depth ladder: safety limits guard physical barriers, settings trip before limits are breached, limiting conditions keep equipment capable, surveillance proves it stays capable, design features pin the safety-significant configuration, and administrative controls hold the whole together. A specification question is placed by naming its category first.

## Anti-patterns

Do not treat the bases summary as part of the technical specifications; the section says it does not become part of them. Do not apply the four limiting-condition criteria as if every equipment item needed a limiting condition: the criteria gate which items do.

## Key Takeaways

- Technical specifications ride in every operating license for 50.21 and 50.22 facilities and are derived from the safety analysis report.
- Five content categories plus the decommissioning case cover what specifications may contain.
- The four limiting-condition criteria decide which reactor items get limiting conditions for operation.
