# Chapter 2: 50.34 contents of applications

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 (eCFR), section 50.34; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

Section 50.34 fixes the technical information an application must carry: a preliminary safety analysis report with a construction permit application, a final safety analysis report with an operating license application, plus security and safeguards plans where they apply. The safety analysis report is the document the licensing basis grows from; 50.36 derives technical specifications from its analyses. This chapter maps the report's content categories.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Two-stage reporting. The preliminary report supports the construction permit with preliminary design, analysis, and program descriptions; the final report for the operating license carries as-built descriptions, design bases with technical justification, final analysis, and the proposed technical specifications.
- Reference-by-similarity. A footnote provision lets an applicant supply required information as a discussion, with specific references, of similarities to and differences from facilities of similar design.

## Key Concepts

- Preliminary report content categories include: site description and safety assessment, facility summary, preliminary design (principal design criteria, design bases and their relation to the criteria, materials and arrangement), preliminary analysis and evaluation of structures systems and components, identification of probable technical specification subjects, organization and operations plans, quality assurance program description tied to Appendix B, research and development identification, technical qualifications, emergency plans, and multiunit and seismic and aircraft-impact items where their trigger dates apply.
- Final report content categories include: current site information, description and analysis of structures systems and components with performance requirements and bases, radioactive material kinds and quantities with effluent and exposure controls within part 20 limits, final analysis and evaluation, resolution of construction-stage safety questions, organizational structure and managerial and administrative controls, preoperational testing and normal operations plans, emergency plans, proposed technical specifications prepared per 50.36, operator requalification program description, pressurized thermal shock protection description, and the site safety assessment carried forward.
- Applications subject to the part 73 sections also include a physical security plan, a training and qualification plan, a cyber security plan per 73.54 criteria, and a safeguards contingency plan; safeguards-related plans carry protection-against-unauthorized-disclosure duties.
- The quality assurance program description must discuss how the applicable Appendix B requirements will be satisfied.

## Mental Models

The safety analysis report is the master document: design descriptions, design bases, safety analyses, programs, and the proposed technical specifications all live inside it or hang from it, and later chapters of this pack reach back into it. The report contents are a checklist of categories, not a plant design: this chapter states what must be described, never what a specific facility's values are.

## Anti-patterns

Treat this chapter as a category map, not a report template: paragraph triggers (license class, dates, facility type) decide which items apply. Do not invent content requirements for documents outside 50.34's list.

## Key Takeaways

- 50.34 pairs each license class with a safety analysis report: preliminary for construction, final for operation.
- Proposed technical specifications are a named item of the final report, prepared per 50.36.
- Appendix B quality assurance descriptions are required in both report stages.
