# Glossary: nrc-10cfr-part50-licensing-basis

Terms this pack uses. Definitions are synthesized orientation notes keyed to the pack's eCFR slices, not substitute section text. Alphabetical.

- **design bases**: the information identifying the specific functions a structure, system, or component must perform and the parameter values chosen as reference bounds for design. A 50.2 definition this pack leans on. (ch01, ch06)
- **eCFR pin-check**: the build-time check of the electronic Code of Federal Regulations. This pack pins title 10 current as of 2026-11-05, checked 2026-11-08. (all chapters, cheatsheet)
- **FSAR (as updated)**: the final safety analysis report submitted under 50.34 as amended and supplemented and updated per 50.71(e) or (f). The 50.59 screening baseline. (ch02, ch05)
- **limiting condition for operation**: the lowest functional capability or performance levels of equipment required for safe operation. One of the 50.36 specification categories. (ch03)
- **limiting safety system setting**: a setting for an automatic protective device on a variable with a significant safety function, chosen so protective action corrects the abnormal situation before a safety limit is exceeded. (ch03)
- **licensing basis**: shorthand for the set of requirements a facility is held to: the license, its technical specifications and conditions, and the safety analysis report as updated. This pack maps the Part 50 pieces; it is never itself a plant's licensing basis. (all chapters, cheatsheet)
- **low population zone**: the area immediately surrounding the exclusion area whose residents could plausibly be protected in a serious accident. A 50.2 definition tied to the site assessment vocabulary. (ch01)
- **principal design criteria**: the design criteria an application must include under 50.34; Appendix A supplies minimum requirements for water-cooled plants. (ch02, ch06)
- **reactor coolant pressure boundary**: the pressure-containing components of boiling and pressurized water-cooled reactors connected to the reactor coolant system, bounded per the 50.2 definition. (ch01, ch03, ch06)
- **safety limit**: a limit on an important process variable necessary to reasonably protect the integrity of physical barriers guarding against uncontrolled release. (ch03)
- **safety-related structures, systems and components**: those relied on to remain functional during and following design basis events to assure boundary integrity, safe shutdown, or accident prevention and mitigation per the 50.2 definition. (ch01, ch07)
- **surveillance requirements**: requirements for test, calibration, or inspection to assure quality of systems and components, operation within safety limits, and that limiting conditions are met. (ch03)
- **technical specifications**: the specification set 50.36 puts into operating licenses, sorted into the named categories. (ch03, ch04, ch05)
