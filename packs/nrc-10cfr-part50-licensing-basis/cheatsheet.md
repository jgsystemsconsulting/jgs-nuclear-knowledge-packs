# Cheatsheet: nrc-10cfr-part50-licensing-basis

Decision rules for which chapter owns a question, the pin rule, the not-advice rule, and the licensing-basis limits.

## Which chapter owns the question

- What Part 50 is, its license classes, or a 50.2 definition: ch01.
- What an application must contain, PSAR or FSAR content: ch02.
- Technical specifications, safety limits, limiting conditions for operation, surveillance requirements: ch03.
- License conditions, quality program implementation, operator staffing, emergency plan conditions: ch04.
- Changes, tests, or experiments without an amendment; the eight screening criteria; departures from a method of evaluation: ch05.
- General Design Criteria groups or a criterion number: ch06.
- Appendix B scope or its hook into 50.34 and 50.54: ch07.

## Pin rule

eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-05, pin-check 2026-11-08. Later amendments are out of scope. The plant licensing basis, not this pack, decides which amendment text binds a given facility.

## Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized; verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences. This pack is not the regulation, not legal advice, and not counsel. Verify any requirement against the current eCFR text before relying on it.

## Licensing-basis limits

- These notes are not a plant-specific licensing basis: a facility's licensing basis is its license, technical specifications, conditions, and updated FSAR, not this pack.
- Selected slices only; sections of Part 50 outside the slices are out of scope.
- Appendix B is a pointer only; its criteria bodies belong to the QA pack.
- Appendix A is an index only; criterion bodies are not here.
- Regulation text only: no NRC staff positions, no staff guidance content (that layer is `nrc-nuclear`).

## When the question is thin

There are no thin-pack handoffs inside the slices: each slice is owned fully. Questions outside the slices (for example 50.44, 50.46, 50.55a, 50.71, appendix G, J, K, or R) are out of scope: say so, and route staff-guidance framing to `nrc-nuclear`.
