## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Three fire defense-in-depth goals in balance

A fire protection program works three goals at once: prevent fires from starting; rapidly detect, control, and extinguish fires that do occur; and protect structures, systems, and components important to safety so a fire does not prevent safe shutdown. Those three goals are this pack's definition of fire defense-in-depth. They are evaluated in balance; no single leg carries the whole safety case.

### Guidance, not regulation

RG 1.189 Rev 5 gives staff guidance on one acceptable way to build a fire protection program that satisfies 10 CFR 50.48 and General Design Criterion 3 in 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix A. A Regulatory Guide describes one method the NRC staff finds acceptable; it is guidance, not regulation, and its text binds no licensee through this pack.

### Pin rule

This pack pins RG 1.189 Rev 5, October 2023, ML23214A287, verified via Federal Register notice 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13. Live NRC listing page checked the same date returned access denied (absent listing); the FR pin stands. Guide PDF body was not captured. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope.

### NFPA named-only rule

NFPA 805 (light water reactor) and NFPA 804 (advanced light water reactor) are copyrighted paywalled standards. They appear in this pack only as named signpost designations. Designation rows live in the live nuclear signpost pack, named in prose only. No NFPA text, figure, table, or method is reproduced or summarized here.

### Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin and closed statement set. This pack is not the guide, not 10 CFR 50.48, not Appendix R, not NFPA 805 or NFPA 804, not a regulation, and not counsel. This pack quotes nothing; RG and CFR verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences by licence policy and this pack uses none.

## Scope & Limits

Fire protection program orientation per RG 1.189 Rev 5 pin facts. This pack is not the guide, not 10 CFR 50.48, not Appendix R, and not NFPA 805 or NFPA 804.

- Program structure at framework depth only. Fire areas, barriers, safe shutdown functions, and associated circuits are named and defined; no rating, distance, loading, suppression value, or staffing value is packaged here.
- NFPA 805 and NFPA 804 are named only as signpost designations owned by the live nuclear signpost pack named in prose. Their text, figures, tables, and methods are copyrighted paywalled material and are never reproduced or summarized at text depth here.
- The 10 CFR 50.48(c) performance-based fire protection option is named at framework depth only. No fire PRA method, fire modelling, performance criteria, or transition step is packaged; PRA technical adequacy belongs to the live RG 1.200 pack named in prose.
- Not a fire hazards analysis, not a fire safe shutdown analysis, not circuit analysis for any real plant; not a design, compliance, or Appendix R verdict; not post-fire procedure drafting; and no operator manual action is credited.
- Zero quotations. Synthesized notes keyed to the recorded pin (88 FR 76989, ML23214A287); RG and CFR verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences by licence policy and this pack uses none. No source-material URLs in pack files.
- Not legal, licensing, design, or fire safety advice. Advice requests are refused.
- Three question classes are thin in this pack and stop at the pack boundary: (1) guide or NFPA body text, section numbers, figures, tables, or any numeric design value; (2) plant-specific analysis (FHA for a named area, safe shutdown or circuit verdicts, fire modelling or fire PRA computation, manual action feasibility); (3) neighbour-owned material (Part 50 criterion text, PRA adequacy verdicts, NFPA 805 transition and licensing mechanics, digital I&C design questions).
- Honest fallback: when the question needs guide body text, plant-specific analysis, or a neighbour-owned method, stop and name the guide itself (NRC RG 1.189 Rev 5) or the owning neighbour as the text source; this pack does not invent the missing content.
- No slug links to any sibling pack. Neighbours are named in prose only, per repo discipline.
- Sector anti-scope still applies: weapons and weapons-design physics, classified or export-controlled or safeguards-sensitive material, 10 CFR Part 35 and other non-power-plant isotope programmes, paywalled standard text.
