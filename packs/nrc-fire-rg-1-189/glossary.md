# Glossary: nrc-fire-rg-1-189

Definitions are synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin (RG 1.189 Rev 5, October 2023, ML23214A287; verified via 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13), not substitute guide or regulation text. Alphabetical.

- **alternative shutdown**: shutdown capability that achieves the safe shutdown functions from locations and with equipment independent of the fire area where redundant train separation cannot be assured; concept only, no plant path assessed here. (ch06)
- **associated circuit**: a circuit that is not itself a safe shutdown circuit but whose fire damage can affect safe shutdown equipment through shared power, control, or spurious operation paths; concept only. (ch05)
- **combustible loading**: the combustible material present in a space, treated as an analysis input concept; no loading value is stated here. (ch02)
- **dedicated shutdown**: a form of independent shutdown capability reserved for the fire-area fallback case when redundant train separation cannot be assured; named as a concept only. (ch06)
- **defense-in-depth**: in the fire sense used by this pack, the three program goals evaluated in balance: prevent fires from starting; rapidly detect, control, and extinguish fires that do occur; and protect so a fire cannot prevent safe shutdown. Distinct from cyber and non-LWR senses carried by live sibling packs. (ch02)
- **fire area**: a plant space treated as a unit for fire protection analysis; named as a concept only, with no boundary drawn here. (ch02)
- **fire barrier**: a feature that limits fire spread between spaces or protects equipment; named as a concept only, with no rating stated here. (ch02)
- **fire brigade**: on-site manual firefighting capability named as a program element; no staffing or response value is stated here. (ch04)
- **fire detection**: systems and features that alarm on fire and may actuate suppression; role and actuation roles named only, with no detector selection stated here. (ch04)
- **fire hazards analysis**: plant-wide, area-by-area identification of fire hazards present, fire protection features provided, and the effect of postulated fires on the ability to achieve and maintain safe shutdown; concept only in this pack, and no analysis is performed here. (ch02)
- **fire protection program**: the program that works the three fire defense-in-depth goals under the RG 1.189 orientation: prevent; detect, control, and extinguish; protect structures, systems, and components so fire does not prevent safe shutdown. (ch01)
- **fire suppression**: automatic and manual means to control or extinguish fire, including water-based and gaseous families; what each family is for is named only, with no design value stated here. (ch04)
- **hot work**: ignition-source activity such as welding or cutting that the prevention program controls; concept only, with no permit condition stated here. (ch03)
- **operator manual action**: an action an operator performs by hand as part of post-fire shutdown; named as a concept only. Whether one may be credited is a regulatory question this pack does not answer. (ch06)
- **performance-based fire protection**: the voluntary alternative under 10 CFR 50.48(c) built on NFPA 805 for light water reactors, named at framework depth only; no method, transition step, or NFPA text is packaged here. (ch01)
- **safe shutdown**: the plant state in which reactivity is controlled, coolant inventory and pressure are controlled, decay heat is removed, vital auxiliaries support those functions, and process monitoring is available after a fire; concept set only. (ch05)
- **safe shutdown equipment**: equipment relied on to achieve and maintain safe shutdown after a fire; named as a concept only, with no equipment list or train verdict here. (ch05)
- **spurious operation**: unwanted start, stop, or misposition of equipment caused by fire damage to circuits; concept only, with no worked case stated. (ch05)
- **transient combustibles**: combustible materials brought into an area for a limited time and controlled as part of the prevention program; concept only, with no quantity limit stated here. (ch03)
