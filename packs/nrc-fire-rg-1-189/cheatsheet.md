Decision rules for which chapter owns which question, pin rule, named-only rules, framework depth limits, thin classes, honest fallback, and not-advice limits.

## Pin rule

Pack pins RG 1.189 Rev 5, October 2023, ML23214A287, verified via Federal Register notice 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13. Guide body not captured. Later revisions out of scope.

## Named-only rules

- NFPA 805 and NFPA 804 designations live in the live nuclear signpost pack named in prose; this pack names the standards and states the NRC guidance position only. No NFPA text, figure, table, or method is reproduced or summarized here.
- Part 50 criterion text is owned by the live Part 50 pack named in prose only.
- PRA technical adequacy is owned by the live RG 1.200 pack named in prose only.
- The risk-informed decision framework for licensing-basis changes is owned by the live RG 1.174 pack named in prose only.

## Framework depth limits

Concepts are defined at orientation depth. No rating, separation distance, combustible loading value, detection or suppression design value, water capacity, suppression duration, brigade staffing number, operator action time, procedure, or plant verdict is packaged here. Guide PDF body was not captured; no section or regulatory position number is stated as read.

## Thin classes stop at the boundary

1. Guide or NFPA body text: position wording, NFPA text, section numbers, or any numeric design value.
2. Plant-specific analysis: fire hazards analysis for a named area, safe shutdown or circuit verdicts, fire modelling or fire PRA computation, or manual action feasibility.
3. Neighbour-owned material: Part 50 criterion text, PRA adequacy verdicts, NFPA 805 transition and licensing mechanics, or digital I&C design questions.

## Honest fallback

This knowledge pack provides high-level orientation on fire protection program guidelines under RG 1.189; it does not reproduce NFPA standards, design plant systems, or evaluate plant-specific compliance. For standard text, consult the referenced NFPA standards; for plant-specific fire protection licensing, consult licensed engineering personnel.

## Not advice

Synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin and closed statement list; zero quotations. This pack is not the guide, not 10 CFR 50.48, not Appendix R, not NFPA 805 or NFPA 804, not regulation, and not legal, licensing, design, or fire safety advice. Advice requests refused.
