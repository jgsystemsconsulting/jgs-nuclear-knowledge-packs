# Chapter 1: RG 1.189 Scope, Regulatory Basis, and Program Goals

Sources: NRC RG 1.189 Rev 5, Fire Protection for Nuclear Power Plants, October 2023, ML23214A287; verified via Federal Register notice 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13. 10 CFR 50.48, 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix A GDC 3, and 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix R named at orientation depth only; selected Part 50 slices are owned by the live Part 50 pack. NFPA 805 and NFPA 804 named only as signpost designations, not sources.

## Core Idea

RG 1.189 Rev 5 (October 2023, ML23214A287; 88 FR 76989, November 8, 2023, notice 2023-24621) gives staff guidance on fire protection for nuclear power plants: one acceptable way to satisfy 10 CFR 50.48 and General Design Criterion 3 in 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix A. A Regulatory Guide describes one method the NRC staff finds acceptable; it is guidance, not regulation, and its text binds no licensee through this pack. A fire protection program works three goals at once: prevent fires from starting; rapidly detect, control, and extinguish fires that do occur; and provide protection for structures, systems, and components important to safety so a fire does not prevent safe shutdown.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Guidance, not regulation. RG 1.189 Rev 5 orients how staff expect a fire protection program to hang together under 10 CFR 50.48 and GDC 3. The guide is the staff-position source; this pack is synthesized orientation keyed to the recorded pin, not the guide and not a substitute for it.
- Three program goals. Prevent; detect, control, and extinguish; protect structures, systems, and components so fire does not prevent safe shutdown. Those three goals are this pack's definition of fire defense-in-depth and are evaluated in balance, not as three independent checklists (ch02).
- Deterministic and performance-based lanes named only. 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix R is the deterministic fire protection rule whose post-fire safe shutdown requirements apply to plants licensed under it. 10 CFR 50.48(c) provides a voluntary performance-based, risk-informed alternative built on NFPA 805 for light water reactors. Both are named at framework depth only: no rule text, no NFPA text, and no compliance path is packaged here.
- Neighbour ownership (prose only). The live Part 50 pack carries selected 10 CFR Part 50 slices, with Appendix A as index; this pack never restates criterion text. NFPA 805 and NFPA 804 designations live in the nuclear signpost; this pack names the standards and states the NRC guidance position only.

## Key Concepts

- Pin facts: RG 1.189 Rev 5, October 2023, ADAMS ML23214A287, verified via Federal Register notice 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13. Guide PDF body not captured. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope.
- 10 CFR 50.48: the fire protection regulation this guide orients against, named only.

## Mental Models

Read RG 1.189 as staff guidance on one acceptable fire protection program shape, not as regulation that supplies the program. Keep the three goals as one balanced set; later chapters open one leg or one analysis slice at a time without dropping the other two. Keep Appendix R and the 10 CFR 50.48(c) performance-based option as named lanes only: this pack points at them and does not walk either compliance path. Place Part 50 criterion text with the live Part 50 pack, and place NFPA designations with the live nuclear signpost; this chapter only names those interfaces.

## Anti-patterns

Treating these orientation notes as a substitute for RG 1.189 is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the notes locate scope, pin, regulatory basis names, and program goals; the guide carries staff-position detail. A companion error is treating the Regulatory Guide as regulation that binds a licensee through this pack. A third error is restating 10 CFR 50.48, Appendix R, or GDC 3 criterion text from these notes, or pulling a compliance path for either the deterministic or performance-based lane from this pack.
