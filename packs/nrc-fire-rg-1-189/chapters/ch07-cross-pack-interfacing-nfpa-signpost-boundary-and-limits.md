# Chapter 7: Cross-Pack Interfacing, NFPA Signpost Boundary, and Limits

Sources: NRC RG 1.189 Rev 5, Fire Protection for Nuclear Power Plants, October 2023, ML23214A287; verified via Federal Register notice 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13. NFPA 805 and NFPA 804 named only, not sources.

## Core Idea

This pack orients one slice: fire protection program structure under RG 1.189 Rev 5 at framework depth. Everything past that slice belongs to neighbours this chapter names in prose only, never by link. NFPA 805 and NFPA 804 designations live in the nuclear signpost; this pack names the standards and states the NRC guidance position only. The live RG 1.200 pack carries PRA technical adequacy orientation; this pack states only that a performance-based fire protection basis presumes adequate fire risk capability and does not restate adequacy. The live RG 1.174 pack carries the risk-informed decision framework for licensing-basis changes. The live Part 50 pack carries selected 10 CFR Part 50 slices, with Appendix A as an index. Fire PRA method documents, fire modelling tools, and NFPA 805 transition mechanics are named only if at all, never summarized.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Signpost boundary. NFPA 805 (light water reactor) and NFPA 804 (advanced light water reactor) are copyrighted paywalled standards. They appear in this repo only as designation rows in the live nuclear signpost. The RG carries the NRC position; NFPA text, figures, tables, fire PRA method, and fire modelling detail are never reproduced or summarized here.
- PRA neighbour boundary. PRA technical adequacy for any fire PRA under a performance-based licensing basis lives with the live RG 1.200 pack. This pack never restates adequacy criteria or method.
- Risk-informed decision boundary. The framework for risk-informed licensing-basis changes lives with the live RG 1.174 pack. This pack never restates that decision process.
- Part 50 neighbour boundary. Selected Part 50 slices, including Appendix A as an index and detailed GDC 3 criterion text, live with the live Part 50 pack. This pack names 10 CFR 50.48, Appendix R, and GDC 3 at orientation depth only and never restates criterion text.
- Limits and stop lines. The full not-covered list and the honest fallback sentence sit in this chapter so orientation material carries its own stop lines.

## Mental Models

Read this chapter as a boundary map, not a second body of content. Inside the line sits RG 1.189 fire protection program orientation at framework depth: goals, hazards analysis idea, prevention, detection and suppression, safe shutdown concepts, and alternative shutdown as a fallback concept. Outside the line sit NFPA standard text, PRA adequacy method, the risk-informed decision framework, Part 50 criterion text, fire modelling, fire PRA computation, and any plant-specific analysis or verdict. When a question points outside the line, the honest move is to name the guide or the owning neighbour and stop, not to stretch this pack to cover it.

## Anti-patterns

Restating neighbour text inside this pack is the named anti-pattern: no NFPA text, no PRA adequacy criteria, no RG 1.174 decision process, and no Part 50 criterion text ships here. A companion error treats named-only hooks as coverage, as if naming NFPA 805, NFPA 804, RG 1.200, RG 1.174, or Part 50 answered the standard, adequacy, decision, or criterion question. A third error asks this pack for a plant fire hazards analysis, a safe shutdown or circuit verdict, an NFPA 805 transition path, or fire safety advice for a real plant. A fourth error invents missing guide body text, section numbers, or numeric design values because the guide PDF body was not captured.
