# Chapter 3: Fire Prevention, Combustible and Ignition Controls

Sources: NRC RG 1.189 Rev 5, Fire Protection for Nuclear Power Plants, October 2023, ML23214A287; verified via Federal Register notice 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13.

## Core Idea

Fire prevention is administrative control: limiting and arranging combustible materials (including control of transient combustibles), controlling ignition sources such as hot work, and maintaining housekeeping and inspection discipline. This chapter is the prevention leg of the three fire defense-in-depth goals from ch02. The pack states the concept set only; no numeric combustible limit, separation value, or permit condition is stated, and no plant procedure is drafted or judged.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Prevention as administrative control. The first program goal works by controlling what can burn and what can start a fire before either becomes a challenge to later legs.
- Combustible control as a concept set. Quantity and arrangement limits, plus control of transient combustibles, are named as control ideas; no limit number is packaged.
- Ignition source control families. Hot work, portable equipment, and smoking are named as control families; no permit condition is packaged.
- Housekeeping and inspection as program elements. Routine discipline keeps combustible and ignition controls current; the chapter names the elements and does not write a procedure.
- Propagation into later legs. A prevention failure grows the fire that detection, suppression, and safe shutdown margin must then face together.

## Key Concepts

- Fire prevention: administrative control that limits fire starts by managing combustibles and ignition sources and by keeping housekeeping and inspection discipline.
- Combustible materials control: limiting and arranging materials that can burn inside plant spaces treated as safety-related for fire protection analysis; concept only, with no quantity or arrangement value stated.
- Transient combustibles: combustible materials brought into a space for work or storage that are not permanent plant loading; named as a control target, with no limit value stated.
- Ignition source control: measures that limit how fires start; hot work, portable equipment, and smoking are named control families only.
- Hot work: work that can introduce an ignition source (for example cutting or welding as family examples); named as a control family, with no permit condition stated.
- Housekeeping: ongoing control of waste, storage, and material condition so combustible and ignition controls stay effective; program element concept only.
- Inspection: checks that confirm combustible and ignition controls remain in place; program element concept only, with no inspection interval stated.

## Mental Models

Read prevention as the first leg of the three-legged stool from ch02. Keep fuel low and arranged, keep ignition sources under control, and keep housekeeping and inspection on the beat so the other two legs see fewer and smaller fires. When prevention slips, the fire that starts is larger or more likely; detection must find it, suppression must fight it longer, and safe shutdown margin is the residual that remains. This chapter names the control set; it does not write a plant procedure, set a combustible limit, or judge a permit.

## Anti-patterns

Treating this chapter as a plant combustible control procedure is the named anti-pattern: the notes locate the concept set and stop. A companion error is stating a numeric combustible limit, separation value, or permit condition from these notes. A third error is reading prevention as a complete safety case on its own; prevention is one leg in balance with detect-and-suppress and safe shutdown protection. A fourth error is drafting or judging housekeeping, inspection, or hot work procedures from orientation notes.

## Key Takeaways

- Fire prevention is administrative control of combustibles, ignition sources, housekeeping, and inspection.
- Combustible control covers quantity and arrangement limits as concepts, including transient combustibles; no limit value is stated here.
- Ignition source control names hot work, portable equipment, and smoking as control families; no permit condition is stated here.
- Prevention failures propagate into detection, suppression, and safe shutdown margin together.
- This pack states the concept set only; no plant procedure is drafted or judged.
