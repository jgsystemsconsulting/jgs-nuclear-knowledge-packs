# Chapter 4: Fire Detection and Suppression Systems

Sources: NRC RG 1.189 Rev 5, Fire Protection for Nuclear Power Plants, October 2023, ML23214A287; verified via Federal Register notice 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13.

## Core Idea

Fire detection and suppression cover alarm and actuation detection, automatic and manual suppression in water-based and gaseous families, and manual firefighting by an on-site fire brigade as a named capability. This chapter is the detect-and-suppress leg of the three fire defense-in-depth goals from ch02. The pack states what each element is for; no detector selection, suppression design value, water capacity, or brigade staffing value is stated. Suppression limits fire growth and duration; it does not by itself prove safe shutdown capability.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Detection role. Detection serves alarm and actuation: it tells people and systems a fire is present and can start automatic response.
- Automatic and manual suppression. Systems may act without an operator and may be started by people; both modes sit in the detect-and-suppress leg.
- Suppression system families. Water-based and gaseous suppression are named families; the chapter states what each family is for and packages no design value.
- Manual firefighting capability. An on-site fire brigade is named as the manual firefighting capability; staffing and response values are not packaged.
- Interface to safe shutdown chapters. Suppression limits fire growth and duration but does not by itself prove safe shutdown capability; that question belongs to ch05 and ch06.

## Key Concepts

- Fire detection: means that sense a fire and provide alarm and actuation signals; concept only, with no detector type or spacing stated.
- Alarm role: notifying plant staff that a fire condition exists so response can begin.
- Actuation role: starting automatic suppression or related protective actions when detection logic calls for it.
- Automatic suppression: suppression that starts without manual initiation once detection or system logic calls for it; concept only.
- Manual suppression: suppression started or applied by people, including hose stations and extinguishers as family examples at concept depth only.
- Water-based suppression: suppression family that uses water as the agent; named family only, with no flow, density, or supply capacity stated.
- Gaseous suppression: suppression family that uses a gas agent; named family only, with no agent concentration or hold time stated.
- Fire brigade: on-site manual firefighting capability named as a program element; no staffing number or response time is stated.

## Mental Models

Read detection as the eyes and the start signal, and suppression as the means that slow or stop growth once a fire is present. Automatic systems buy time; the brigade extends reach where systems do not finish the job. Hold one interface sentence fixed: even a well-working suppress-and-fight response only limits how big and how long the fire gets; whether the plant can still shut down safely is a separate question answered by safe shutdown capability and circuit analysis in later chapters. This chapter names the families and roles; it does not select detectors, size systems, or staff a brigade.

## Anti-patterns

Treating detection or suppression design detail as packaged here is the named anti-pattern: no detector selection, sprinkler density, agent concentration, water supply capacity, suppression duration, or brigade staffing or response value appears in these notes. A companion error is reading a successful suppress-and-fight picture as proof of safe shutdown capability; suppression limits growth and duration and does not close the safe shutdown question. A third error is collapsing the detect-and-suppress leg into the whole safety case; the three goals stay in balance with prevention and safe shutdown protection. A fourth error is drafting brigade procedures or system design criteria from orientation notes.

## Key Takeaways

- Detection covers alarm and actuation roles; suppression covers automatic and manual modes in water-based and gaseous families.
- An on-site fire brigade is the named manual firefighting capability; no staffing or response value is stated here.
- This pack states what each element is for; no detector selection, suppression design value, water capacity, or brigade staffing value is stated.
- Suppression limits fire growth and duration; it does not by itself prove safe shutdown capability.
- Detect-and-suppress is one leg in balance with prevention and safe shutdown protection.
