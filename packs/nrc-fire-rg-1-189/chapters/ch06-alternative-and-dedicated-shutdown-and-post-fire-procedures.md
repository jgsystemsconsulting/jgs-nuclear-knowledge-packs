# Chapter 6: Alternative and Dedicated Shutdown and Post-Fire Procedures

Sources: NRC RG 1.189 Rev 5, Fire Protection for Nuclear Power Plants, October 2023, ML23214A287; verified via Federal Register notice 88 FR 76989 on 2026-11-13.

## Core Idea

Alternative or dedicated shutdown capability is the fallback when redundant train separation inside one fire area cannot be assured. The control room and the cable spreading room are the commonly named cases at orientation depth. The fallback means the safe shutdown functions from ch05 are performed from locations and with equipment independent of the fire area. Post-fire operating procedures are the human half of that fallback. An operator manual action is named here as a concept; whether one may be credited is a regulatory question this pack does not answer. No procedure is written here, no manual action is judged feasible, no timeline is stated, and no plant fallback is assessed.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Separation-cannot-be-assured trigger. When redundant trains share a fire area and one train free of fire damage cannot be assured, the program looks to an independent shutdown path rather than to more of the same separation idea.
- Alternative and dedicated shutdown as independent-path concepts. The ch05 function set (reactivity control, coolant inventory and pressure control, decay heat removal, vital auxiliaries, process monitoring) is still the target; the difference is where and with what equipment the functions are performed.
- Post-fire procedures as the human half. Written post-fire operating procedures carry the operator side of the fallback; this pack names the idea and drafts no procedure.
- Operator manual action named only. The concept is named with its regulatory sensitivity stated; credit, timing, and feasibility stay outside the pack.

## Key Concepts

- Alternative shutdown: shutdown capability that achieves the safe shutdown functions from locations and with equipment independent of the fire area where redundant train separation cannot be assured; concept only.
- Dedicated shutdown: a form of independent shutdown capability reserved for the fire-area fallback case; named as a concept only, with no system design stated here.
- Control room and cable spreading room: the commonly named fire areas at orientation depth where redundant train separation often cannot be assured; named cases only, not a plant list or verdict.
- Independence from the fire area: the fallback path uses locations and equipment outside the fire area under study so fire damage inside that area does not defeat the shutdown functions.
- Post-fire operating procedures: procedures that guide operators through post-fire safe shutdown or alternative shutdown actions; named as a program element, never drafted here.
- Operator manual action: a named concept for an action an operator performs by hand as part of post-fire shutdown; whether one may be credited is a regulatory question this pack does not answer.

## Mental Models

Read ch05 as the normal path: keep one redundant train free of fire damage by separation or barriers, and analyse circuits so fire damage does not spuriously defeat that train. Read this chapter as the path taken when that normal path cannot be assured inside one fire area. Picture the control room or cable spreading room as a space where both trains' cables or controls can sit together; the independent path then does the same function set from somewhere else, with gear the fire in that space cannot touch. The written procedure is the human half of that picture. Naming an operator manual action is not the same as crediting one: credit is a regulatory question answered outside these notes.

## Anti-patterns

Crediting, timing, or judging feasible any operator manual action from these notes is the named anti-pattern. A companion error is drafting a post-fire procedure or assessing a plant fallback path here. A third error is treating the control room and cable spreading room mentions as a complete plant list or as a verdict that every plant needs alternative shutdown in those spaces. A fourth error is collapsing this chapter back into ch05 redundant-train protection; this chapter is the independent-path fallback when that protection cannot be assured. A fifth error is stating a timeline, staffing number, or action sequence as if the pack carried procedure content.

## Key Takeaways

- Alternative or dedicated shutdown is the fallback when redundant train separation inside one fire area cannot be assured.
- The control room and cable spreading room are the commonly named cases at orientation depth; they are named cases only.
- The fallback performs the ch05 safe shutdown functions from locations and with equipment independent of the fire area.
- Post-fire operating procedures are the human half of the fallback; this pack drafts none.
- An operator manual action is named here as a concept; whether one may be credited is a regulatory question this pack does not answer.
