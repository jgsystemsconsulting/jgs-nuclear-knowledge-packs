# Glossary: nrc-non-lwr-ticap-rg-1-233

Terms this pack uses. Definitions are synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin (RG 1.233 Rev 0, June 2020, ML20091L698; verified via 85 FR 35339 on 2026-11-13), not substitute guide or regulation text. Alphabetical.

- **anticipated operational occurrence**: licensing basis event family named at definition depth; no frequency bound packaged here. (ch03)
- **beyond design basis event**: licensing basis event family named at definition depth; no frequency or consequence bound packaged here. (ch03)
- **defense-in-depth**: layered capability and programmatic provisions that keep risk-informed selections from standing on a single thin line of protection. (ch05)
- **design basis accident**: conservative subset of events carried into deterministic design-basis analysis. (ch03)
- **design basis event**: licensing basis event family named at definition depth; no frequency bound packaged here. (ch03)
- **licensing basis**: shorthand set of requirements a facility is held to. This pack maps methodology orientation only and never itself states any plant licensing basis. (ch01)
- **licensing basis event**: event the method places into the licensing basis so later classification and defense-in-depth stages work from a defined event set. (ch03)
- **Licensing Modernization Project**: industry methodology work product carried as NEI 18-04 Rev 1 and named here only as the endorsed document. (ch01)
- **performance-based**: focus on required functions and outcomes rather than on fixed equipment prescriptions alone. (ch02)
- **principal design criteria**: design commitments the methodology results feed; development owned by the live RG 1.232 pack and never restated here. (ch06)
- **risk-informed**: risk insights inform licensing-basis and application-content selections; risk numbers alone do not replace engineering judgement or deterministic checks. (ch02)
- **safety-related**: classification family at definition depth; no performance criterion packaged here. (ch04)
- **special treatment**: additional quality and reliability controls that follow from classification. (ch04)
- **technology-inclusive**: method frame not locked to light-water plant features or inherited light-water event lists. (ch02)
- **TICAP**: name and content-of-application purpose at orientation depth only; no TICAP text packaged here. (ch06)
