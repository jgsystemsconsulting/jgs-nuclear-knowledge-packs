## Pin rule

Pack pins RG 1.233 Rev 0, June 2020, ML20091L698; verified via Federal Register notice 85 FR 35339 on 2026-11-13. Guide PDF body not captured. Later revisions out of scope.

## Named-only rules

- NEI 18-04 Rev 1 named only as the endorsed methodology document. Its text, figures, and tables are copyrighted industry material and are never reproduced or summarized at text depth here.
- Principal design criteria development owned by the live RG 1.232 pack; PRA technical adequacy by the live RG 1.200 pack; operating-plant risk-informed decision framework by the live RG 1.174 pack; Part 52 application mechanics by the live Part 52 pack. All named in prose only, never restated.
- 10 CFR Part 53 is named only; no rule text is packaged here and its status is not described from drafts.

## Stage outline limits

Methodology stages sit at outline depth only. Event categories, classification families, and defense-in-depth faces are named and defined; no numeric frequency threshold, dose limit, frequency-consequence target, or SSC performance value is packaged here. No evaluation performed, no design passes or fails, and no plant-specific licensing basis or adequacy verdict ships in this pack.

## Thin classes stop at the boundary

1. Guide or NEI 18-04 text level: section or position wording, clarification text, any numeric threshold, target, or criterion value.
2. Design-specific analysis: selecting LBEs, classifying SSCs, or judging defense-in-depth adequacy for a named design, or any PRA or dose computation.
3. Neighbour-owned method: PDC development detail, PRA adequacy verdicts, RG 1.174 decision process, Part 52 mechanics, or Part 53 status and content.

## Honest fallback

This knowledge pack provides orientation on the RG 1.233 endorsed licensing basis methodology at stage outline depth; it does not reproduce RG 1.233 or NEI 18-04 text or evaluate any real design. For the methodology text, consult RG 1.233 and NEI 18-04; for a plant-specific licensing basis, consult licensed engineering personnel.

## Not advice

Synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin and closed statement list; zero quotations. This pack is not the guide, not NEI 18-04, not regulation, and not legal or licensing advice. Advice requests refused.
