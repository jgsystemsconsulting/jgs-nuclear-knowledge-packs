# Chapter 6: Content of Application and Principal Design Criteria Integration

Sources: NRC RG 1.233 Rev 0, Guidance for a Technology-Inclusive, Risk-Informed, and Performance-Based Methodology to Inform the Licensing Basis and Content of Applications for Licenses, Certifications, and Approvals for Non-Light Water Reactors, June 2020, ML20091L698; verified via Federal Register notice 85 FR 35339 on 2026-11-13. Principal design criteria development is owned by the live RG 1.232 pack and Part 52 application mechanics by the live Part 52 pack; neither is restated here.

## Core Idea

Methodology results inform what an application contains. Event selection, classification, and defense-in-depth evaluation supply the event and classification basis that application content answers. The principal design criteria state the design commitments; the stages in this pack supply the basis those commitments answer, and criteria development itself stays owned by the live RG 1.232 pack. No safety analysis report chapter outline is stated as read from the guide, and no application is drafted here. This pack carries no plant-specific licensing basis and no adequacy instruction or verdict for any design.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Results into content. Event selection, classification, and defense-in-depth outcomes feed application content under the existing licensing paths; the stages produce the basis, not the application.
- PDC interface. The live RG 1.232 pack carries non-LWR principal design criteria adaptation; this pack states how the methodology feeds PDC development and does not restate PDC method.
- Existing paths only. Detailed application mechanics stay with the live Part 50 and Part 52 packs; this pack names the interfaces and does not restate their text.

## Mental Models

Picture three stage outputs arriving as one input bundle at the application door. Event families say what the basis must cover. Classification says which SSCs carry credited functions. Defense-in-depth says depth survived the risk-informed choices. The bundle feeds application content and PDC commitments. This chapter describes the handoff only; the rooms past the door belong to neighbour packs.
