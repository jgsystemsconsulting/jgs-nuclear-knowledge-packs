# Chapter 2: Technology-Inclusive, Risk-Informed, and Performance-Based Framework

Sources: NRC RG 1.233 Rev 0, Guidance for a Technology-Inclusive, Risk-Informed, and Performance-Based Methodology to Inform the Licensing Basis and Content of Applications for Licenses, Certifications, and Approvals for Non-Light Water Reactors, June 2020, ML20091L698; verified via Federal Register notice 85 FR 35339 on 2026-11-13. PRA adequacy is owned by the live RG 1.200 pack; the risk-informed decision framework for operating plants is owned by the live RG 1.174 pack. Neither is restated here.

## Core Idea

The endorsed methodology is technology-inclusive, risk-informed, and performance-based at once. Technology-inclusive means the frame is not locked to light-water plant features or to an inherited LWR event list. Risk-informed means risk insights shape selections rather than standing alone as the decision. Performance-based means attention stays on what functions must achieve, not only on prescribed equipment lists. The three qualifiers work together: drop any one and the method slides back toward technology-locked, risk-only, or prescription-only framing. The baseline approach is a staged outline: establish design and risk information, select licensing basis events, classify structures, systems, and components, evaluate defense-in-depth adequacy, and let the results inform principal design criteria and application content. A stage name is not a procedure.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Three-qualifier orientation. Technology-inclusive, risk-informed, and performance-based name the method's stance; they are not separate parallel methods.
- Staged baseline approach. Connected stages at outline depth: establish design and risk information; select licensing basis events; classify SSCs; evaluate defense-in-depth adequacy; inform principal design criteria and application content from those results.
- Complementary inputs. PRA results and deterministic engineering judgement both feed the method. Where PRA scope or uncertainty is limited, deterministic and engineering judgement complement the risk insights.
- Adequacy presumption, not adequacy method. The method presumes a PRA of quality adequate to the use made of it and stops there. PRA technical adequacy detail is out of this pack.

## Key Concepts

- Technology-inclusive: the methodology frame applies across non-LWR technologies rather than forcing water-cooled structures, coolants, or event lists onto other designs.
- Risk-informed: risk insights inform licensing-basis and application-content selections; risk numbers alone do not replace engineering judgement or deterministic checks.
- Performance-based: focus stays on required functions and outcomes rather than on fixed equipment prescriptions alone.
- Establish design and risk information: early stage that gathers the design picture and risk picture the later stages use; named only at outline depth here.
- Select licensing basis events: stage that groups events for the licensing basis; detail belongs to ch03.
- Classify SSCs: stage that sorts structures, systems, and components by safety function credit; detail belongs to ch04.
- Evaluate defense-in-depth adequacy: stage that checks plant capability and programmatic faces together; detail belongs to ch05.
- Inform PDC and application content: stage outcomes feed principal design criteria work and application content; detail belongs to ch06, with PDC method owned by the live RG 1.232 pack.
- PRA and deterministic judgement as complementary inputs: both used; neither erases the other inside this outline.
- PRA quality presumption: quality must fit the use made of the PRA; this pack states the presumption only.
- Neighbour ownership (prose only). The live RG 1.200 pack carries PRA technical adequacy orientation, and the live RG 1.174 pack carries the risk-informed decision framework for operating-plant licensing-basis changes. This pack never restates either framework.

## Mental Models

Picture three lenses locked together on one staged walk. Technology-inclusive keeps the walk free of LWR-only defaults. Risk-informed lets PRA insight shape which events and functions matter. Performance-based keeps the walk on functions and outcomes. The walk itself is outline only: gather design and risk information, select events, classify SSCs, check defense-in-depth, then carry results into PDC and application content. PRA supplies insight; deterministic judgement fills where PRA cannot; neither lens is a full substitute. Adequacy of the PRA for the use made of it is presumed here and judged elsewhere.

## Anti-patterns

Treating any single qualifier as the whole method is the chapter's named anti-pattern: technology-inclusive alone, risk-informed alone, or performance-based alone each miss the stance the guide names. A companion error is reading the stage list as a runbook or analysis procedure. A third error is pulling PRA technical adequacy criteria or the operating-plant risk-informed decision process into this pack; those stay with the live RG 1.200 and RG 1.174 packs. A fourth error is stating numeric thresholds, targets, or acceptance values for any stage; none are on record here.

## Key Takeaways

- The method is technology-inclusive, risk-informed, and performance-based together; the three qualifiers define one stance, not three separate methods.
- Baseline stages at outline depth: establish design and risk information, select licensing basis events, classify SSCs, evaluate defense-in-depth adequacy, and inform principal design criteria and application content.
- PRA and deterministic engineering judgement are complementary inputs; a stage name is not a procedure.
- The method presumes PRA quality adequate to the use made of it and stops there; adequacy detail and operating-plant risk-informed decision framing are owned by live sibling packs.
- No adequacy criteria, decision process, or numeric values are packaged in this chapter.
