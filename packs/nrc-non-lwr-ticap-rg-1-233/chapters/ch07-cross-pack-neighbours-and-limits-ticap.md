# Chapter 7: Cross-Pack Neighbours and Limits

Sources: NRC RG 1.233 Rev 0, Guidance for a Technology-Inclusive, Risk-Informed, and Performance-Based Methodology to Inform the Licensing Basis and Content of Applications for Licenses, Certifications, and Approvals for Non-Light Water Reactors, June 2020, ML20091L698; verified via Federal Register notice 85 FR 35339 on 2026-11-13. NEI 18-04 Rev 1 and 10 CFR Part 53 named only, not sources.

## Core Idea

This pack orients one slice: the RG 1.233 endorsed methodology at stage outline depth. Everything past that slice belongs to neighbours this chapter names in prose only, never by link. The live RG 1.232 pack carries non-LWR principal design criteria adaptation; this pack states how the methodology feeds PDC development and does not restate PDC method. The live RG 1.200 pack carries PRA technical adequacy orientation, and the live RG 1.174 pack carries the risk-informed plant-specific change baseline; neither is restated here. The live Part 52 pack carries design certification, combined license, ITAAC, and RG 1.206 application structure. 10 CFR Part 53 is named only; no rule text is packaged here and its status is not described from drafts.

## Frameworks Introduced

- PDC neighbour boundary. Non-LWR principal design criteria adaptation lives with the live RG 1.232 pack. This pack names the feed from methodology results into PDC work and stops there.
- PRA and decision-framework boundary. PRA technical adequacy and the operating-plant risk-informed decision framework live with sibling packs. The RG 1.174 framework differs in purpose from this non-LWR methodology: it frames licensing-basis changes to operating plants, while this pack orients how a non-LWR licensing basis and application content are first informed.
- Application mechanics boundary. Design certification, combined license, ITAAC, and application structure live with the live Part 52 pack. This pack never restates Part 52 mechanics.
- Rule boundary. 10 CFR Part 53 is named only; no rule text is packaged here and its status is not described from drafts. Nothing here predicts how the methodology will map to any future rule.

## Key Concepts

- Neighbour naming: sibling packs named in prose only, never slug-linked, per repo discipline.
- PDC feed: methodology results inform commitments the principal design criteria state; criteria development itself stays outside this pack.
- PRA presumption: method presumes PRA quality adequate to the use made of it; adequacy proof stays with the live RG 1.200 pack.
- Decision-framework difference: operating-plant change framing under RG 1.174 answers a different question than non-LWR basis formation under RG 1.233.
- Thin classes: three question classes stop at the pack boundary, (1) guide or NEI 18-04 text level, (2) design-specific analysis for a named design or any PRA or dose computation, (3) neighbour-owned method including PDC detail, PRA adequacy verdicts, the RG 1.174 decision process, Part 52 mechanics, or Part 53 status and content.
- Stop line: point where orientation ends and reader returns to the guide, NEI 18-04, neighbour packs, or licensed engineering personnel.

## Mental Models

Read this chapter as a boundary map, not a second body of content. Inside the line sits RG 1.233 methodology orientation at stage outline depth. Outside the line sit PDC adaptation, PRA adequacy, the operating-plant decision framework, Part 52 mechanics, and Part 53. When a question points outside the line, the honest move is to name the neighbour and stop, not to stretch this pack to cover it.

## Anti-patterns

Restating neighbour text inside this pack is the named anti-pattern: no PDC method, no PRA adequacy criteria, no RG 1.174 decision process, no Part 52 mechanics, and no Part 53 rule description ships here. A companion error treats named-only hooks as coverage, as if naming RG 1.200, RG 1.174, RG 1.232, Part 52, or Part 53 answered the method or rule question. A third error asks this pack for a plant-specific licensing basis or an adequacy verdict it cannot give.
