# Chapter 1: RG 1.233 Scope, Purpose, and LMP Relationship

Sources: NRC RG 1.233 Rev 0, Guidance for a Technology-Inclusive, Risk-Informed, and Performance-Based Methodology to Inform the Licensing Basis and Content of Applications for Licenses, Certifications, and Approvals for Non-Light Water Reactors, June 2020, ML20091L698; verified via Federal Register notice 85 FR 35339 on 2026-11-13. NEI 18-04 Rev 1 named only; body not captured.

## Core Idea

RG 1.233 Rev 0 (June 2020, ML20091L698; 85 FR 35339, June 9, 2020) gives guidance for a technology-inclusive, risk-informed, and performance-based methodology to inform the licensing basis and the content of applications for licenses, certifications, and approvals for non-light water reactors. A Regulatory Guide describes one method the NRC staff finds acceptable; it is guidance, not regulation, and its text binds no licencee through this pack. The guide endorses, with staff clarifications, NEI 18-04 Revision 1, the Licensing Modernization Project (LMP) methodology document. An LWR-framed licensing approach leaves gaps for non-LWR designs; this guide supplies a technology-inclusive complement rather than a water-cooled event list or criteria set forced onto other technologies.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Purpose and applicability. RG 1.233 orients how a non-LWR applicant informs licensing basis and application content through a staged methodology. The guide is the staff-position source; this pack is synthesized orientation keyed to the recorded pin, not the guide and not a substitute for it.
- Endorsement and endorsed document. The Regulatory Guide is US Government work. NEI 18-04 Rev 1 is copyrighted industry material named only here as the endorsed methodology document. This pack describes the methodology at stage-outline depth only; no NEI 18-04 text, figure, table, or staff clarification wording is reproduced, and neither body was captured in this repo.
- Staged methodology outline. The method selects licensing basis events, classifies structures, systems, and components, evaluates defense-in-depth adequacy, and uses the results to inform the licensing basis, the principal design criteria, and the content of the application. A stage name is not a procedure.
- Existing licensing paths. Methodology results inform application content under 10 CFR Part 50 and 10 CFR Part 52. Detailed Part 50 and Part 52 text, and non-LWR principal design criteria development under RG 1.232, are owned by live sibling packs; this pack names the interfaces and does not restate their text.

## Mental Models

Read RG 1.233 as guidance on how to inform a non-LWR licensing basis and application content, not as regulation that supplies either. Keep the endorsement and the endorsed document apart: the RG is the staff-position source and US Government work; NEI 18-04 is named only and stays outside the pack. Treat the stage list as a map of later chapters, not as analysis steps to run from these notes. Place Part 50, Part 52, and RG 1.232 foundation text with their live sibling packs; this chapter only names the interface.

## Anti-patterns

Treating these orientation notes as a substitute for RG 1.233 is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the notes locate scope, purpose, pin, and LMP relationship; the guide carries staff-position detail. A companion error is treating NEI 18-04 text, figures, or tables as pack content, or summarizing that body under a synthesis label. A third error is pulling Part 50, Part 52, or RG 1.232 method detail from this pack; those texts stay with live sibling packs. A fourth error is reading stage names as procedures or analysis instructions.

## Key Takeaways

- RG 1.233 Rev 0 (June 2020, ML20091L698; 85 FR 35339) guides a technology-inclusive, risk-informed, and performance-based methodology for non-LWR licensing basis and application content; it is guidance, not regulation.
- The guide endorses NEI 18-04 Rev 1 (LMP) with staff clarifications; NEI 18-04 is copyrighted industry material named only here.
- The methodology is staged at outline depth: select licensing basis events, classify SSCs, evaluate defense-in-depth adequacy, and inform licensing basis, principal design criteria, and application content.
- Results feed existing Part 50 and Part 52 paths; detailed text and PDC development are owned by live sibling packs and are not restated here.
- This pack orients foundation and pin only; guide body and NEI 18-04 body were not captured.
