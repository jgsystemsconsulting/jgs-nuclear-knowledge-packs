## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Endorsement is not the endorsed document

RG 1.233 endorses a technology-inclusive methodology for informing the non-LWR licensing basis and application content. NEI 18-04 Rev 1 (Licensing Modernization Project) is the named endorsed methodology document. The RG is US Government work; NEI 18-04 is copyrighted industry material named only here. A Regulatory Guide describes one method staff finds acceptable; it is guidance, not regulation.

### Stages in outline, not analysis

The methodology is oriented as connected stages: select licensing basis events; classify structures, systems, and components; evaluate defense-in-depth adequacy; and use those results to inform application content and principal design criteria commitments. Stages are named and defined at outline depth only. No numeric thresholds, targets, or adequacy verdicts are packaged here.

### PRA and deterministic judgement are complementary

The framework is risk-informed and performance-based. PRA quality is presumed adequate for the use made of it; this pack does not carry PRA technical adequacy. Live neighbour packs own PRA adequacy and operating-plant risk-informed decision framing.

### Pin rule

This pack pins RG 1.233 Rev 0, June 2020, ML20091L698, verified via Federal Register notice 85 FR 35339 on 2026-11-13. Live NRC listing page checked the same date returned 404 (dead listing); FR pin stands. Guide PDF body not captured. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope.

### Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin and closed statement set. This pack is not the guide, not NEI 18-04, not regulation, and not counsel. This pack quotes nothing; RG verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences by licence policy and this pack uses none.
