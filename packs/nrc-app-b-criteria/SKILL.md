## When to use
**Prerequisites:** basic regulatory vocabulary (licensee, quality assurance program, regulation versus guidance), and awareness that a facility QA commitment binds through its own licence documents, not through this pack. RG 1.28 endorsement material and NQA-1 mapping live with the RG 1.28 orientation pack, named here in prose.

Use this skill for lookup questions about the eighteen Appendix B criteria: what a named criterion requires, which criterion owns a program topic, and how the criteria order across the program stages. The pack answers with synthesized reference notes keyed by criterion numeral and title.

- **Without arguments**: read Core Frameworks, then the chapter index.

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### One appendix, eighteen criteria

Appendix B states quality assurance criteria for activities affecting quality in numbered criteria I to XVIII. A question is located by naming the criterion it touches. ch01 carries the full numeral and title map; ch02 to ch05 carry the criterion substance grouped by program stage.

### Criterion number is the address

Answers cite the criterion by roman numeral and its title as printed in the eCFR text. Groupings in this pack are lookup aids only; the appendix numbers every criterion separately and no grouping here changes that.

### Pin rule

This pack pins eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, checked 2026-11-11. Later amendments are out of scope.

### Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized lookups. They are not the regulation, not a QA program, and not counsel. Verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences.

## Scope & Limits

This pack pins eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09 (pin-check 2026-11-11). Later amendments are out of scope.

- Criterion lookup only: the eighteen Appendix B criteria (I to XVIII) in reference-note form, plus the appendix frame. This is not all of Part 50 and not a QA program.
- Not the RG 1.28 body. Endorsement positions, clarifications, and the NQA-1 edition pathway live in the RG 1.28 orientation pack, named in prose, never linked.
- No NQA-1 text. ASME NQA-1 is named only as the endorsed standard; its requirements and per-criterion mapping are never packaged.
- Not legal advice and not licensing advice. Advice requests refused.
- Not a plant-specific QA program and not a QA program description. A facility QA commitment binds through its own licence documents.
- Synthesized notes keyed to the recorded eCFR As-of date. Verbatim excerpts capped at two sentences.
- No slug links to any sibling pack. Neighbours are named in prose only, per repo discipline.
- Two question classes are thin in this pack and stop at the pack boundary: (1) RG 1.28 endorsement and NQA-1 mapping questions; (2) QA program design, audit program design, or licensee QA program description questions.
