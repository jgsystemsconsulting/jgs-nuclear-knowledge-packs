# Cheatsheet: nrc-app-b-criteria

Decision rules for which chapter owns a question, the pin rule, the not-advice rule, and the thin classes.

## Which chapter owns the question

- What Appendix B is, its introduction, scope, or the eighteen-criteria map: ch01.
- Organization or the QA program criterion (I, II): ch02.
- Design, procurement, instructions, or document control criteria (III-VII): ch03.
- Material identification, special processes, inspection, test, or measuring equipment criteria (VIII-XII): ch04.
- Handling, status, nonconforming material, corrective action, records, or audits criteria (XIII-XVIII): ch05.
- Where the pack stops and what lives elsewhere: ch06.

## Pin rule

eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11. Later amendments are out of scope. Answers cite the criterion numeral and title as printed in the pinned text.

## Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized lookups; verbatim excerpts are capped at two sentences. This pack is not the regulation, not legal or licensing advice, and not counsel. Verify any requirement against the current eCFR text before relying on it.

## Lookup limits

- Criterion lookup only: Appendix B criteria I to XVIII plus the frame. Not all of Part 50.
- Not the RG 1.28 body. Endorsement positions and the NQA-1 edition pathway live in the RG 1.28 orientation pack, named in prose.
- No NQA-1 text. NQA-1 is named only as the endorsed standard.
- Not a plant-specific QA program and not a QA program description. A facility QA commitment binds through its own licence documents.

## When the question is thin

Two classes are thin in this pack and stop at the pack boundary: (1) RG 1.28 endorsement and NQA-1 mapping questions; (2) QA program design, audit program design, or licensee QA program description questions. State the thin reason and stop.
