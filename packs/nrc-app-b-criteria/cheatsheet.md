# Cheatsheet: nrc-app-b-criteria

Decision rules: chapter owns question, pin rule, not-advice rule, and thin classes.

## Chapter owns question

- Appendix B frame, introduction, scope, or eighteen-criteria map: ch01.
- Organization or QA program criterion (I, II): ch02.
- Design, procurement, instructions, or document control criteria (III-VII): ch03.
- Material identification, special processes, inspection, test, or measuring equipment criteria (VIII-XII): ch04.
- Handling, status, nonconforming material, corrective action, records, or audits (XIII-XVIII): ch05.
- What pack covers, neighbours, or limits: ch06.

## Pin rule

eCFR title 10 current 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11. Criterion numeral and title printed in pinned text; later amendments out scope.

## Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized lookups; verbatim excerpts capped two sentences. This pack is not regulation, not legal or licensing advice, and not counsel. Verify any requirement against current eCFR text before relying on it.

## Lookup limits

- Criterion lookup only: Appendix B criteria I to XVIII plus frame. Not all Part 50.
- Not RG 1.28 body. Endorsement positions and NQA-1 edition pathway live in RG 1.28 orientation pack, named in prose.
- No NQA-1 text. NQA-1 named only as endorsed standard.
- Not plant-specific QA program and not QA program description. Facility QA commitment binds through its own licence documents.

## When question thin

Two classes are thin in pack and stop pack boundary: (1) RG 1.28 endorsement and NQA-1 mapping questions; (2) QA program design, audit program design, or licensee QA program description questions. State thin reason and stop.
