# Chapter 2: Criteria I-II: organization and QA program

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix B (eCFR), criteria I-II; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-11 (title 10 current as of 2026-11-09).

## Core Idea

Criteria I and II are program-bearing criteria: Criterion I puts establishment and execution of the quality assurance program on the applicant and fixes who holds responsibility and authority; Criterion II requires the program established early, documented in writing, carried out through plant life, and regularly reviewed. Every remaining criterion operates inside the structure these two create.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Responsibility stays while work moves. The applicant may delegate program work to contractors, agents, or consultants, but retains responsibility for the program.
- Assurance functions separate from doing. Quality assurance functions mean assuring activities are appropriately performed and verifying, by checking, auditing, and inspecting, that activities affecting safety-related functions were correctly performed.

## Key Concepts

- Criterion I Organization. The applicant is responsible for establishing and executing the quality assurance program; delegation does not transfer responsibility. Authority and duties of persons and organizations performing activities affecting safety-related functions are established and delineated in writing. Persons and organizations performing quality assurance functions have sufficient authority and organizational freedom to identify quality problems, initiate or provide solutions, and verify implementation; they report to a management level that assures that authority and organizational freedom, including independence from cost and schedule when opposed to safety considerations.
- Criterion II Quality Assurance Program. The applicant establishes the program at the earliest practicable time, documented by written policies, procedures, or instructions, carried out throughout plant life. The program identifies structures, systems, and components covered and the major organizations participating with designated functions. Control over activities affecting quality is provided to an extent consistent with importance to safety; activities are accomplished under suitably controlled conditions (appropriate equipment, suitable environmental conditions, prerequisites satisfied). The program accounts for need of special controls, processes, test equipment, tools, and skills, and provides indoctrination and training as necessary assure suitable proficiency. The applicant and participating managements regularly review status and adequacy of the program and their parts.

## Mental Models

Read the pair as frame then engine: Criterion I answers who owns quality assurance and who may verify independently of cost and schedule pressure; Criterion II answers what exists on paper and how it stays alive. Criteria III to XVIII are program content this pair must carry.

## Anti-patterns

Turning the criteria into organization-chart or program-design advice; the criteria require structure and review, not a specific chart shape. Reading Criterion II as approval of any particular program description; facility programs bind through their own licence documents, not through this pack.

## Key Takeaways

- Criterion I: applicant responsibility, written delineation of authority and duties, quality assurance functions with organizational freedom and independence.
- Criterion II: documented program at earliest practicable time, carried out through plant life, extent consistent with importance to safety, regularly reviewed.
