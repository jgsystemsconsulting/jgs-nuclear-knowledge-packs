# Chapter 6: Neighbours and limits

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix B (eCFR), frame facts; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-11 (title 10 current as of 2026-11-09).

## Core Idea

This pack carries criterion lookup on Appendix B only: the appendix frame, the eighteen criteria, and nothing else. Named neighbours own everything adjacent. The RG 1.28 orientation pack owns endorsement and NQA-1 mapping questions. The Part 50 licensing basis pack owns the licensing-basis questions around Appendix B, including the § 50.34 application hook and the updated FSAR. RG 1.28 Rev 6 exists and endorses ASME NQA-1 for design and construction; that endorsement position, and any NQA-1 edition pathway, is recorded in the RG 1.28 orientation pack, never here.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Slice discipline. A pack that pins live Appendix B text answers criterion questions and stops where the other sources begin; knowing the stop is part of the answer.

## Key Concepts

- What this pack is: the eighteen criteria plus the frame, in synthesized reference-note form, pinned to eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09 and checked 2026-11-11.
- What this pack is not: not the regulation text itself (read the current eCFR text before relying on any requirement); not the RG 1.28 body; no NQA-1 text (NQA-1 is named only); not a QA program, not a QA program description, and not program design guidance; not legal or licensing advice; not a Part 50 reference, not Appendix A coverage, and not any other slice.
- Neighbour homes, named in prose: the RG 1.28 orientation pack for endorsement positions and NQA-1 mapping; the Part 50 licensing basis pack for the licensing-basis sections around Appendix B, including the § 50.34 application hook; guidance-layer framing lives in `nrc-nuclear`.

## Mental Models

Two thin classes stop at the pack boundary. First, RG 1.28 endorsement and NQA-1 mapping questions: thin here because this pack carries no endorsement position and no NQA-1 text; route those to the RG 1.28 orientation pack. Second, QA program design, audit program design, or licensee QA program description questions: thin here because Appendix B states what the criteria require, not how a facility builds its program; a facility's QA commitment binds through its own licence documents. State the thin reason and stop at the pack boundary.

## Anti-patterns

Summarizing an endorsement position for RG 1.28 or an NQA-1 edition pathway; that is the orientation pack's content, not this pack's. Presenting named neighbours as coverage promises of this pack; the neighbours are signposts, not included content.

## Key Takeaways

- Criterion lookup only: Appendix B criteria I to XVIII plus the frame.
- RG 1.28 and NQA-1 are named-only neighbours; their substance stays out.
- Program design and licensing questions stop at the boundary, in prose, with the thin reason stated.
