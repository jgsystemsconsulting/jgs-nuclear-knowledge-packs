# Chapter 4: Criteria VIII-XII: material, process, inspection, and test

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix B (eCFR), criteria VIII-XII; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-11 (title 10 current as of 2026-11-09).

## Core Idea

Criteria VIII to XII control the physical work: identification of items so the wrong or defective part is never used, qualification of special processes, inspection by persons other than the performers, a test program that identifies required tests, and calibration of measuring and test equipment. These five criteria exercise the material and measurement half of the program spine.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Traceable identity. Every item carries identification, on itself or in records traceable to it, held through fabrication, erection, installation, and use.
- Verification by separation. Inspections are performed by individuals other than those who performed the activity inspected.

## Key Concepts

- Criterion VIII Identification and Control Materials, Parts, and Components. Measures maintain identification (heat number, part number, serial number, or other appropriate means) and are designed to prevent use incorrect or defective material, parts, and components, including partially fabricated assemblies.
- Criterion IX Control Special Processes. Special processes, including welding, heat treating, and nondestructive testing, are controlled and accomplished by qualified personnel using qualified procedures in accordance with applicable codes, standards, specifications, criteria, and other special requirements.
- Criterion X Inspection. A program of inspection of activities affecting quality is established and executed by or for the organization performing the activity, to verify conformance with the documented instructions, procedures, and drawings; examinations are performed by individuals other than those who performed the activity inspected. Examinations, measurements, or tests occur for each work operation where necessary assure quality; where direct inspection is impossible or disadvantageous, indirect control by monitoring processing methods, equipment, and personnel applies, and both apply where neither alone is adequate. Mandatory hold points requiring witnessing by the applicant's designated representative are indicated in appropriate documents.
- Criterion XI Test Control. A test program assures all testing required to demonstrate structures, systems, and components perform satisfactorily in service is identified and performed in accordance with written test procedures incorporating requirements and acceptance limits from applicable design documents. The program includes, where appropriate, proof tests before installation, preoperational tests, and operational tests. Procedures assure prerequisites met, instrumentation available, and suitable environmental conditions; results are documented and evaluated.
- Criterion XII Control Measuring and Test Equipment. Tools, gages, instruments, and other measuring and testing devices used in activities affecting quality are properly controlled, calibrated, and adjusted at specified periods to maintain accuracy within necessary limits.

## Mental Models

Read the group as material identity then proof: VIII keeps the right part in place, IX keeps risky processes in qualified hands, X and XI generate objective evidence that items and their tests meet documented criteria, and XII keeps the instruments behind that evidence trustworthy. Inspection (X) verifies work already done; testing (XI) demonstrates behavior under conditions.

## Anti-patterns

Reading inspection and test as interchangeable; Criterion X verifies conformance during work, Criterion XI runs a program of identified tests. Treating Criterion XII as calibration procedure template; it requires control and scheduled accuracy, not a specific calibration document form.

## Key Takeaways

- VIII: traceable identification prevents incorrect or defective item use.
- IX: special processes demand qualified personnel and qualified procedures.
- X: inspection verifies by persons other than performers, with hold points where mandatory.
- XI: required tests identified, procedured, and their results evaluated.
- XII: measuring and test equipment controlled and calibrated at specified periods.
