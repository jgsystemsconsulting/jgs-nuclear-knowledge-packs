# Chapter 1: Appendix B frame and eighteen-criteria map

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix B (eCFR), title and introduction; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-11 (title 10 current as of 2026-11-09).

## Core Idea

Appendix B to 10 CFR Part 50, Quality Assurance Criteria for Nuclear Power Plants and Fuel Reprocessing Plants, states eighteen quality assurance criteria, numbered I to XVIII. The appendix introduction ties each application type to its required quality assurance showing: the § 50.34 route carries construction permit and operating license applicants, while Part 52 application types cite named sections (combined licenses 52.79, early site permits 52.17, design approvals 52.137, design certifications 52.47, manufacturing licenses 52.157), and matching Part 53 sections are cited alongside each. The criteria then govern all activities affecting safety-related functions of structures, systems, and components. This chapter fixes the frame and the numeral-title map the rest of the pack indexes.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Application-type hook. The introduction names the safety analysis report document each applicant type must carry its quality assurance description in; the criteria themselves apply once the hook is satisfied.
- Activity scope, not item scope alone. The appendix reaches activities: designing, purchasing, fabricating, handling, shipping, storing, cleaning, erecting, installing, inspecting, testing, operating, maintaining, repairing, refueling, and modifying.

## Key Concepts

- Appendix definition: quality assurance comprises all planned and systematic actions necessary provide adequate confidence structure, system, or component perform satisfactorily in service; includes quality control, the subset tied to physical characteristics and predetermined requirements.
- Scope sentence: pertinent requirements apply all activities affecting safety-related functions structures, systems, and components.
- Criteria I and II carry organization and the quality assurance program; III to VII carry design, procurement, instructions, and document control; VIII to XII carry material, process, inspection, and test; XIII to XVIII carry handling, status, nonconformances, corrective action, records, and audits.

## Mental Models

Read Appendix B as an indexed program spine: one introductory frame, then eighteen separately numbered criteria that order roughly as program stage, from organization through design and procurement controls, into material and inspection controls, ending in records and audits. Question locates by criterion numeral; map below doubles pack index.

Eighteen-criteria map (numeral, live title, one-line function):

| Numeral | Title | Function |
|---|---|---|
| I | Organization | Assign responsibility and authority for the quality assurance program |
| II | Quality Assurance Program | Document and carry out the program through plant life |
| III | Design Control | Translate design bases into specifications; verify design by others |
| IV | Procurement Document Control | Put quality requirements into procurement documents |
| V | Instructions, Procedures, and Drawings | Prescribe quality-affecting activities with acceptance criteria |
| VI | Document Control | Control issuance, approval, distribution, and changes of documents |
| VII | Control Purchased Material, Equipment, and Services | Assure purchased items conform to procurement documents |
| VIII | Identification and Control Materials, Parts, and Components | Keep identification traceable; prevent incorrect-item use |
| IX | Control Special Processes | Welding, heat treating, nondestructive testing by qualified personnel and procedures |
| X | Inspection | Verify conformance by persons other than activity performers |
| XI | Test Control | Identify required tests; written procedures; documented results |
| XII | Control Measuring and Test Equipment | Calibrate and control measuring and test devices |
| XIII | Handling, Storage and Shipping | Prevent damage or deterioration in handling and storage |
| XIV | Inspection, Test, and Operating Status | Mark status; preclude inadvertent bypass or operation |
| XV | Nonconforming Materials, Parts, or Components | Control and disposition items not conforming to requirements |
| XVI | Corrective Action | Identify, correct, and report conditions adverse to quality |
| XVII | Quality Assurance Records | Retain sufficient, identifiable, retrievable records |
| XVIII | Audits | Planned periodic audits by personnel independent of audited areas |

## Anti-patterns

Reading the map as requirement text; the criteria bodies live in ch02 to ch05. Treating ch01 as licensing-basis coverage; application content requirements belong the Part 50 licensing basis pack, named in prose.

## Key Takeaways

- Appendix B states eighteen criteria, I to XVIII, under one introduction.
- The introduction hooks application types to § 50.34 and named Part 52 and Part 53 sections.
- Criteria apply to activities affecting safety-related functions, not only to items.
- Numerals and titles above are the pack's addressing scheme.
