# Chapter 1: Appendix B frame and eighteen-criteria map

Sources: 10 CFR Part 50 Appendix B (eCFR), title and introduction; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-11 (title 10 current as of 2026-11-09).

## Core Idea

Appendix B to 10 CFR Part 50, Quality Assurance Criteria for Nuclear Power Plants and Fuel Reprocessing Plants, is the part's quality assurance layer: an introduction that ties each license-application type to a required quality assurance showing, followed by eighteen criteria numbered I to XVIII. The introduction hooks the appendix into the licensing structure through the sections that require it: § 50.34 for construction permit and operating license applicants, and named Part 52 sections for combined licenses (52.79), early site permits (52.17), design approvals (52.137), design certifications (52.47), and manufacturing licenses (52.157), each cited alongside a matching Part 53 section. The criteria then govern all activities affecting the safety-related functions of structures, systems, and components. This chapter fixes that frame and the numeral-title map the rest of the pack indexes.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Application-type hook. The introduction names, for each application type, the section of Part 50, Part 52, or Part 53 that requires a description of the quality assurance program in the applicant's safety analysis report; the criteria themselves apply once that hook is satisfied.
- Activity scope, not item scope alone. The appendix reaches activities, not only hardware: designing, purchasing, fabricating, handling, shipping, storing, cleaning, erecting, installing, inspecting, testing, operating, maintaining, repairing, refueling, and modifying.

## Key Concepts

- Appendix definition: as used in the appendix, quality assurance comprises all those planned and systematic actions necessary to provide adequate confidence that a structure, system, or component will perform satisfactorily in service. It includes quality control, the subset of those actions related to the physical characteristics of a material, structure, component, or system measured against predetermined requirements.
- Scope sentence: the pertinent requirements of the appendix apply to all activities affecting the safety-related functions of structures, systems, and components.
- Criteria I and II carry organization and the quality assurance program; criteria III to VII carry design, procurement, instructions, and document control; criteria VIII to XII carry material, process, inspection, and test; criteria XIII to XVIII carry handling, status, nonconformances, corrective action, records, and audits.

## Mental Models

Read Appendix B as an indexed program spine: one introductory frame, then eighteen separately numbered criteria that run roughly in program-stage order, from organization through design and procurement controls, into material and inspection controls, and ending in records and audits. Any question locates by criterion numeral; the map below doubles as the pack index.

Eighteen-criteria map (numeral, title as printed in the pinned text, one-line function):

| Numeral | Title | Function |
|---|---|---|
| I | Organization | Assign responsibility and authority for the quality assurance program |
| II | Quality Assurance Program | Document the program and carry it out through plant life |
| III | Design Control | Translate design bases into specifications; verify the design by others |
| IV | Procurement Document Control | Put quality requirements into procurement documents |
| V | Instructions, Procedures, and Drawings | Prescribe quality-affecting activities with acceptance criteria |
| VI | Document Control | Control issuance, approval, distribution, and changes of documents |
| VII | Control of Purchased Material, Equipment, and Services | Assure purchased items conform to procurement documents |
| VIII | Identification and Control of Materials, Parts, and Components | Keep identification traceable; prevent use of incorrect items |
| IX | Control of Special Processes | Welding, heat treating, and nondestructive testing by qualified personnel and procedures |
| X | Inspection | Verify conformance by persons other than the activity performers |
| XI | Test Control | Identify required tests; written procedures; documented results |
| XII | Control of Measuring and Test Equipment | Calibrate and control measuring and test devices |
| XIII | Handling, Storage and Shipping | Prevent damage or deterioration in handling and storage |
| XIV | Inspection, Test, and Operating Status | Mark status; preclude inadvertent bypass or operation |
| XV | Nonconforming Materials, Parts, or Components | Control and disposition items that do not conform to requirements |
| XVI | Corrective Action | Identify, correct, and report conditions adverse to quality |
| XVII | Quality Assurance Records | Retain sufficient, identifiable, retrievable records |
| XVIII | Audits | Planned periodic audits by personnel independent of the audited areas |

## Anti-patterns

Reading the map as requirement text; the criteria bodies live in ch02 to ch05, one group per chapter. Treating this chapter as licensing-basis coverage; the application content requirements behind the hooks belong to the Part 50 licensing basis pack, named in prose, not here.

## Key Takeaways

- Appendix B states eighteen criteria, I to XVIII, under one introduction.
- The introduction hooks application types to § 50.34 and to named Part 52 and Part 53 sections.
- The criteria apply to activities affecting safety-related functions, not only to items.
- The numerals and titles in the map are the pack's addressing scheme.
