# RG 1.28 Rev 6 quality assurance program criteria orientation

**Source**: NRC RG 1.28 Rev 6, September 2023, ML23177A002; FR-notice-pinned 2026-11-08. | **Licence**: Public Domain (US Government work, 17 U.S.C. 105) | **Chapters**: 7

## When to use

**Prerequisites:** basic regulatory vocabulary (regulation versus guidance, endorsement, licensee, applicant) and awareness that 10 CFR 50 Appendix B, not this guide or this pack, is what binds. The standard the guide endorses is ASME NQA-1; its text is outside this pack.

The pack answers with synthesized, FR-notice-pinned notes, cited by chapter.

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Endorsement with conditions

RG 1.28 Rev 6 works by endorsement: the NRC accepts named requirement sets of ASME NQA-1-2017, NQA-1-2019, and NQA-1-2022 (Part I and Part II requirements) for quality assurance programs during design and construction of nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants, and states clarifications and regulatory positions alongside. Read endorsement plus conditions together: either piece alone misstates the acceptable path. A second, narrower endorsement covers NEI 14-05A Rev 1 on accreditation in lieu of commercial grade surveys for laboratory calibration and test services.

### Regulation, guide, and standard as three layers

The binding layer is 10 CFR 50 Appendix B and its eighteen criteria. The guide layer is RG 1.28 Rev 6, which describes methods the NRC staff considers acceptable for implementing the regulations; a guide is not the regulation. The standard layer is ASME NQA-1, the consensus vehicle whose requirement shape the guide endorses. A question is located by naming which layer it touches.

### Pin rule

This pack pins RG 1.28 Rev 6, September 2023, accession ML23177A002, issued through Federal Register notice 2023-19548 (88 FR 62292), pin date 2026-11-08. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope. The guide text, not this pack, states the criteria and conditions themselves.

### Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized. They are not the guide, not the regulation, and not counsel. Verbatim excerpts of RG 1.28 text are capped at two sentences; ASME NQA-1 is quoted zero times and named with edition and owner only.

## Scope & Limits

This pack pins RG 1.28 Rev 6, September 2023, ML23177A002 (Federal Register notice 2023-19548), pin date 2026-11-08. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope.

- ASME NQA-1 is named with editions (2017, 2019, 2022) and owner (ASME) only; its text is not packaged and no signpost rows for it exist in this pack.
- Appendix B criteria are a names-only orientation list; the criterion bodies belong to the App B pack, not this pack.
- NEI 14-05A is a name hook only: no document content, no accreditation or survey procedure material.
- Not legal advice. Not a plant-specific quality assurance program: an actual program is the licensee's or applicant's own document, not these notes.
- Sector anti-scope still applies: weapons and weapons-design physics, classified or export-controlled or safeguards-sensitive material, 10 CFR Part 35 and other non-power-plant isotope programmes, paywalled standard text.
