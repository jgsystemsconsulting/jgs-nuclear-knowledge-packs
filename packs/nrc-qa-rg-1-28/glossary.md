# Glossary: nrc-qa-rg-1-28

Synthesized orientation definitions keyed to this pack's RG 1.28 Rev 6 facts, not substitute source text. Alphabetical.

- **accreditation in lieu of commercial grade surveys**: the endorsed topic of NEI 14-05A Rev 1 (Sep 2020) in RG 1.28 Rev 6: accreditation is the acceptable alternative route to commercial grade surveys for laboratory calibration and test services. (ch06)
- **Appendix B**: 10 CFR 50 Appendix B, the regulation that holds the quality assurance criteria for nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants. RG 1.28 Rev 6 serves those criteria; this pack names the eighteen criteria titles-only, and the criterion bodies belong to the App B pack. (ch02)
- **clarification**: a guide statement issued alongside the endorsement that resolves how an endorsed requirement reads. One of the two condition types RG 1.28 Rev 6 carries. (ch05)
- **commercial grade survey**: the survey route that the endorsed NEI 14-05A Rev 1 topic replaces with accreditation for laboratory calibration and test services. This pack carries no survey procedure content. (ch06)
- **design and construction phase**: the phase scope of RG 1.28 Rev 6 and its endorsement: quality assurance programs during design and construction of nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants. (ch01)
- **endorsement**: the mechanism RG 1.28 Rev 6 works by: the NRC finds named requirement sets of a consensus standard acceptable, with certain clarifications and regulatory positions attached. Rev 6 endorses ASME NQA-1-2017, NQA-1-2019, and NQA-1-2022 this way, and also endorses NEI 14-05A Rev 1 on one topic. (ch01)
- **NQA-1**: the consensus standard ASME owns and publishes, whose Part I and Part II requirements RG 1.28 Rev 6 endorses in three editions: NQA-1-2017, NQA-1-2019, and NQA-1-2022. ASME copyright; the standard's text is not packaged here and is named with edition and owner only. (ch03)
- **Part I requirements**: one of the two NQA-1 requirement sets the Rev 6 endorsement names; the endorsed vehicle that maps onto the Appendix B criteria shape. Requirement bodies are not in this pack. (ch04)
- **Part II requirements**: the second NQA-1 requirement set the Rev 6 endorsement names, alongside Part I, for quality assurance programs during design and construction. Requirement bodies are not in this pack. (ch04)
- **QA program**: the quality assurance program whose expectations RG 1.28 Rev 6 addresses for the design and construction of nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants. The guide describes an acceptable way to meet those expectations; the regulation (Appendix B) binds. (ch01)
- **QA program description**: the description 50.34 requires in both safety analysis report stages, with equivalent descriptions in Part 52 applications; 50.54(a) makes implementing it a standing license condition. (ch07)
- **regulatory guide**: a document in the NRC series that describes methods the staff considers acceptable for implementing the agency's regulations. The guide is not the regulation. (ch01)
- **regulatory position**: a guide statement giving the NRC's position where it needs more, less, or different than the endorsed text provides. Position bodies live in RG 1.28 Rev 6 itself, not in this pack. (ch05)
