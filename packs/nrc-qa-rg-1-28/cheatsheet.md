# Cheatsheet: nrc-qa-rg-1-28

Decision rules for which chapter owns a question, the pin rule, the not-advice rule, the QA program limits, and where questions run past this pack.

## Which chapter owns the question

- What RG 1.28 Rev 6 is, how it relates to 10 CFR 50 Appendix B, guide versus regulation, or the design and construction phase scope: ch01.
- The eighteen Appendix B criteria as a names-only list or the criteria-to-requirements bridge: ch02.
- Which ASME NQA-1 editions are endorsed and how the edition pathway works: ch03.
- What the endorsement names as Part I and Part II requirements and what adopting that shape means: ch04.
- How clarifications and regulatory positions condition the endorsement: ch05.
- NEI 14-05A Rev 1 and accreditation in lieu of commercial grade surveys for laboratory calibration and test services: ch06.
- Where the QA program description enters applications (50.34, Part 52 equivalents) or how 50.54(a) keeps it standing: ch07.

## Pin rule

RG 1.28 Rev 6, September 2023, accession ML23177A002, issued through Federal Register notice 2023-19548; pin 2026-11-08. Later revisions of RG 1.28 are out of scope. Verify any point against the guide itself before relying on it.

## Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized, not copied. Verbatim excerpts of RG 1.28 text are capped at two sentences, and this pack quotes ASME NQA-1 zero times. These notes are not legal advice and not counsel. Verify everything against the current guide text.

## QA program limits

- These notes are not a plant-specific quality assurance program and contain no program advice.
- ASME NQA-1 is named only: editions 2017, 2019, and 2022 and owner ASME; no standard text appears here.
- Appendix B criterion bodies belong to the App B pack; this pack carries the eighteen criteria as titles only.
- NEI 14-05A is a name hook only; no accreditation or survey procedure content.

## When the question is thin

Questions that need standard text, Appendix B criterion bodies, or plant-specific program content are out of scope for this pack: say so, and point the reader at the guide, the standard, and the regulation rather than improvising from these notes.
