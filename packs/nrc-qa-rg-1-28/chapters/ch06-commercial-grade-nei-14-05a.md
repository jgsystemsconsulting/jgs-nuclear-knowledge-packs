# Chapter 6: NEI 14-05A accreditation hook

Sources: NRC RG 1.28 Rev 6, Quality Assurance Program Criteria (Design and Construction), September 2023, ML23177A002 (Federal Register notice 2023-19548); pin 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

Beside the NQA-1 endorsement, Rev 6 also endorses NEI 14-05A Rev 1 (Sep 2020), on one named topic: accreditation in lieu of commercial grade surveys for laboratory calibration and test services. This chapter is a name hook. It frames the topic the endorsement reaches and nothing more; no NEI document content and no survey procedure advice appear in this pack.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Second endorsement, narrower scope. The same guide that endorses the NQA-1 requirement sets also endorses one industry document on one topic; two endorsements with different breadths ride in the same guide.
- Name-hook discipline. The pack names the document, its revision and date, and the endorsed topic; every question about how accreditation or surveys work belongs to the endorsed document and the guide's own text.

## Key Concepts

- The endorsed document: NEI 14-05A Rev 1, dated September 2020.
- The endorsed topic, in one orientation frame: accreditation in lieu of commercial grade surveys for laboratory calibration and test services.
- Reading of the topic at orientation level: for laboratory calibration and test services, accreditation is the acceptable alternative route that the endorsement names, in place of commercial grade surveys.
- What stays outside the hook: any NEI document content, any accreditation procedure, any survey procedure, and any program-specific advice.

## Mental Models

Hold it as a one-line exception list: the main endorsement carries the program-shape requirement sets, and this second endorsement carries a single substitution (accreditation instead of commercial grade surveys) for one service class (laboratory calibration and test services). The pack owns the line, not the mechanics behind it.

## Anti-patterns

Asking this pack how to run an accreditation or a survey is the named anti-pattern: the hook names the endorsed route, and the mechanics live in the endorsed document and the guide. Stretching the topic beyond laboratory calibration and test services misreads the endorsement's breadth.

## Key Takeaways

- Rev 6 endorses NEI 14-05A Rev 1 (Sep 2020) on accreditation in lieu of commercial grade surveys for laboratory calibration and test services.
- The hook is names-and-topic only in this pack.
- The second endorsement is narrower than the NQA-1 endorsement.
