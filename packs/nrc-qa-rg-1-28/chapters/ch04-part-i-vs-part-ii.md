# Chapter 4: Part I and Part II requirements shape

Sources: NRC RG 1.28 Rev 6, Quality Assurance Program Criteria (Design and Construction), September 2023, ML23177A002 (Federal Register notice 2023-19548); pin 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

What the Federal Register notice says the Rev 6 endorsement covers is the Part I and Part II requirements of the endorsed NQA-1 editions, for quality assurance programs during design and construction of nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants. This chapter is descriptive orientation on what it means for a program to adopt that endorsed standard shape; it carries no requirement content from either part.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Adopted-shape orientation. A program that follows RG 1.28 Rev 6 adopts the shape of an endorsed standard's Part I and Part II requirements, as conditioned by the guide, instead of inventing a bespoke program architecture.
- Endorsement scope as two named requirement sets. The notice names Part I and Part II as the requirement sets the endorsement reaches; this pack holds the names and the scope sentence, never the requirement text.

## Key Concepts

- The endorsement's coverage statement: Part I and Part II requirements of ASME NQA-1-2017, NQA-1-2019, and NQA-1-2022.
- The phase the coverage serves: quality assurance programs during design and construction.
- Adopting an endorsed shape means the program's structure follows requirement sets the NRC has already found acceptable, so review weight falls on conformance and on the guide's conditions rather than on justifying a novel architecture.
- What this pack does not carry: no paraphrase of any Part I or Part II requirement, no subpart names, no requirement counts. The requirement bodies live in the standard, and the guide conditions how they are met.

## Mental Models

Skeleton and furniture: the endorsed Part I and Part II requirements are the skeleton a quality assurance program hangs its own detail on, and the guide's conditions adjust the skeleton. This pack names the skeleton and points at where the conditions sit; it carries neither skeleton text nor furniture.

## Anti-patterns

Coming to this pack for requirement text is the named anti-pattern: the pack holds the endorsement's coverage statement, not the requirements it covers. Treating the Part I and Part II labels as if this pack explained their contents repeats the same error in smaller print.

## Key Takeaways

- Rev 6 endorses the Part I and Part II requirements of the three named editions, for design and construction quality assurance programs.
- A conforming program adopts the endorsed shape, conditioned by the guide.
- Requirement content is out of this pack by design.
