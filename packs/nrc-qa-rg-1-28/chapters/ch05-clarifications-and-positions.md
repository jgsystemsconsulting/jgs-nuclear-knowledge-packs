# Chapter 5: Clarifications and regulatory positions

Sources: NRC RG 1.28 Rev 6, Quality Assurance Program Criteria (Design and Construction), September 2023, ML23177A002 (Federal Register notice 2023-19548); pin 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

Rev 6 endorses the NQA-1 requirement sets with certain clarifications and regulatory positions. The mechanism matters: the guide text conditions the standard where the NRC needs more, less, or different than the standard says, so a reader must read endorsement plus conditions together. This chapter explains the mechanism only; the position bodies are in the guide itself, and this pack does not enumerate or summarize specific positions.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Conditioned endorsement. An endorsement here is not a clean handoff: the NRC accepts the standard's requirement sets and, in the same guide, states clarifications and regulatory positions that modify how they read.
- The read-together rule. Conformance is judged against the standard as conditioned by the guide, so reading either piece alone misstates the acceptable path.

## Key Concepts

- Clarification: a guide statement that resolves how an endorsed requirement reads, issued by the NRC alongside the endorsement.
- Regulatory position: a guide statement stating the NRC's position where it needs more, less, or different than the endorsed text provides.
- The conditions live in RG 1.28 Rev 6's own text; this pack names the mechanism and holds none of the position bodies.
- Why the mechanism exists: the endorsement lets the guide stay short, pointing at a consensus standard, while keeping NRC control over the points where the standard alone would not state the acceptable path.

## Mental Models

Endorsement with amendments: read the endorsement as accepting a base document (the standard's requirement sets) together with a set of modifications (the clarifications and regulatory positions) published alongside it. The base plus the modifications, read together, is the acceptable path; the base alone is not.

## Anti-patterns

Adopting the standard without the guide's conditions is the named anti-pattern: the endorsement is conditioned, and conditions-first reading is required. Expecting this pack to list the positions is the companion error: the pack says the positions exist and where they live, and stops there.

## Key Takeaways

- Rev 6's endorsement carries clarifications and regulatory positions in the guide text itself.
- Endorsement plus conditions, read together, state the acceptable path.
- No position bodies are in this pack.
