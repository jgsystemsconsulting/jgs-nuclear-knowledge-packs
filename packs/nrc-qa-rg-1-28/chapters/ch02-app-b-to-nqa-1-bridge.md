# Chapter 2: Appendix B criteria to NQA-1 bridge

Sources: NRC RG 1.28 Rev 6, Quality Assurance Program Criteria (Design and Construction), September 2023, ML23177A002 (Federal Register notice 2023-19548); pin 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

10 CFR 50 Appendix B states eighteen quality assurance criteria, and RG 1.28 Rev 6 is the guide that serves those criteria for design and construction. The eighteen 10 CFR 50 Appendix B criteria are named here as an orientation list; the criterion bodies belong to the App B pack. The bridge this chapter states stays at orientation level: NQA-1 Part I requirements are the endorsed vehicle that maps onto this criteria shape, and the mapping details live in the guide and the standard, not in this pack.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Orientation-list discipline. This pack names the eighteen criteria by roman numeral and title only; no criterion body text and no per-criterion requirement summary appear anywhere in it.
- The bridge. The endorsed standard's Part I requirements are the vehicle that maps onto the Appendix B criteria shape; the guide carries the mapping and the standard carries the requirement text.

## Key Concepts

- The eighteen criteria, roman numeral and title only: I Organization, II Quality Assurance Program, III Design Control, IV Procurement Document Control, V Instructions, Procedures, and Drawings, VI Document Control, VII Control of Purchased Material, Equipment, and Services, VIII Identification and Control of Materials, Parts, and Components, IX Control of Special Processes, X Inspection, XI Test Control, XII Control of Measuring and Test Equipment, XIII Handling, Storage, and Shipping, XIV Inspection, Test, and Operating Status, XV Nonconforming Materials, Parts, or Components, XVI Corrective Action, XVII Quality Assurance Records, XVIII Audits.
- The criteria shape is Appendix B's; the endorsed vehicle is ASME NQA-1, whose Part I requirements RG 1.28 Rev 6 endorses for the design and construction phase.
- The mapping from requirement to criterion is guide-and-standard material: this pack holds the names and the relationship, nothing deeper.

## Mental Models

Hold the bridge as shape and vehicle: Appendix B supplies the criteria shape (eighteen named headings), and the endorsed standard supplies the vehicle whose Part I requirements fill that shape. The pack carries the shape and names the vehicle; it never carries the fill.

## Anti-patterns

Do not quote or paraphrase any of the eighteen criteria from this pack: the names are orientation, the bodies belong to the App B pack. Do not treat the list as coverage: knowing criterion titles is not knowing what any criterion requires.

## Key Takeaways

- Appendix B has eighteen criteria, from Organization through Audits, named here titles-only.
- NQA-1 Part I requirements are the endorsed vehicle that maps onto that criteria shape.
- Mapping details and criterion bodies are out of this pack by design.
