# Chapter 1: RG 1.28 role and the Appendix B relation

Sources: NRC RG 1.28 Rev 6, Quality Assurance Program Criteria (Design and Construction), September 2023, ML23177A002 (Federal Register notice 2023-19548); pin 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

RG 1.28, Quality Assurance Program Criteria (Design and Construction), is Nuclear Regulatory Commission guidance describing an acceptable way to meet quality assurance program expectations for the design and construction of nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants. The guide is not the binding layer: 10 CFR 50 Appendix B holds the quality assurance criteria, and the guide works by endorsing a consensus standard, with conditions. This chapter fixes that layered picture before the rest of the pack builds on it.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Regulation, guide, standard. The regulatory guide series describes methods the NRC staff considers acceptable for implementing the agency's regulations; the guide is not the regulation. For quality assurance, the binding layer is 10 CFR 50 Appendix B, the acceptable-means layer is this guide, and the endorsed consensus standard is the vehicle the guide accepts.
- Endorsement with conditions. Rev 6 does not merely point at a standard: it endorses named requirement sets of that standard with certain clarifications and regulatory positions. Chapters 3 to 5 carry that mechanism.

## Key Concepts

- The pin: RG 1.28 Rev 6, September 2023, accession ML23177A002, issued through Federal Register notice 2023-19548 (88 FR 62292, docket NRC-2023-0054). The proposed version that became Rev 6 was DG-1403.
- Phase scope: quality assurance programs during design and construction of nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants.
- The guide-versus-regulation distinction: regulatory guides describe methods the NRC staff considers acceptable for implementing regulations; the regulation itself, here 10 CFR 50 Appendix B, is what binds.
- 10 CFR Parts 50 and 52 are the Code of Federal Regulations parts the guide pairs with; Appendix B holds the quality assurance criteria the guide serves.
- The endorsement target is ASME NQA-1 (three editions, named in ch03); the conditions that shape the endorsement are named clarifications and regulatory positions (ch05).

## Mental Models

Read quality assurance for design and construction as three layers: the regulation (10 CFR 50 Appendix B) binds, the guide (RG 1.28 Rev 6) describes one acceptable path through it, and the consensus standard (ASME NQA-1) supplies the requirement shape the guide endorses. A question is located by naming which layer it touches.

## Anti-patterns

Treating the guide as the regulation is the chapter's named anti-pattern: an applicant is bound by Appendix B, not by RG 1.28. A companion error is reading the endorsement as unconditioned; the clarifications and regulatory positions are part of what the guide accepts.

## Key Takeaways

- RG 1.28 Rev 6 is guidance: an acceptable way to meet Appendix B quality assurance expectations for design and construction.
- The binding layer is 10 CFR 50 Appendix B; the guide endorses a consensus standard with conditions.
- The guide's scope covers nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants in the design and construction phase.
