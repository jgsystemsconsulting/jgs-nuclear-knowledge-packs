# Chapter 3: NQA-1 edition pathway

Sources: NRC RG 1.28 Rev 6, Quality Assurance Program Criteria (Design and Construction), September 2023, ML23177A002 (Federal Register notice 2023-19548); pin 2026-11-08.

## Core Idea

RG 1.28 Rev 6 endorses ASME NQA-1-2017, NQA-1-2019, and NQA-1-2022 (Part I and Part II requirements); the standard text itself is not packaged here. Endorsing three editions means each is an acceptable path: an applicant picks an endorsed edition path rather than the newest by default. This chapter is signpost-only; it names the editions and the owner, and carries no content from any edition.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Edition-path selection. Three editions are endorsed, so the choice among them is a pathway choice for the applicant, not a race to the latest publication year.
- Signpost discipline. The pack names the standard, its editions, and its owner (ASME); every question about what the standard requires belongs to the standard itself.

## Key Concepts

- The endorsed standard is ASME NQA-1, owned and published by ASME (the American Society of Mechanical Engineers).
- The endorsed editions are NQA-1-2017, NQA-1-2019, and NQA-1-2022; each one's Part I and Part II requirements are within what Rev 6 endorses.
- The endorsement covers quality assurance programs during design and construction of nuclear power plants and fuel reprocessing plants.
- Endorsement semantics: an endorsed edition is an acceptable path; the newest edition is not automatically the required one.
- The standard's text is copyrighted by ASME and is never reproduced in this pack; the standard is named with edition and owner only.

## Mental Models

Picture the endorsed editions as a menu of acceptable paths rather than a single mandated version: the guide's endorsement admits 2017, 2019, and 2022, and the applicant's program declares which path it follows. Conditions attach to the endorsement either way, and ch05 carries those.

## Anti-patterns

Assuming the newest edition is the required edition is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the endorsement names three acceptable editions, not a latest-edition mandate. A companion error is asking this pack for standard text; the pack's NQA-1 content is names, editions, and owner only.

## Key Takeaways

- ASME NQA-1-2017, NQA-1-2019, and NQA-1-2022 are each an endorsed pathway under RG 1.28 Rev 6.
- An applicant picks an endorsed edition path; newest-by-default is not the rule.
- No edition's text lives in this pack.
