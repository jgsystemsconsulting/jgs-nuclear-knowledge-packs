# Chapter 1: NUREG-0700 Scope, Purpose, and the HFE Review Process

Sources: NRC NUREG-0700 Revision 4, Human-System Interface Design Review Guidelines, January 2026, ML26022A094; pin verified via official NRC publication page on 2026-11-13. NUREG-0800 SRP Chapter 18 and NUREG-0711 Rev 3 named relationship depth only, not sources.

## Core Idea

NUREG-0700 Revision 4 (January 2026, ML26022A094; manuscript completed August 2025) provides human-system interface design review guidelines used in the NRC staff human factors engineering review of control rooms and other HSIs. The guidelines are review guidance, not regulation, and their text binds no licensee through this pack. This chapter fixes where NUREG-0700 sits inside the staff HFE review, what a design review guideline is, who uses the document, and the four-part architecture this pack mirrors.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Review frame, three named parts. NUREG-0800 SRP Chapter 18 frames the staff HFE review, NUREG-0711 Rev 3 (the HFE Program Review Model) structures the program-level review, and NUREG-0700 supplies design review guidelines for evaluating the detailed HSI design within that review. SRP Chapter 18 and NUREG-0711 frame the HFE review that uses these guidelines; this pack states the relationship and does not carry review criteria.
- Design review guideline as review aid. A design review guideline states one review consideration an HSI design is checked against; it orients staff judgment and does not itself approve or reject any design. This pack teaches the shape of that checking, not the checks.
- Staff reviewer as primary user. NRC staff use the guidelines when reviewing applicant and licensee HSI designs; applicants and licensees read at orientation depth to understand how staff frame the review. No party gains design instruction or a licensing position from this pack.
- Four-part architecture as pack map. The guideline set is organized in four parts by review object: basic HSI elements, HSI systems, workstation and workplace design, and further design topics including maintainability, degraded conditions, and integration. This pack carries structure only at that four-part dispatch level. Prior revisions exist; this pack states no older-revision structure, count, or authorship as a Revision 4 fact.

## Key Concepts

- Pin facts: NUREG-0700 Revision 4, January 2026, ADAMS ML26022A094, manuscript completed August 2025; J. O'Hara (BNL), S. Fleger (NRC), N. Hughes Green (NRC), and S. Morrow (NRC). Guideline body not captured here.
- Human-system interface: the displays, controls, alarms, procedures, and workplace through which operators monitor and control the plant; the review object of the whole guideline set.
- Human factors engineering review: the staff review discipline that checks human performance is supported by design; NUREG-0700 serves inside it, and this pack states the relationship only.
- Design review guideline: one review consideration applied to an HSI design; guidance for reviewers, not a requirement on any licensee.
- Control room and other HSIs: the main control room plus local control stations and other interfaces where operators act; the guideline set covers the full set, and later chapters dispatch each review object.
- Review frame parties: SRP Chapter 18 names the staff review, NUREG-0711 Rev 3 names the program-level model, NUREG-0700 names the detailed design guidelines; no criterion or element from either framing document is restated here.
- Pack map: ch01 scope, purpose, and review frame; ch02 basic HSI elements; ch03 control and automation systems including alarms and soft controls; ch04 computer-based procedures and communications; ch05 workstations, local control stations, and workplace design; ch06 maintainability, degraded HSI conditions, and integration; ch07 cross-pack interfacing, accident monitoring boundary, and limits.

## Mental Models

Read NUREG-0700 as staff review guidance that sits under a framed HFE review, not as a standalone design standard. Keep the three frame documents in role: SRP Chapter 18 frames, NUREG-0711 structures the program review, NUREG-0700 details the design checks. Keep the four parts as review objects, not design phases; a reviewer moves across parts when one operator task touches elements, systems, workplace, and degraded conditions at once.

## Anti-patterns

Treating these orientation notes as a substitute for the guideline document is this chapter's named anti-pattern: notes locate scope, pin, review frame, and part architecture, while the guideline document carries review detail. A companion error imports a review procedure step, a Chapter 18 acceptance criterion, or a program-review element from the framing documents and treats it as packaged here; the frame is named only. A third error reads an older-revision structure, count, or authorship claim into Revision 4; prior revisions are acknowledged and no fact about them ships as a Revision 4 fact.

## Key Takeaways

- NUREG-0700 Revision 4 (January 2026, ML26022A094) provides HSI design review guidelines for the NRC staff HFE review of control rooms and other HSIs; review guidance, not regulation.
- SRP Chapter 18 frames the staff HFE review, NUREG-0711 Rev 3 structures the program-level review, and NUREG-0700 supplies the detailed design guidelines; relationship only, no criteria carried.
- A design review guideline is one review consideration checked against an HSI design; staff reviewers are the primary users.
- The set is organized in four parts by review object, and this pack mirrors that architecture at dispatch level only.
- This pack orients scope and frame only; the guideline body is not captured.
