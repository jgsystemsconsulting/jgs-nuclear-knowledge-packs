# Cheatsheet: nrc-hsi-nureg-0700

Decision rules on which chapter owns which question, pin rule, no-numbers and no-guideline-items rule, accident monitoring boundary, thin classes, honest fallback, and not-advice limits.

## Which chapter owns which question

- NUREG-0700 scope, purpose, HFE review process, pin facts, and pack map: ch01.
- Basic HSI elements, display design review principles, and interaction: ch02.
- Control and automation systems including alarms, soft controls, SPDS concept, group-view displays, and mode awareness: ch03.
- Computer-based procedures and communication systems: ch04.
- Workstations, local control stations, and workplace design: ch05.
- Maintainability, degraded HSI conditions, and integration of HSI resources as a crew whole: ch06.
- Cross-pack interfacing, accident monitoring boundary, and pack limits: ch07.
- Term definitions at orientation depth with chapter pointers: glossary.md.

## Pin rule

Pack pins NUREG-0700 Revision 4, January 2026, ML26022A094, verified via the official NRC publication page on 2026-11-13. Manuscript completed August 2025. Guideline body not captured. Later revisions out of scope.

## No-numbers and no-guideline-items rule

No guideline item text, item numbers, figures, tables, anthropometric tables, or any numeric design value is packaged here: no display values, reach or viewing dimensions, lighting or acoustic values, alarm setpoints, or procedure wording. Concepts ship at orientation depth only. The guideline document itself is the source for any design or review decision.

## RG 1.97 boundary

The live accident monitoring pack carries the Regulatory Guide 1.97 variable selection and display criteria; this pack covers the HSI presentation and interaction design review of displays, alarms, and controls only, and does not restate variable criteria. Digital I&C platform questions, cyber security program questions, and post-fire safe shutdown capability are neighbour-owned and named only.

## Three thin classes

1. Guideline level: guideline item text, item numbers, figures, tables, or any numeric design value.
2. Plant or design specific: HSI verdicts for a named design, control room evaluation, staffing or workload analysis, or procedure or alarm design for a real plant.
3. Neighbour-owned material: accident monitoring criteria, digital I&C design questions, cyber security controls, fire protection or safe shutdown analysis, or HFE program review method.

## Honest fallback

This knowledge pack provides high-level orientation on human-system interface design review guidelines under NUREG-0700 Revision 4; it does not reproduce guideline text, evaluate plant-specific designs, or provide human factors engineering advice. For guideline text, consult NUREG-0700; for plant-specific HFE licensing or review, consult licensed engineering personnel.

## Not advice

Synthesized orientation notes keyed to the recorded pin and closed statement list; zero quotations. Pack is not the guideline document, not NUREG-0711, not SRP Chapter 18, not regulation, and not legal, licensing, HFE review, or design advice. Advice requests are refused.
