# Glossary: nrc-cyber-rg-5-71

Synthesized orientation definitions keyed to this pack's RG 5.71 Rev. 1 and 10 CFR 73.54 facts, alphabetical, not substitute source text.

- **10 CFR 73.54**: the Part 73 section titled Protection of digital computer and communication systems and networks. It requires licensees to protect digital computer and communication systems and networks associated with safety-related and important-to-safety functions from cyber attacks. Pin: eCFR pin-check 2026-11-09; title 10 latest amendment 2026-11-03; section unchanged since 2017-01-01. (ch02)
- **cyber security program**: the program RG 5.71 describes a method for: graded, defense-in-depth, with defensive controls named as categories and lifecycle elements running from establishment through reassessment. This pack carries the shape, not the method detail. (ch03)
- **defensive architecture**: the guide's description of how a program's protections are arranged: mutually reinforcing defensive layers and defensive controls across the systems and networks the regulation covers. A framework description, never a site design. (ch04)
- **defensive controls**: the controls a program's architecture is built from, named in this pack as categories only; no products, configurations, detection logic, or tuning appear here. (ch04)
- **defense-in-depth**: the approach the cyber security program uses, per the guide's framework: layered protection rather than a single barrier. This pack states the approach and no design. (ch03)
- **digital computer and communication systems and networks**: the assets 10 CFR 73.54 protects, when associated with safety-related and important-to-safety functions. Named here at orientation level only. (ch02)
- **graded approach**: the word the guide's framework uses for how the program is graded. Stated here as the shape word only; no scheme detail is carried. (ch03)
- **Part 73 shape**: the frame in which 73.54 and RG 5.71 sit for civil power reactors, with physical protection and other Part 73 areas as neighbouring topics outside this pack's body. (ch07)
- **PDD criteria**: criteria covered by RG 1.152 Rev 4 alongside SDOE. The SDOE body lives in the live nrc-nuclear pack, named in prose; no criteria content is packaged here. (ch05)
- **program lifecycle**: the element chain of establishing, implementing, monitoring, sustaining, and reassessing the program, sustained and reassessed over the plant life. A framework statement, no procedure. (ch06)
- **program reassessment**: the lifecycle element in which the program is reassessed, continuing over the plant life; named as an element without schedule or procedure. (ch06)
- **RG 1.152**: NRC Regulatory Guide 1.152, Rev 4, July 2023, accession ML23054A463, covering PDD criteria and SDOE. It does not address control-of-access features meant to stop malicious cyberattacks. Its SDOE body sits in the live nrc-nuclear pack, named in prose. (ch05)
- **RG 5.71**: NRC Regulatory Guide 5.71, Cyber Security Programs for Nuclear Power Reactors, Rev. 1, February 2023, accession ML22258A204; Rev 1 current as of the 2026-11-08 verify, Rev 0 January 2010, ML090340159. It describes a method NRC staff consider acceptable; not a substitute for the regulation. (ch01)
- **SDOE**: the secure development and operating environment covered by RG 1.152 Rev 4 (July 2023, ML23054A463). The SDOE body sits in the live nrc-nuclear pack, named in prose; this pack carries only the fence between SDOE and cyber security. (ch05)
- **SDOE fence**: the boundary between RG 1.152's SDOE coverage and malicious-cyberattack protection under 10 CFR 73.54 and RG 5.71; each question belongs to one side. (ch05)
- **staff review**: the framework-level fact that the program is documented and subject to NRC staff review. No review procedure, checklist, or schedule is carried here. (ch07)
