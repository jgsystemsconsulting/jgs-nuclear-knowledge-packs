# Cheatsheet: nrc-cyber-rg-5-71

Decision rules for which chapter owns a question, the pin rule, the not-advice rule, the sensitive-content limits, and where questions run past this pack.

## Which chapter owns the question

- What RG 5.71 is, its pin, its revision history, or its relation to the live nrc-nuclear SDOE pack: ch01.
- What 10 CFR 73.54 requires and its eCFR pin: ch02.
- The program's graded, defense-in-depth shape and its category-level controls: ch03.
- The defensive architecture of mutually reinforcing layers and controls: ch04.
- Which side of the SDOE fence a question belongs to: ch05.
- The program lifecycle, sustained and reassessed over the plant life: ch06.
- The Part 73 frame, documentation, and staff review at framework level: ch07.

## Pin rule

RG 5.71 Rev. 1, February 2023, accession ML22258A204; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared; Rev 1 current, Rev 0 January 2010, ML090340159). 10 CFR 73.54 eCFR pin-check 2026-11-09; title 10 latest amendment 2026-11-03; section unchanged since 2017-01-01. Later revisions of the guide are out of scope. Verify any point against the guide and the regulation before relying on it.

## Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized, not copied: this pack quotes nothing, and verbatim from eCFR text is capped at two sentences by licence policy. These notes are not legal advice and are not a plant-specific cyber security program. Verify everything against the current guide text and the regulation text.

## Sensitive-content limits

- No security-control implementation guidance; program controls are named as categories only, never products, configurations, detection logic, or tuning.
- No threat-actor or tactic detail.
- No DBT references and no force-on-force tactics beyond public RG and regulation text.
- G-02 and G-03 are named only as idea-stage neighbours; nothing is packaged from them.

## When the question is thin

Questions that need control detail, site program specifics, tactic detail, or compliance advice for a named plant are out of scope for this pack: say so, and point the reader at RG 5.71 Rev. 1 (ML22258A204) and 10 CFR 73.54 rather than improvising from these notes.
