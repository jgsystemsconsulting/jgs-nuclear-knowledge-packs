# Chapter 6: Program lifecycle and periodic review

Sources: NRC RG 5.71 Rev. 1, Cyber Security Programs for Nuclear Power Reactors, February 2023, ML22258A204; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared).

## Core Idea

The cyber security program is a running program, not a one-time build. At framework level: establishing, implementing, monitoring, sustaining, and reassessing the program are its lifecycle elements, sustained and reassessed over the plant life. Change and emerging-threat handling are lifecycle concerns named without tactic or threat-actor detail; the words locate the topics and stop there.

## Frameworks Introduced

- The lifecycle element chain. Establish, implement, monitor, sustain, reassess: the program's elements in their frame, continuing across the plant's operating life.
- Change and emerging threats as lifecycle concerns. Handling change and emerging threats belongs to the running program; this pack names the concerns without any tactic or threat-actor detail.

## Key Concepts

- Program lifecycle: the element chain from establishing the program through its sustained operation and reassessment.
- Sustained and reassessed over the plant life: the time frame the lifecycle elements run across.
- Monitoring: one of the lifecycle elements; named as an element, with no procedure attached here.
- Change and emerging-threat handling: lifecycle concerns, named without tactic or threat-actor detail.

## Mental Models

Read the lifecycle as a loop, not a checklist item: the program is established and implemented, then monitored, sustained, and reassessed over the plant life, and the loop continues. The loop is the framework statement; no schedule, no procedure, and no site programme step lives in this pack.

## Anti-patterns

Reading this chapter as a lifecycle procedure or a review schedule is the chapter's named anti-pattern. No lifecycle procedure, no review schedule, and no site programme advice appears here; the pack carries the framework statement about the elements and their time frame, nothing beyond it.

## Key Takeaways

- Establishing, implementing, monitoring, sustaining, and reassessing the program are its lifecycle elements.
- The program is sustained and reassessed over the plant life.
- Change and emerging-threat handling are lifecycle concerns, named here without tactic or threat-actor detail.
- The guide's own text carries any procedure detail; these notes carry none.
