# Chapter 7: The Part 73 shape and staff review

Sources: NRC RG 5.71 Rev. 1, Cyber Security Programs for Nuclear Power Reactors, February 2023, ML22258A204; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared); 10 CFR Part 73 (eCFR), section 73.54; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-09 (title 10 latest amendment 2026-11-03; section unchanged since 2017-01-01).

## Core Idea

RG 5.71 covers civil power-reactor cyber security programs. Within that frame, the guide describes a method NRC staff consider acceptable for a cyber security program under 10 CFR 73.54; the guide is not a substitute for the regulation. The program is documented and subject to NRC staff review at framework level, and physical protection and other Part 73 areas are neighbouring topics, not this pack's body. Physical protection and other Part 73 topics are separate idea-stage neighbours; their requirements are not packaged here.

## Frameworks Introduced

- The regulatory frame for civil power reactors. 73.54 carries the protection requirement and RG 5.71 describes an acceptable method for the program; both sit inside the Part 73 world at orientation level.
- Documentation and staff review as framework concepts. The program is documented and subject to NRC staff review; this pack frames both at framework level and carries no review procedure.

## Key Concepts

- Civil power-reactor scope: RG 5.71 covers cyber security programs for civil power reactors; the pack's body stops at that scope.
- Documentation: the program is documented; a framework-level concept in this pack, with no document contents stated.
- NRC staff review: the program is subject to staff review at framework level; no review procedure, checklist, or schedule appears here.
