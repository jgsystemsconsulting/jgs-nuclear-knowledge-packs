# Chapter 2: 10 CFR 73.54, the regulation

Sources: 10 CFR Part 73 (eCFR), section 73.54; eCFR pin-check 2026-11-09 (title 10 latest amendment 2026-11-03; section unchanged since 2017-01-01).

## Core Idea

10 CFR 73.54, titled Protection of digital computer and communication systems and networks, is the regulation under this guide. At orientation level, from public eCFR text: 10 CFR 73.54 requires licensees to protect digital computer and communication systems and networks associated with safety-related and important-to-safety functions from cyber attacks. That is the protection goal stated at framework level; the regulation's own text, not these notes, carries its full requirements.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Regulation first, guide second. The requirement to protect sits in 10 CFR 73.54; RG 5.71 describes a method NRC staff consider acceptable for a cyber security program that meets it. The guide is not a substitute for the regulation.
- The named-out boundary. Program specifics for a named site are beyond public text and beyond this pack. The regulation and the guide are public; a site's own program is not.

## Key Concepts

- Section title: Protection of digital computer and communication systems and networks.
- Protection scope at orientation level: digital computer and communication systems and networks associated with safety-related and important-to-safety functions, protected from cyber attacks.
- eCFR pin: pin-check 2026-11-09; title 10 latest amendment date 2026-11-03; section 73.54 unchanged since 2017-01-01. A part-73 view snapshot dated 2026-06-16 lags the title-level meta; the cited pin is the title-level 2026-11-03.
- What this chapter does not carry: no control detail, no program specifics for any named site.

## Mental Models

Read 73.54 as the what and RG 5.71 as one described how: the regulation states what licensees protect and from what, and the guide describes an acceptable method for the program. The protection goal is stated here without control detail; controls belong to the program, and the program for a real site belongs to the site.

## Anti-patterns

Reading this orientation as legal advice or as a plant-specific cyber security program is the chapter's named anti-pattern. These notes frame the regulation; they carry none of its text, no compliance judgement, and no program specifics for any named site, which are beyond public text and beyond this pack.
