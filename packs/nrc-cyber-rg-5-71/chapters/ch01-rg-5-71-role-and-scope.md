# Chapter 1: RG 5.71 role and scope

Sources: NRC RG 5.71 Rev. 1, Cyber Security Programs for Nuclear Power Reactors, February 2023, ML22258A204; pin 2026-11-08 (P9 VERIFY cleared).

## Core Idea

RG 5.71 is Nuclear Regulatory Commission guidance titled Cyber Security Programs for Nuclear Power Reactors. Rev. 1 (February 2023, accession ML22258A204) is the current revision as of the 2026-11-08 verify, and it covers cyber security programs for nuclear power reactors. RG 5.71 describes a method NRC staff consider acceptable for meeting the regulation; it does not replace 10 CFR 73.54. This pack orients the guide's framework; the guide itself, not these notes, is what a program reads against.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Orientation, not the guide. The pack carries synthesized notes on what the guide covers and how its subject is framed: a graded, defense-in-depth cyber security program under 10 CFR 73.54. The guide's own text stays in the guide.
- The two-document shape. The regulation (10 CFR 73.54) states what licensees protect; the guide describes a method the staff consider acceptable for a cyber security program. Neither document is reproduced here, and these notes are a substitute for neither.

## Key Concepts

- The pin: RG 5.71 Rev. 1, February 2023, accession ML22258A204. Rev 1 is current as of the 2026-11-08 verify; the original Rev 0, January 2010, carried accession ML090340159.
- Subject scope: cyber security programs for nuclear power reactors.
- Guide character: RG 5.71 describes a method NRC staff consider acceptable for a cyber security program; the guide is not a substitute for the regulation.
- Relation to the SDOE side: the live nrc-nuclear pack carries RG 1.152 criteria and the secure development and operating environment; this pack carries the malicious-cyberattack side of that fence (ch05).

## Mental Models

Read the guide as a description of an acceptable method, not as the requirement itself: the regulation requires protection, and the guide describes one way a cyber security program can be shaped. Anything that looks like program detail belongs to the guide and to the regulation, which this pack does not reproduce.

## Anti-patterns

Reading these orientation notes as a substitute for the guide or the regulation is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the notes locate and frame, the sources decide. A companion error is treating a later revision of the guide as covered; this pack pins Rev. 1 and later revisions are out of scope.

## Key Takeaways

- Rev 1 is current as of the 2026-11-08 verify; Rev 0 was January 2010, ML090340159.
- The guide describes a method the staff consider acceptable; 10 CFR 73.54 carries the requirement, and this pack reproduces neither document.
