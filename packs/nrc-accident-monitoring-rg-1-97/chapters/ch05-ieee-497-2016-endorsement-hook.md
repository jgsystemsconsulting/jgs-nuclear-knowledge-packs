# Chapter 5: IEEE 497-2016 endorsement hook

Sources: NRC RG 1.97 Rev. 5, Criteria for Accident Monitoring Instrumentation for Nuclear Power Plants, May 2019, ML18136A762 (Federal Register notice 2019-08819, 84 FR 18591); pin 2026-11-09 (dual-source verified).

## Core Idea

Rev. 5 endorses IEEE Std 497-2016 with exceptions and clarifications, and the wording of those exceptions and clarifications lives in the guide. IEEE Std 497-2016 is the standard RG 1.97 Rev. 5 endorses with exceptions and clarifications; its text is not packaged here. The standard is named only in this pack: designation and edition, no clause text, no tables, no criteria, and no close paraphrase.

## Frameworks Introduced

- The endorsement hook. A regulatory guide can endorse a consensus standard as its method statement. RG 1.97 Rev. 5 does this for IEEE Std 497-2016, with exceptions and clarifications recorded in the guide. The endorsement fact is a pack fact; the standard's content is not.
- The named-only boundary. IEEE Std 497-2016 is copyrighted by IEEE. This pack carries the designation, the edition, and the endorsement fact, and nothing from inside the standard. The repository's signpost pack carries no IEEE 497 row today, so no signpost link is implied by this pack.

## Key Concepts

- IEEE Std 497-2016: the standard RG 1.97 Rev. 5 endorses with exceptions and clarifications; IEEE copyright; designation and edition only in this pack.
- Exceptions and clarifications: the guide's own wording that adapts the endorsed standard; that wording lives in the guide.
- The text boundary: the standard's text is not packaged here, in this pack or in any sibling surface this build touches.

## Mental Models

Read the endorsement as a two-document system: the guide says which standard it endorses and records its exceptions and clarifications, and the standard holds the detailed criteria. Both documents sit outside this pack. A question about a criterion in the standard routes to the standard itself and to the guide's exceptions wording, never to these notes.

## Anti-patterns

Paraphrasing standard content instead of naming the standard is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the designation and the endorsement fact are pack facts, the clauses are not. A companion error is implying a signpost link to the standard; the signpost pack carries no IEEE 497 row today, and this pack implies none.

## Key Takeaways

- Rev. 5 endorses IEEE Std 497-2016 with exceptions and clarifications; the wording lives in the guide.
- IEEE Std 497-2016 is named only in this pack; its text is not packaged here.
- Questions about the standard's content route to the standard and the guide, not to these notes.
