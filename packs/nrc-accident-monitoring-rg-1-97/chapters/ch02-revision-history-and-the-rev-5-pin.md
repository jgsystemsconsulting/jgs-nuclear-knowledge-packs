# Chapter 2: Revision history and the Rev. 5 pin

Sources: NRC RG 1.97 Rev. 5, Criteria for Accident Monitoring Instrumentation for Nuclear Power Plants, May 2019, ML18136A762 (Federal Register notice 2019-08819, 84 FR 18591); pin 2026-11-09 (dual-source verified).

## Core Idea

The revision history at pin level is short. RG 1.97 Rev. 5 (May 2019, accession ML18136A762) is the current revision, noticed through Federal Register notice 2019-08819 (84 FR 18591, May 1, 2019). DG-1335 (accession ML17083A134) was the proposal, and it was issued as final RG 1.97 Rev. 5 in May 2019. Rev. 4 (June 2006) is the predecessor. No post-2019 superseding revision was found in the pin pass behind this pack.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Proposal to final. DG-1335 was the proposal stage of the document that issued as final Rev. 5; the guide's current revision carries the final designation, not the draft one.
- Provenance correction. An older planning note described this guide as sitting on a draft route with a proposed revision pending. That provenance was stale: the proposal had already issued as final Rev. 5 in May 2019, and the note was corrected at build. This pack carries the corrected state.

## Key Concepts

- Rev. 5 pin: May 2019, ML18136A762; Federal Register notice 2019-08819, 84 FR 18591; dual-source verified 2026-11-09.
- DG-1335 (ML17083A134): the proposal that issued as final Rev. 5.
- Rev. 4 (June 2006): the predecessor revision.
- Older-revision usage: older revisions used Type and Category variable schemes tied to plant licensing bases.
- Many operating plants reference an older RG 1.97 revision in the current licensing basis; read the governing revision from the plant record.

## Mental Models

Sort revision questions by document role: the proposal (DG-1335) explains intent, the final guide (Rev. 5) carries the criteria, and the predecessor (Rev. 4) still lives in some plants' licensing records. The revision that governs a given plant is a licensing-record fact, not a history fact; history says what was issued, the plant record says what binds.

## Anti-patterns

Presenting Rev. 5 as proposed or pending is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the proposal issued as final in May 2019. A companion error is reading the revision history as a ruling on which revision a named plant follows; that ruling comes from the plant's licensing record (ch06).

## Key Takeaways

- Rev. 5 (May 2019, ML18136A762) is the current revision; Rev. 4 (June 2006) is the predecessor.
- DG-1335 (ML17083A134) was the proposal and issued as final Rev. 5.
- Many operating plants still reference an older revision; the governing revision comes from the plant record.
