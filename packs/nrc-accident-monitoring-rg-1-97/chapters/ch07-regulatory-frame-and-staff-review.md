# Chapter 7: Regulatory frame and staff review

Sources: NRC RG 1.97 Rev. 5, Criteria for Accident Monitoring Instrumentation for Nuclear Power Plants, May 2019, ML18136A762 (Federal Register notice 2019-08819, 84 FR 18591); pin 2026-11-09 (dual-source verified).

## Core Idea

RG 1.97 presents criteria for accident monitoring instrumentation, and it is an orientation entry point here, not a replacement for the guide text or a plant licensing record. In the regulatory frame, the guide sits as NRC staff guidance for civil power reactors: it states criteria the staff consider for accident monitoring instrumentation, and questions about specific variables, ranges, categories, qualifications, displays, or design detail are directed to RG 1.97 Rev. 5 and to IEEE Std 497-2016 as named sources. The pack does not answer them.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Guidance, not requirement text. RG 1.97 presents criteria; it is staff guidance that reviewers and licensees read against the plant's own record. This pack frames where the guide sits and carries no acceptance criteria and no review checklist.
- Where questions go. A question with a variable, a range, a category, a qualification, a display, or a design detail in it routes to the guide and the endorsed standard. IEEE Std 497-2016 is the standard RG 1.97 Rev. 5 endorses with exceptions and clarifications; its text is not packaged here. Human-system interface design guidance sits in the neighbouring NUREG-0700 domain; that guidance is not packaged here.

## Key Concepts

- Regulatory frame: RG 1.97 Rev. 5 as NRC staff guidance for accident monitoring instrumentation criteria at civil power reactors, framed at orientation level only.
- Staff review framing: review reads the guide's criteria against a plant's record; this pack states that frame and carries no acceptance criteria.
- Named sources for detail questions: RG 1.97 Rev. 5 (ML18136A762) and IEEE Std 497-2016, named only.
- NUREG-0700: the neighbouring human-system interface domain, named only, not built, and never linked.

## Mental Models

Read the frame as a routing table: orientation notes route, the guide and the endorsed standard answer, and the plant licensing record rules on which revision binds. Anything that would need acceptance criteria, qualification values, or design detail is out of these notes and into the named sources.

## Anti-patterns

Reading staff-review framing as acceptance criteria is the chapter's named anti-pattern: the pack states where review reads from and carries no criteria of its own. A companion error is pulling display or interface questions into this pack; those sit in the neighbouring NUREG-0700 domain, which is not built and is not linked.

## Key Takeaways

- RG 1.97 Rev. 5 sits as NRC staff guidance presenting criteria for accident monitoring instrumentation.
- Questions about specific variables, categories, or design detail are directed to RG 1.97 Rev. 5 and to IEEE Std 497-2016 as named sources.
- The pack carries no acceptance criteria, no HSI design guidance, and no instrumentation design how-to.
