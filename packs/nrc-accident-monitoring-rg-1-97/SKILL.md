## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Accident monitoring at framework level

Accident monitoring instrumentation provides the control-room operator information about plant conditions during and after accidents, and RG 1.97 presents criteria for that instrumentation. The guide frames which variables are monitored, the criteria used to select them, design criteria, and a categorization of monitored variables; Rev. 5 adds severe-accident-management-guideline variables. Variable lists, category criteria, and design detail live in RG 1.97 Rev. 5 and the endorsed standard; they are not reproduced here.

### Which revision governs

Rev. 5 is technology-neutral and extends applicability language for 10 CFR Part 52, but many operating plants reference an older RG 1.97 revision in the current licensing basis. The revision that governs a named plant is read from that plant's licensing record, not assumed to be Rev. 5; an older reference stands until the licensing record changes.

### Pin rule

This pack pins RG 1.97 Rev. 5, May 2019, accession ML18136A762, Federal Register notice 2019-08819, 84 FR 18591, pin date 2026-11-09, dual-source verified. DG-1335 (ML17083A134) was the proposal and was issued as final Rev. 5 in May 2019; Rev. 5 is the current revision and Rev. 4 (June 2006) is the predecessor. No post-2019 superseding revision was found in the pin pass. The guide and standard texts, not this pack, state the criteria themselves.

### Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized. They are not the guide, not the endorsed standard, and not counsel. This pack quotes nothing; RG verbatim is capped at two sentences by licence policy and the cap stays unused. IEEE Std 497-2016 and NUREG-0700 are named only, never linked, and their text is never reproduced here. Plant-specific monitoring answers are beyond public text and beyond this pack.
