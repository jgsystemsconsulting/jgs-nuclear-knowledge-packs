# Glossary: nrc-accident-monitoring-rg-1-97

Synthesized orientation definitions keyed to this pack's RG 1.97 Rev. 5 facts, alphabetical, not substitute source text.

- **accident monitoring**: the frame subject of this pack: showing plant conditions to the control-room operator during and after accidents, so the operator has information when it matters. The pack carries the frame; the guide carries the criteria. (ch01)
- **accident monitoring instrumentation**: the instrumentation that provides the control-room operator information about plant conditions during and after accidents. RG 1.97 presents criteria for it. Named here at orientation level only. (ch01)
- **categorization**: the guide's sorting of monitored variables. The frame exists; the labels live in the guide and the endorsed standard, with the older Type and Category note as the only named exception. (ch04)
- **current licensing basis**: the licensing record a plant works from. It may name an older RG 1.97 revision, and that reference stands until the record changes. (ch06)
- **design criteria**: the criteria frame the guide presents for accident monitoring instrumentation. The frame is a pack fact; the criteria text lives in the guide and the endorsed standard. (ch04)
- **DG-1335**: the proposal (accession ML17083A134) that was issued as final RG 1.97 Rev. 5 in May 2019. It appears in this pack only as the proposal; Rev. 5 is the current revision. (ch02)
- **endorsement with exceptions and clarifications**: the relationship Rev. 5 sets up with IEEE Std 497-2016: the guide endorses the standard and records its own adaptations. The wording of those adaptations lives in the guide. (ch05)
- **IEEE Std 497-2016**: the standard RG 1.97 Rev. 5 endorses with exceptions and clarifications. IEEE copyright. Named only in this pack: designation and edition, and its text is not packaged here. (ch05)
- **monitored variables**: the plant conditions the guide frames for monitoring, selected by the criteria the guide presents. The list of variables lives in the guide; Rev. 5 adds severe-accident-management-guideline variables to the subject matter. (ch03)
- **NUREG-0700**: the neighbouring human-system interface design domain. Named only; that guidance is not packaged here. (ch07)
- **regulatory frame**: where RG 1.97 sits: NRC staff guidance presenting criteria for accident monitoring instrumentation for civil power reactors, framed here at orientation level only. (ch07)
- **RG 1.97**: NRC Regulatory Guide 1.97, Criteria for Accident Monitoring Instrumentation for Nuclear Power Plants, Rev. 5, May 2019, accession ML18136A762; Federal Register notice 2019-08819, 84 FR 18591; pin 2026-11-09, dual-source verified. It presents criteria for accident monitoring instrumentation; not a substitute for the guide text or a plant licensing record. (ch01)
- **severe-accident-management-guideline variables**: the variable family Rev. 5 adds. Named in this pack as an addition only; the variables themselves live in the guide. (ch03)
- **staff review**: the framework-level fact that review reads the guide's criteria against a plant's record. No acceptance criteria, checklist, or procedure is carried here. (ch07)
- **technology-neutral**: the Rev. 5 frame word for criteria coverage that does not lean on one plant technology; the reach of that neutrality is stated in the guide. (ch06)
- **Type and Category schemes**: the variable schemes older RG 1.97 revisions used, tied to plant licensing bases. The note is a pack fact; the schemes' content lives in the older guide revisions. (ch02)
