# Cheatsheet: nrc-accident-monitoring-rg-1-97

Decision rules for which chapter owns a question, the pin rule, the not-advice rule, the named-only limits, and where questions run past this pack.

## Which chapter owns the question

- What RG 1.97 is, its subject scope, its pin, or its orientation limits: ch01.
- Revision history, the Rev. 5 pin, DG-1335 as the issued proposal, or the Rev. 4 predecessor: ch02.
- The monitored-variables frame, selection criteria as a frame, or the severe-accident-management-guideline addition: ch03.
- Categorization and design criteria as frames, or the no-design-how-to boundary: ch04.
- The IEEE Std 497-2016 endorsement hook and the named-only boundary: ch05.
- Technology-neutral applicability, 10 CFR Part 52, or which revision governs a plant: ch06.
- The regulatory frame, staff review framing, or where detail questions are directed: ch07.

## Pin rule

RG 1.97 Rev. 5, May 2019, accession ML18136A762; Federal Register notice 2019-08819, 84 FR 18591; pin 2026-11-09, dual-source verified. DG-1335 (ML17083A134) was the proposal and issued as final Rev. 5; Rev. 5 is the current revision. Rev. 4 (June 2006) is the predecessor. An older revision named in a plant licensing record governs that plant; read the governing revision from the plant record.

## Not source and not advice

Notes are synthesized, not copied: this pack quotes nothing, and RG verbatim is capped at two sentences by licence policy, a cap this pack never uses. These notes are not legal advice and are not a plant-specific monitoring system design. Verify everything against RG 1.97 Rev. 5 and the plant licensing record before relying on it.

## Named-only limits

- IEEE Std 497-2016 is named only: designation, edition, and the endorsement fact; no clause text, tables, or criteria appear here.
- NUREG-0700 is named only as the neighbouring human-system interface domain; no HSI design guidance is packaged here.
- Variable lists, category criteria, ranges, and design detail live in the guide and the endorsed standard; none are reproduced here.
- No instrumentation design how-to appears in this pack: no sensor, range, qualification, display, or configuration prescription.

## When the question is thin

Questions that need a specific variable, range, category, qualification, display, or design detail, an HSI design answer, or a ruling on the governing revision for a named plant are out of scope for this pack: say so, and point the reader at RG 1.97 Rev. 5 (ML18136A762), IEEE Std 497-2016 as a named source, and the plant licensing record rather than improvising from these notes.
