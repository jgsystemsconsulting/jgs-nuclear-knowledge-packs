# Cheatsheet: nrc-10cfr-part52-col

Decision rules: which chapter owns a question, the pin rules, the thin classes, and the not-advice limit.

## Which chapter owns the question

- What Part 52 is, its structure, general provisions, or the Part 50 contrast: ch01.
- Early site permits, ESP contents or finality, site redress or LWA hooks: ch02.
- Design certifications, DC finality, or what the appendices are: ch03.
- Combined licenses, 52.79 application contents, ITAAC during construction or closure: ch04.
- 52.98, exemptions, variances, amendment mechanics, or where 50.59 belongs: ch05.
- RG 1.206 identity, revision, currency, or application-structure role: ch06.
- Manufacturing licenses, or finality compared across subparts: ch07.

## Pin rules

eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11. RG 1.206 Rev 1, October 2018, ML18131A181, pin 2026-11-10 via Federal Register notice 2018-22262; guide PDF body not captured. Later amendments and later guide revisions out of scope.

## When the question is thin

Two classes are thin in this pack and stop at the pack boundary: (1) design-specific appendix rule bodies (Part 52 Appendices A to E named only); (2) non-LWR, SMR-framework, and Part 53 questions (named only as neighbouring frameworks). State the thin reason and stop.

## Not advice, not basis

These notes are synthesized orientation from public-domain eCFR slices and RG pin facts. They are not legal or licensing advice, not plant-specific licensing basis, and not any applicant's application. Advice requests are refused.
