# Glossary: nrc-10cfr-part52-col

Terms this pack uses. Definitions are synthesized orientation keyed to the pack's eCFR slices, not substitutes for section text. Alphabetical.

- **combined license (COL)**: one license under Subpart C authorizing construction and operation of a nuclear power plant; operation waits on the ITAAC closure finding under 52.103. (ch01, ch04)
- **design certification (DC)**: approval of a standard plant design by rulemaking under Subpart B, landing in Part 52 itself and referenced by later applications; finality under 52.63. (ch01, ch03)
- **early site permit (ESP)**: Subpart A permit resolving site suitability ahead of design and construction decisions; finality under 52.39. (ch01, ch02)
- **eCFR pin-check**: build-time check of the Electronic Code of Federal Regulations. This pack pins title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, checked 2026-11-11. (all chapters, cheatsheet)
- **exemption**: relief from a requirement; 52.7 carries the part-level specific exemptions hook. (ch01, ch05)
- **finality**: repeated Part 52 rule shape holding resolved findings stable within each section's stated bounds, with per-subpart sections 52.39, 52.63, 52.98, and 52.171. (ch02, ch03, ch05, ch07)
- **ITAAC (inspections, tests, analyses, and acceptance criteria)**: completion verifications for a COL; reported during construction under 52.99, and operation under 52.103 waits on the closure finding that acceptance criteria are met. (ch04)
- **limited work authorization (LWA)**: permission for limited site work before a full license; hooks at 52.27 (Subpart A, after an ESP) and 52.91 (Subpart C), named only in this pack. (ch02, ch04)
- **manufacturing license**: Subpart F license authorizing manufacture of nuclear power reactors for later installation at a site; finality under 52.171. (ch01, ch07)
- **RG 1.206**: NRC Regulatory Guide 1.206, "Applications for Nuclear Power Plants"; this pack carries Revision 1 pin facts only (October 2018, ML18131A181; guide PDF body not captured). (ch06)
- **variance**: relief tailored to a combined license under 52.93, "Exemptions and variances," distinct from part-level exemptions. (ch05)
