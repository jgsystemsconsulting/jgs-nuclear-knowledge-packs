# Chapter 7: Manufacturing licenses and finality pattern

Sources: 10 CFR Part 52 (eCFR), sections 52.110, 52.155, 52.157, 52.171; eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

A manufacturing license, issued under live Subpart F ("Manufacturing Licenses"), authorizes manufacture of nuclear power reactors, with the manufactured reactor installed later at a site under a different proceeding. Subpart F carries its own finality section, 52.171, "Finality of manufacturing licenses; information requests," and Subpart C's termination section (52.110, "Termination of license") marks where license finality ends. Chapter collects the finality pattern across subparts and closes the pack's scope.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Manufacture now, install later. The license covers manufacture; installation happens in a later proceeding, so Subpart F finality binds what was resolved at manufacture.
- Finality pattern across subparts. One rule shape repeats with per-subpart bounds: ESP finality under 52.39 (ch02), DC finality under 52.63 (ch03), COL finality under 52.98 after issuance under 52.97 (ch04, ch05), and the Subpart F instance under 52.171.
- Staff-review shape, framework level. Each action subpart carries the same skeleton: filing, contents, standards, administrative review, hearing and referral hooks, issuance. Chapters name verified sections only; staff positions stay out.

## Key Concepts

- 52.155, "Filing of applications," and 52.157, "Contents of applications; technical information in final safety analysis report," frame the Subpart F application.
- 52.171 is Subpart F's finality instance, with an information-request mechanism like the other finality sections.
- 52.110, "Termination of license," is the Subpart C termination hook: finality runs through the license's life and ends with the license.
- Where a manufacturing license transfers, Subpart F carries its own transfer hook in the same shape Subpart C does.

## Mental Models

Finality as per-subpart ratchet: each subpart holds its resolved findings still within its own section's bounds. Comparing 52.39, 52.63, 52.98, and 52.171 shows the repeated shape; the comparison never replaces the per-subpart rule text.

## Anti-patterns

Treating finality as absolute across changed circumstances: every finality section carries bounds and information-request mechanisms, and each binds only its own subpart's findings. Reading this pattern summary as a substitute for per-subpart rule text: bounds live in each section, not in the pattern.

## Key Takeaways

- Manufacturing licenses authorize manufacture for later installation under Subpart F.
- Subpart F finality instance: 52.171, bounded like the other finality sections.
- Finality pattern repeats across subparts: 52.39, 52.63, 52.98, 52.171, each with per-subpart bounds.
- Scope closer: selected slices only; design-specific appendix bodies (Part 52 Appendices A to E) named, never summarized; Part 53 and non-LWR principal design criteria named only as neighbouring frameworks; not legal or licensing advice, not plant-specific licensing basis, not any applicant's application, and advice requests are refused.
