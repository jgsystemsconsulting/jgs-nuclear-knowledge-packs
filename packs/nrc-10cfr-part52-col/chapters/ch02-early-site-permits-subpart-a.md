# Chapter 2: Early site permits

Sources: 10 CFR Part 52 (eCFR), sections 52.17, 52.21, 52.24, 52.26, 52.28, 52.31, 52.39; eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

An early site permit (ESP), issued under live Subpart A ("Early Site Permits"), resolves site suitability ahead of any reactor decision: the permit approves a site for one or more plant and reactor types while leaving design, construction, and operation open. Later applications can reference the permit instead of re-proving the site. Chapter orients what the permit decides, what it leaves open, and where its finality sits.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Site ahead of design. ESP separates site questions from design questions. Permit decides site; reactor choice stays open for a later combined license or construction permit application.
- Contents and review hooks. Application carries general and technical information (52.17), administrative review and hearings run under 52.21, and issuance lands in 52.24 against the subpart's standards.
- Finality instance. 52.39, "Finality of early site permit determinations," is Subpart A's finality section, in the pattern ch07 collects.

## Key Concepts

- 52.17 holds application technical information; it also carries the site redress plan hook (52.17(c)) referenced later when the site redress duty activates.
- 52.21 administrative review and hearings; 52.24 issuance of the early site permit, whose standards include the requested activities' environmental impacts being redressable.
- 52.26 sets permit duration (a fixed term, not open-ended); 52.31 states criteria for renewal; 52.28 covers transfer of the permit.
- 52.39 finality: determinations underlying the permit carry forward, bounded by the rule's own terms. Not unlimited; later proceedings can revisit what the bounds reserve.
- Site redress hooks named only: where a COL issues for the site, the permit remains in effect solely for site redress purposes (52.25); a withdrawn or denied COL application triggers the approved site redress plan (Subpart C hook, 52.91 area).
- Limited work authorization hooks named only: 52.27, "Limited work authorization after issuance of early site permit," in Subpart A; Subpart C carries its own LWA section (52.91).

## Mental Models

ESP as site bank account: site findings deposit under Subpart A; later COL proceedings withdraw the reference instead of re-litigating the site, within the bounds 52.39 states. Design and operation stay outside the account.

## Anti-patterns

Treating an ESP as construction authorization: it approves the site, not building work; ground disturbance rides separate LWA hooks. Treating 52.39 finality as unlimited: it binds within stated bounds and changed circumstances can defeat it.

## Key Takeaways

- ESP decides site suitability; design, construction, and operation stay open.
- Contents (52.17), review (52.21), issuance (52.24), duration (52.26), transfer (52.28), renewal criteria (52.31) frame the action.
- 52.39 is Subpart A's finality instance; site redress and LWA hooks are named only.
