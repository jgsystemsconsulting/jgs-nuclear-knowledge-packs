# Chapter 4: Combined licenses and ITAAC

Sources: 10 CFR Part 52 (eCFR), sections 52.79, 52.81, 52.85, 52.97, 52.99, 52.103; eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

A combined license (COL), issued under live Subpart C ("Combined Licenses"), authorizes construction and operation of a nuclear power plant in one license. The license wraps everything the two-step Part 50 route splits apart, and it can reference an early site permit or a standard design certification instead of re-proving them. Its distinctive mechanism is ITAAC: inspections, tests, analyses, and acceptance criteria that verify, during and after construction, that the plant as built matches what the application promised.

## Frameworks Introduced

- One license, two phases. The COL authorizes building and operating; issuance (52.97) starts the construction phase, and operation under the license (52.103) waits on ITAAC closure.
- Referenced approvals. A COL application can reference an ESP (ch02) or a DC (ch03); referenced determinations carry their finality forward under Subpart C's own finality rules (52.98, ch05; referenced-approval finality hook 52.83).
- ITAAC close the loop. 52.99 carries inspection during construction plus ITAAC schedules and notifications; 52.103 conditions operation on closure of the ITAAC.

## Key Concepts

- 52.79 holds application technical information in the final safety analysis report: the design, analyses, and commitments the license later holds the plant to. Orientation here is structural, not an application checklist.
- 52.81 states standards for review; 52.85 runs administrative review and hearings; 52.97 issues the license.
- 52.99: during construction the licensee reports on ITAAC; schedules and notifications keep the NRC in the loop.
- 52.103: operation under a COL requires the preoperational closure finding that ITAAC acceptance criteria are met; the finding is what gates operation.
- Limited work authorization hook named only: 52.91 authorizes limited work authorization activities ahead of a COL.

## Mental Models

COL as contract with a completion test: application promises (52.79), construction verifies against the promise (52.99), closure finding confirms promise met (52.103), then operation begins. ITAAC is the acceptance test written into the license itself.

## Anti-patterns

Writing ITAAC as ordinary technical specifications: tech specs govern operation; ITAAC governs completion of construction and closure before operation. Turning 52.79 orientation into application advice: this chapter describes what the section carries; it is not application advice for any real facility.

## Key Takeaways

- COL authorizes construction and operation in one license under Subpart C.
- Contents (52.79), review (52.81, 52.85), issuance (52.97) frame the license.
- ITAAC runs during construction (52.99) and its closure finding gates operation (52.103).
