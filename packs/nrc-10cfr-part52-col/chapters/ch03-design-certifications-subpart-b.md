# Chapter 3: Design certifications

Sources: 10 CFR Part 52 (eCFR), sections 52.48, 52.51, 52.63; eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

A standard design certification (DC), issued under live Subpart B ("Standard Design Certifications"), approves a nuclear power plant design by rulemaking: the certified design lands in the CFR itself, and later applicants reference the certification rather than re-proving the design. The design decision gets made once, centrally, and reused. Chapter orients the rulemaking shape, the review hooks, and where the DC's finality sits.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Design by rulemaking. Unlike ESP or COL, a DC is not a license issued to an applicant; it is a certification added to Part 52 through notice-and-comment rulemaking, so the approved design binds as regulation.
- Approve once, reference many. Later applications, notably combined licenses, reference the certified design. ch04 carries that reference step.
- Finality instance. 52.63, "Finality of standard design certifications," is Subpart B's finality section, in the pattern ch07 collects.

## Key Concepts

- 52.48, "Standards for review of applications," carries the review standards for a DC application.
- 52.51, "Administrative review of applications," runs the application review step; issuance lands in a separate subpart section ("Issuance of standard design certification") that puts the certification into this part by rule.
- 52.63 finality: the certification's determinations carry forward, bounded by the rule's own terms and its information-request mechanism.
- Appendices named only: Part 52 carries design-specific appendices (Appendices A to E) whose rulemaking text is design-specific rule text, not orientation material. Their bodies are out of this pack's slices.

## Mental Models

DC as published template: certification publishes the design into the CFR; every COL referencing it reuses the template instead of re-opening the design. Finality keeps the template stable within the bounds 52.63 sets.

## Anti-patterns

Summarizing an appendix rule from its title: the letters carry design-specific rule text and stand for their own bodies, which this pack never restates. Treating a DC as site approval: it certifies the design and says nothing about a site; site questions belong to Subpart A.

## Key Takeaways

- DC approves a design by rulemaking; later applications reference it.
- Standards (52.48), administrative review (52.51), and 52.63 finality frame the slice.
- Design-specific appendices are named only, never summarized.
