# Chapter 1: Part 52 frame and licensing actions

Sources: 10 CFR Part 52 (eCFR), sections 52.0, 52.1, 52.2, 52.3, 52.6, 52.7, 52.9, 52.10, 52.11; eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

10 CFR Part 52, "Licenses, Certifications, and Approvals for Nuclear Power Plants," holds alternative licensing routes that resolve site and design questions ahead of construction and, in the combined license case, authorize construction and operation in one license. The part-level general provisions (52.0 through 52.11) sit before any subpart heading: scope, vocabulary, and standing mechanics every action below leans on. Chapter orients part's shape; later chapters own each action.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Action-first map. Each licensing action lives in its own subpart: Subpart A early site permits, Subpart B standard design certifications, Subpart C combined licenses, Subpart F manufacturing licenses. Question gets located by naming the action it touches.
- Frame under actions. General provisions apply across the part: definitions (52.1), interpretations (52.2), written communications (52.3), completeness and accuracy of information (52.6), specific exemptions (52.7), jurisdictional limits (52.9), attacks and destructive acts (52.10), OMB approval (52.11).
- Route contrast. Part 50 runs the older two-step route: construction permit, then operating license. Part 52 is not a renumbered Part 50; section numbers and structures differ.

## Key Concepts

- 52.0 states scope and applicability of 10 CFR Chapter I provisions; 52.1 definitions supply pack vocabulary.
- General provisions sit part level; live Subpart A heading is "Early Site Permits" and opens at 52.12 ("Scope of subpart"), with 52.13 tying the subparts together. The early site permit slice is not part of general provisions.
- Subpart map in live TOC: A early site permits; B standard design certifications; C combined licenses; D [Reserved]; E standard design approvals; F manufacturing licenses; G [Reserved]; H enforcement.
- Specific exemptions (52.7) recur in ch05; 52.9 sets jurisdictional limits for the routes.

## Mental Models

Read Part 52 as frame plus actions: pick action subpart, borrow the general-provisions frame underneath it. Question is located by naming which layer and which action it touches; general provisions never substitute for the action subpart's own rules.

## Anti-patterns

Reading Part 52 as renumbered Part 50: parallel concepts exist (site ahead of design, one-step COL versus two-step CP/OL), but numbers and structures do not correspond. Treating the general-provisions slice (52.0 to 52.11) as the whole part: it frames, it does not license.

## Key Takeaways

- Part 52 holds site, design, combined, and manufacturing routes in separate subparts over a part-level general-provisions frame.
- General provisions span 52.0 to 52.11 and sit before Subpart A.
- Part 50 two-step route is the contrast case, named only.
