# Chapter 6: RG 1.206 Rev 1: applications guide pin orientation

Sources: NRC Regulatory Guide 1.206 Rev. 1, "Applications for Nuclear Power Plants," October 2018, ML18131A181; Federal Register issuance notice 2018-22262; pin 2026-11-10. Guide PDF body not captured.

## Core Idea

Regulatory Guide 1.206, "Applications for Nuclear Power Plants," is the NRC guide that orients content and format for Part 52 applications. This pack pins the guide's identity, revision history, and role beside Part 52, and it carries nothing from the guide's body: the guide PDF was not captured, and every RG claim here rests on the issuance-notice pin. The chapter cannot tell a reader what any guide section says.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Regulation versus one acceptable structure. Part 52 regulation binds; RG 1.206 describes one acceptable application structure for Part 52 applications (ESP, DC, COL, ML). The guide substitutes for no regulation.
- Pin-only guidance layer. Identity, revision, and currency are carried; content positions are not. Where a question needs guide text, the pack says so and stops.
- Currency check discipline. Guide revisions age; the pin date makes the claim checkable.

## Key Concepts

- Identity: Regulatory Guide 1.206, Revision 1, October 2018, ADAMS package accession ML18131A181.
- Issuance: Federal Register notice 2018-22262 (83 FR 51712-51713, October 12, 2018), docket NRC-2017-0145.
- Revision history: Revision 0 issued June 2007 under package ML070720184; Revision 1 issued 2018; Revision 1 was still current at the pin date 2026-11-10, cited as living guidance in later FR notices 2026-01756 and 2026-19568, with no Revision 2 issuance found in a Federal Register search spanning 2018 through 2026.
- Currency check and how done: search the Federal Register for a Revision 2 issuance notice; absence of one at the pin date supports "Revision 1 current," and a later check can flip it.
- Capture limit: NRC listing and PDF paths returned 403 in-session, so the guide PDF body was not captured; ML and identity rest on the FR issuance notice, not on a body read.

## Mental Models

RG 1.206 as an application skeleton guide beside the regulation: ch01's regulation-versus-guidance frame, applied to one guide, pinned hard enough to verify and thin enough to never over-claim.

## Anti-patterns

Citing guide section numbers: none are verified and none appear here. Stating guide content requirements or acceptance positions: not captured, not carried. Presenting this pin as a body read: the Sources line and this chapter both say the PDF was not captured.

## Key Takeaways

- RG 1.206 Rev 1 (October 2018, ML18131A181) pins via FR notice 2018-22262; Revision 1 current at pin date 2026-11-10.
- Guide role: one acceptable application structure for Part 52 applications; not regulation, and this pack states no staff positions.
- Guide section detail and acceptance positions are out of this pack; this chapter cannot supply them.
