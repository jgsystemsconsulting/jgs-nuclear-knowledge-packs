# Chapter 5: Standing mechanics: amendments, variances, and 52.98

Sources: 10 CFR Part 52 (eCFR), sections 52.7, 52.93, 52.98; eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09, pin-check 2026-11-11.

## Core Idea

Part 52 licenses do not freeze after issuance: finality (52.98) says what stays resolved, while amendment, exemption, and variance hooks say how a licensed action changes. Chapter orients the change mechanics this pack's slices carry: 52.98 finality of combined licenses, Subpart C's exemptions and variances (52.93), and the part-level specific exemptions hook (52.7, already met in ch01's general slice). The pack carries no Part 50 change-rule text.

## Frameworks Introduced

- Finality with a change valve. 52.98, "Finality of combined licenses; information requests," keeps resolved matters stable within its bounds and states when the NRC can request information.
- Relief hooks. 52.93, "Exemptions and variances," carries Subpart C's route for COL-specific relief; 52.7, "Specific exemptions," is the part-level exemption hook applying across the part.
- 50.59 belongs elsewhere. The 50.59 change rule is Part 50 territory: it lives in the live Part 50 orientation pack and its 50.59 chapter. This pack names the boundary and restates none of 50.59's screening criteria.

## Key Concepts

- 52.98 finality bounds: combined license determinations carry forward within the rule's stated bounds; the information-request mechanism polices drift rather than re-opening findings.
- Amendment mechanics: where cited sections carry change text at orientation level, the shape is license amended through the subpart's own process, not relief from the rule.
- 52.93 exemptions and variances: relief tailored to a particular license, distinct from 52.7's part-level exemptions.
- Boundary statement: 50.59 is the Part 50 change rule for existing licenses; questions about its criteria stop at this pack's boundary and route to the live Part 50 orientation pack.

## Mental Models

Finality and change hooks as a ratchet with a release valve: 52.98 holds findings still; 52.93 and 52.7 release specific points on stated grounds. The valve is part of the rule, not permission to ignore it.

## Anti-patterns

Importing 50.59 screening criteria into Part 52 prose: the criteria live in the Part 50 pack and stay there. Presenting variance or exemption hooks as applicant guidance: they are named hooks in the rules, not advice on seeking relief.

## Key Takeaways

- 52.98 is Subpart C finality; its bounds and information requests frame license stability.
- 52.93 and 52.7 are the variance and exemption hooks, named with one-line roles.
- 50.59 belongs to the live Part 50 orientation pack; this pack restates none of it.
