**Prerequisites:** basic regulatory vocabulary (license, certification, regulation versus guidance), and awareness that Part 50 and Part 52 are two different licensing routes. Part 50 orientation lives in the live Part 50 pack, named in prose where this pack contrasts the routes.

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### One part, several licensing actions

Part 52 holds distinct licensing actions in distinct subparts: early site permits, standard design certifications, combined licenses, and manufacturing licenses, over a general-provisions frame. A question is located by naming which action it touches. The Part 50 two-step route (construction permit, then operating license) is the contrast case and is named only as the older route.

### Finality carries forward

Each subpart carries finality provisions: an issue resolved inside an earlier action stays resolved when a later action references it, within the bounds the rule states. The pattern repeats per subpart with subpart-specific section numbers. ch07 collects the pattern after ch02 to ch04 meet it in place.

### ITAAC close the loop before operation

A combined license carries inspections, tests, analyses, and acceptance criteria. During construction the licensee reports on them; before operation the closure finding confirms the acceptance criteria are met. ch04 owns this slice.

### Regulation versus one acceptable structure

Part 52 is regulation and binds. RG 1.206 describes one acceptable application structure for Part 52 applications. The guide is not a substitute for the regulation, and this pack states no staff positions. RG content in this pack is pin orientation only: identity, revision, and role. The guide PDF body was not captured.

### Pin rule

## Scope & Limits

This pack pins eCFR title 10 current as of 2026-11-09 (pin-check 2026-11-11) and RG 1.206 Rev 1, October 2018, ML18131A181 (pin 2026-11-10). Later amendments and later guide revisions are out of scope.

- Selected slices only: general-provisions slice (52.0 to 52.11, part-level coverage), early site permits, design certifications, combined licenses and ITAAC, 52.98 with exemption and variance hooks, manufacturing licenses, and RG 1.206 Rev 1 pin orientation. This is not all of 10 CFR Part 52; sections and appendices outside the slices are out of scope.
- Design-specific appendix rules (Part 52 Appendices A to E) are named only as rulemaking text; no appendix body is summarized. Questions on appendix rule bodies are thin in this pack: say so and stop.
- Regulation orientation, not staff guidance. This pack states no staff positions. RG 1.206 is orientation for one acceptable application structure and is not a substitute for the regulation.
- Not legal advice and not licensing advice. Advice requests are refused.
- Not plant-specific licensing basis, and not any applicant's application. A facility licensing basis binds through its own licence documents.
- Part 53 and non-LWR principal design criteria are out of scope, named only as neighbouring frameworks. Questions on them are thin in this pack: say so and stop. No forward slug links: later packs on those frameworks are not live, are named only, and are never linked.
- Verbatim excerpts capped at two sentences. Notes are synthesized orientation.
